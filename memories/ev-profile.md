Ev (they/them unless stated otherwise) is rusty-but-conversant in
differential geometry and prefers that language for geometric concepts
(transversality, jets, pushforwards, inclusions) over CAD-industry jargon
— define CAD terms (pcurve, shelling, offset) when first used, but don't
simplify the math. They read carefully and probe asymmetries and fudged
invariants; vague hand-waves get caught. They favor functional style,
structural enforcement of invariants over runtime discipline, and "define
what something is" framing. See [[cad-working-style]].

**An `[ev]` PR is a decision document.** Lead with the decisions, each
with only the context needed to make it and the alternative you
rejected. Evidence, corrected numbers, filed defects and tracker state
go in the program's `log.md` behind one pointer — in the PR body they
bury the question.
