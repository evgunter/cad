# FORK-S5Q — what quiets an at-rest finding (designer B)

## For Ev

**Recommendation (likely): one rule, entailment at the finding's cells.** A finding is quiet exactly when a *holding* assertion *entails* it: the assertion's two selections are the finding's two cells, it reads a measure's output directly, and every value its relation and bound admit lies in the finding's stratum of that measure. The three phrases D10 turns on are not three rules; they fall out of this one.

**Premise check.** "The same measure" presumes a finding has a measure. It does not: the census decides contact by a zero residual and interference by a vertex in the other copy's material, and measures nothing. What a finding has is a *stratum*: contact is "these two cells coincide" (C5's `g = 0`, structural or not), interference is "these two materials overlap" (`g < 0`). An assertion speaks to a finding when its holding implies that stratum, whatever primitive it reads. "Does not straddle zero" is the wrong test for the same reason: `gap ≥ 0` admits `[0, ∞)`, which does not straddle zero, yet says nothing about touching; D10's own example (`= 0` for contact, a bound on one side of zero for interference) is the stratum test, not the straddle test. The text should say what its examples mean.

**Definitions.**

- **Cell.** `(copy, name)`: a `Body` output of a world placement and one `StableName` of that copy (a face, an edge, or a vertex where the record names one).
- **Stratum table.** Each `MeasurePrimitive` states which of its values mean clearance, contact and interference, beside `dim()`: `Gap` (`> 0`, `= 0`, `< 0`); `Distance` and `MinClearance` (`> 0`, `= 0`, none: both are magnitudes); `Angle` (none: `0` means parallel, not coincident). It is one table in `measure.rs`, extended with each new primitive and never consulted by evaluation.
- **Admitted set.** `{x : x relation b}` for the bound's value `b` in the same evaluation, `b`'s sign decided against zero through the one margined decision assertions already use (a bound inside the band reads as zero).
- **Entails.** The admitted set lies inside the finding's stratum of the assertion's primitive.

**The rule, as a table.** Quiet needs: verdict `Holds` in the same evaluation (`Violated`, `Unevaluated`, poisoned, or a primitive with no `f64` value such as `MinClearance`, quiets nothing); `value` reads one `Measure`'s output directly; the measure's two selections equal the finding's two cells as a set; and entailment.

| assertion | contact finding | interference finding |
|---|---|---|
| `Gap = 0`, `Distance = 0` | quiet | — (never holds there) |
| `Gap = b`, `Gap ≤ b`, `b < 0` | — (never holds) | quiet |
| `Gap ≤ 0` | loud (admits `< 0` too) | loud (admits `0` too) |
| `Gap ≥ 0`, `Gap ≥ b` (`b < 0`), any `Angle`, any bound on a defined variable over a measure | loud | loud |

No primitive is special-cased: an unsigned primitive cannot quiet an interference because its admitted set under any bound that could hold is never inside an empty stratum, and a mis-roled `Gap` (outer and inner swapped) at an interference reads positive and is `Violated`, so the verdict carries the role check.

**Sites.**

- **Contact.** The finding is stage 4's value-decided coincidence record at rest, and its site is the two cells that record names, of two copies. An assertion matches when its selections are those two cells. Not an incident face of them: a corner or a tilted edge resting on a face has no measure that could hold anyway, and widening the match would only let a holding assertion on a neighbouring face answer for a cell it does not measure.
- **Interference.** One finding per connected component of the two copies' overlapping material. Its site is the faces of each copy that bound that component; an assertion matches when it names one bounding face of each copy. The component is found by intersecting the two copies (the boolean, `f64`, at the census's band), on interfering pairs only. A pair whose intersection the kernel refuses (a sliver, a carrier pair without a join arm) is a **could-not-look** finding of the check — it could not say *where* — not a loud interference: DS6 already has that category, and it refuses at every severity but Off, which is the safe direction. Localizing by the census's witness instead (vertex-incident faces) is rejected: a pin in a bore has no vertex of either copy in the overlap except a seam vertex, so the witness names the wrong faces or none.

**Worked example.** A pin (radius 5.01) pressed through a plate's bore (radius 5), the pin also cutting into a rib of the plate. The overlap has two components: the annular tube between the bore wall and the pin wall, bounded by `{bore wall, plate top, plate bottom} × {pin wall}`, and the rib notch, bounded by rib faces and the pin wall. `Gap(bore wall of the plate copy, pin wall of the pin copy) ≤ −0.005 mm`, holding, names a bounding face of each copy of the first component and quiets it; the rib notch names no bore face and stays loud. The same assertion over the unplaced bodies' faces matches no cell (the cells are the copies') and quiets nothing. With `≤ 0` it quiets nothing; with `≤ −0.02 mm` it is `Violated` and quiets nothing. Two copies resting face to face by values: a contact finding on the two faces, quiet under `Gap = 0` or `Distance = 0` over them, loud under `≥ 0`.

**What it makes representable, and not.**

- An assertion cannot hide a second overlap: a distinct component has distinct bounding faces. One connected overlap with several mating pairs (a block wider than its pocket in both axes, one ring-shaped overlap) is quiet under an assertion on any one of its pairs; the report lists the component's faces, so the others are visible, not hidden. Tightening this to "every opposed same-kind pair bounding the component" is a later, stricter, reversible step.
- A contact whose cells no primitive measures (a vertex on a face, an edge lying in a face it is not parallel to, a curved face against a plane) is loud until one exists or the contact is constructed. The cure is a primitive with that arm, which adds a row to the stratum table and changes no rule. After stage 2 E no vertex is selectable, so a vertex-on-face contact cannot be quieted at all today.
- `gap ≤ 0` quiets nothing. A press fit states its minimum interference, as every real fit does; "no clearance" is not an intent about overlap, and admitting both strata is exactly the indifference the lint exists to report.
- An assertion on `min(gap₁, gap₂)` or `2·gap` quiets nothing: entailment through arithmetic is not decided. Two assertions say it.

**Cost at rest.** Contact: an index of assertions by cell pair, one bound-sign decision per candidate; nothing geometric. Interference: one boolean intersection per interfering pair, which is the only new geometry; the quieting itself is the same index. Nothing evaluates a measure for the rule; it reads verdicts and written bounds.

**Ratified text to change (D10, Assertions).** The sentence "A finding is quiet exactly when an assertion on the same measure at the same site has a bound the observation meets and that does not straddle zero: a contact finding under an assertion that the gap is zero, an interference finding under a bound on one side of zero." becomes:

> A finding is quiet exactly when a holding assertion entails it: its measure's two selections are the finding's two cells, its value is that measure's output, and every value its relation and bound admit lies in the finding's stratum of that measure, `{0}` for a contact (`= 0`) and the negative values for an interference (`≤ b` or `= b` with `b < 0`). Each measure states which of its values mean clearance, contact and interference; one whose values cannot mean the finding quiets nothing. A contact finding's cells are the two the census decided coincident. An interference finding is one connected component of two copies' overlapping material, its cells the faces of each copy that bound it; a component the kernel cannot bound is a finding that the check could not look.

**Rejected final states.**

- *The literal straddle rule* (`≥ 0` quiets contact, `≤ 0` quiets both). It contradicts D10's own example, and it quiets the one hazard the contact finding exists for: a point coincidence under variation that nothing said should hold. Sure.
- *Site = the copy pair.* One holding fit assertion quiets every overlap between two copies, including the rib notch above. Sure.
- *Site = an incident face of the record's cell.* Buys nothing today (no measure holds there) and lets a neighbour answer. Likely.

**Confidence.** Entailment as the principle: sure. The stratum table as its home: likely. Per-component interference sites by intersection: likely (cost is the open question; the fallback is the witness, which is worse, not a different rule). Could-not-look for a refused intersection: likely.

## Round 2

Having read A's "For Ev": I move on four of the five points and hold on none outright. A's premise is stronger than mine: a finding *is* a sign observation of C5's gap, so the stratum is `Gap`'s by definition and needs no table.

For contact an aligned `Gap = 0` does entail coplanarity, so if stage 4 records a flush pair of two copies as a finding, that assertion is the one that quiets it; this is a stage-4 detail, not a split worth Ev's time. Sure.
2. **Interference site — move to A's (c).** The sunk flange answers my "the report lists the faces": a quiet finding is one nobody reads, so an unintended overlap merged into an intended component is hidden, exactly what the lint exists for. (c) is my principle applied literally: `Gap(f, g)` describes the shell between its carriers and entails the overlap inside it and nothing else. A's reversibility argument is right too: (c)→(b) only quiets more later; (b)→(c) re-louds. Likely on semantics; the enclosure over curved bounding pieces is the cost to measure (heat sink), and a piece it cannot decide stays loud, which is safe.
3. **Contact site — move to A (structural identity extends the match).** Entailment through the door's theorem: if two faces of one copy are one construction, `Gap(x, f₁) = 0` entails `Gap(x, f₂) = 0`, so one assertion over a grooved plate's two tops is a statement about both. **D10 sentence I now propose** (replacing "A finding is quiet exactly when … a bound on one side of zero."):

> A finding observes the sign of C5's gap between two copies: a contact `g = 0` at two cells, an interference `g < 0` over one connected overlap. It is quiet exactly when holding assertions say the same: each reads a `Gap`'s output directly, over an opposed pair of faces of the two copies, admits only values of the finding's sign (`= 0` for a contact; `≤ b` or `= b` with `b` negative for an interference), and covers the site: a contact's two cells, or faces structurally one with them; for an overlap, every face bounding it lies between the carriers of one such pair. ## Round 3

**1. Disconnected regions.** Under points 1–2 an assertion is about a *carrier pair*, and for every pair `Gap` has an arm for (parallel planes, concentric spheres, coaxial cylinders) the gap is one number over the whole pair. So two disconnected regions of contact or overlap between one such pair sit at the same gap and are one fact; the rule quiets both under one assertion, and nothing else is expressible about them short of splitting the face, which is construction, D10's road. The cases:

- *One planar pair, two regions.* A bar-shaped block bottom across a C-shaped plate top (both single faces): two contact rectangles. The census today reports vertex-on-face and edge-in-face events per region; whatever stage 4 records (one face-pair record or two), `Gap(bottom, top) = 0` quiets all of it. Right: coplanarity is one condition.
- *One cylinder pair, two overlap components.* A pin through a bore that a cross-slot cuts into two bore faces: two components, each bounded by its own bore face. Point 4 reads carriers, and faces of one construction share a carrier (point 3's structural identity), so one assertion on either bore face covers both; faces coplanar only by value do not share one, and need their own.
- *Two copies, components bounded by different faces* (the rib, the sunk flange). Point 4 distinguishes them: each bounding piece must lie in some asserted pair's shell. The intersection today returns a multi-solid body (`Body::solids`) whose faces carry the operands' names, so components and their bounding faces are read off it directly.
- *Curved faces touching in several regions at different local gaps*: a wavy face on a plane at two crests, a cylinder across a saddle, a cam on a flat follower. Each touch is its own value-coincidence (crest 1's height equals the plane's, crest 2's does). This is the class Ev means, and the converged rule cannot distinguish them, but it also never quiets them: `Gap` has no arm, and the census refuses curved × planar and non-conformal curved × curved within reach as `CensusUndecidable`, so today they are could-not-look, not findings. Nothing is quieted too much; distinction is lost only where it has no content yet.
- *Conformal curved pair* (ball in socket, pin in an equal bore, with or without a window in the socket): every piece is at `g = 0` globally. One fact.

**2. The witness, as a final state.**

- *What it is.* A `Point` variable the assertion reads: `Assert { measure, relation, bound, at: Option<Point> }`. It must be in the finding's space and must move with the copies, so it is *defined* over their selections (a vertex, a point of a face at `(u, v)`, an edge's midpoint); a free point with coordinates in the space's frame is the absolute coordinate D10 retired, and goes stale on every placement edit.
- *"Unplaced" intersection.* Two parts' own bodies live in different frames and have no intersection; the only reading is the copies intersected in the space's computing frame, as a report body the check builds and drops, never a `Body` variable and never placed into the world. That is what point 4 already computes.
- *Predicate.* With a witness, the assertion quiets exactly the component containing it (point-in-solid against that component, decided; on the boundary, refused, no quiet), under the same measure (1–2) and shell (4) conditions restricted to that component. Without one, every component in the shell (the converged rule). A witness in no component is reported on the assertion. For a contact, the component is the coincidence record whose region holds the point.
- *Required?* "Only when there are several components" makes the schema depend on a value: a document valid at one setting refuses at another after an edit moves a wall. Optional always, never required.
- *Negative witnesses.* "This point is not in the overlap" is an ordinary assertion on a signed point-to-material depth, held or violated like any other; it quiets nothing and needs no rule. A primitive for later, if wanted.
- *With point 5.* No intersection, no component, no witness check: loud, unchanged. *Staleness:* the witness moves with its copy; an edit that moves the overlap off it re-louds the finding (safe) while the assertion still checks its gap. *Cost:* one point-in-solid probe per assertion and component; the schema change is the real cost.
- *Against the converged rule.* For `Gap`'s arms the shell already fixes *where*: components in one shell are at one gap, so the witness only separates components with no distinct intent (part 1). It does not replace point 4: the sunk flange is one connected component, and a witness in its radial shell quiets the flange with it. So today the witness quiets strictly less than, or the same as, the shell rule, and buys nothing.
- *The third shape the cases suggest: the witness is a measure argument.* The regions that need distinguishing (the curved class) are those at different *local* gaps, and what they lack is a measure. `Gap { outer, inner, at: Point }`, the signed material separation of the two carriers at the foot of `at` on `outer`, is defined for any carrier pair (cylinder on plane, a crest on a plane) and localizes by construction: its site is the region or component holding `at`, and the quieting rule is unchanged (holding, entailing, covering its site). That gives Ev's distinction exactly where it has content, keeps the assertion's signature as D10 states it, and lands when the census can decide those pairs.

*Recommendation (likely):* keep points 3–4 as converged; no witness on the assertion. State the pointed `Gap` arm as the road for non-conformal pairs, as one sentence in the PR body, not in D10 (D10 names no primitives).

**3. Zero.** Where a dimensioned zero is written, today and under D10:

- assertions: `Gap = 0`, `≤ 0`, `≥ 0` (contact, no clearance, no interference);
- datum origins: `Datum::Plane/Axis/Point { origin: [S; 3] }` are `Length` slots, so a plane through the frame origin writes three zeros (its normal's components are `Scalar`, fine);
- mates: `MateFrame.offset` and `Alignment` slots, where flush is zero; A11 (2)'s offsets retire with stage 3, but a distance mate's `d = 0` case stays whatever shape replaces them;
- formulas: `0 * w` already yields an exact `Length` zero under VR5's lattice (`Scalar × Length`), so the lattice holds a zero of every dimension today, reached by a trick;
- the GUI's offer: every typed `0 mm` offers to share with every other `0 mm` (origins, offsets, bounds), noise that declining answers with yet another zero; a constant has no identity, so nothing is offered;
- analysis: a free `0 mm` without tolerance is already a constant in every lane (VR8), so nothing is lost; a toleranced origin stays writable as a named variable valued 0.

*Ev's alternative.* Relations against zero only: `Gap ≤ b` becomes `gap − b ≤ 0`, a definition over a measure, which the quieting rule cannot read through (direct read, round 2), or `Gap + c ≤ 0` with `c` a variable, the bound under another name. It removes zero from assertions only, by making every other bound a definition, and avoids no zero elsewhere in the list above. "Disallow `Gap ≤ −1`": a bound is a variable already (VR4), and no constant can stand in a bound slot today except through `0 * w`, so there is nothing to disallow. By Ev's own test it is not worth it.

*Recommendation (likely):* the exact constant `0` at every dimension, since it is the one quantity the same in every unit and the additive identity of every dimension the lattice already carries. VR5: the constants are the dimensionless rationals, the rational fractions of `turn`, and `0` at any dimension. VR6 unchanged in principle: a lone `0` at a slot's root is the constant, a typed `0 mm` is a value from a family and mints a variable, the spelling deciding the meaning. It serves assertions, origins and offsets at once, makes the bound's sign exact for the quieting rule, and changes no mechanism.
