**1. The kinds.** `dof` is what a value carries; `H` is what it forgets.

| kind (working name) | `H` | dof | readers today |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Frame` | trivial | 6 | profile plane, mate frames, a world placement's pose, `FaceFrame` |

The lattice's other elements — a rotation with no position (`H` = translations), a plane with an in-plane direction but no origin (`H` = in-plane translations), a screw (`H` = helical) — have no reader and are not kinds. A carrier is a pose of its kind plus its shape invariants (a cylinder is an `Axis` and a radius; a torus a `Pivot` and two radii), which is what D10's canonical form already compares.
