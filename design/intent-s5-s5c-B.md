# FORK-S5C — the at-rest census as a check (designer B)

## For Ev

**Recommendation** (`likely` overall; each claim marked):

1. **The census becomes one registry resident, `AtRest`, and nothing at rest refuses except the registry's one refusing door.** Its findings are three: an *unproven contact* (two faces of two copies the census decided coincident and stage 4's door could not prove structural — the same evidence type the `unproven-coincidence` lint reports inside a body, with the same recourse: make it one construction), an *interference* (overlap between two copies, localised to the faces bounding it), and a *could-not-look* (a pair the census had no lane for, or could not decide, with the cause). Each carries `quiet: Option<assertion>`. Severity is `Severity` (Off/Warn/Error), default Warn. `enforce_checks` at Error refuses loud and could-not-look findings; quiet ones never; Warn refuses nothing. `assemble`, `Assembly`, `AssemblyError::AtRest` and the viewer's `Refused` badge are gone. `sure` that this is D10's shape; `likely` on default Warn.

2. **"Neither refuses where the census has a lane" is read as a fail-loud clause, not a refusal mandate.** Where the census has no lane, the resident reports that it could not look, as a finding about the pair, never silence and never a clean report. That is DS6's existing "a check that could not look says so as a FINDING" posture, which the registry already holds for shell orientation and chart coherence. I could not see why the sentence is hedged rather than stated, so I propose rewording it (below) to say the principle: no outcome of the census is silence. `likely`.

3. **Assertions are the at-rest resident's exception vocabulary, and DS6's rule is generalised to say so.** Ev's principle in DS6 is "blocking is fine if exceptions are declarable". An assertion is declarable in the document, carries provenance (the person wrote it), is keyed by site (selections of the copies by stable name), and its staleness direction is its own verdict: a `Gap = 0` whose faces separate, or a `Gap ≤ −0.005 mm` whose pin now clears, is `Violated`, loud on its own, with no second mechanism. It is stronger than DS6's waiver (an acknowledgment "about a finding"), because it is a geometric claim that is verified, so it cannot rot the way a pair-keyed acknowledgment can when the overlap grows. `sure`.

4. **`Separation` retires.** Every pair it reports (boxes cannot prove apart, from different copies) is a pair the census examines and decides, or says it could not. Keeping both reports the heat sink's five fins twice in two vocabularies, and the weaker one. Its obligation (the disjointness `graft_disjoint_all_keyed` leaves to callers) is discharged by the resident's interference arm, which is the material test the boxes only gate. `sure`.

5. **Callers.** The viewer's badge is a projection of the checks report (counts: loud, quiet, could-not-look) and `AtRestBadge` as a stored type goes; the body is always kept. Python's `assemble` goes; `run_checks` reports `CheckId.AtRest` with the three finding classes, and a script that wants the old gate calls `enforce_checks` with the resident at `Error`. The tour's gallery rows become counts over the same report. Export reads nothing of it (D10: it reads the world's coordinates and nothing else). `sure`.

**The final state.** The kernel's tier-3′ census returns a report, not errors: per examined pair, apart / contact at these entities / overlap with witness / undecided with cause. Tier 1–3 validity stays `ValidationError`; the gather already runs it per source, and the aggregate never reaches the census malformed. The document layer's resident reads that report once per product, asks stage 4's door which contacts are structural, localises each overlap by intersecting the two copies (an intersection that refuses is one unlocalised finding, loud), and applies one function `quieted_by(finding, doc, evaluation)`: quiet iff some assertion reads a measure over selections of the finding's two copies naming its site, its verdict in this evaluation is `Holds`, and its admitted set lies inside the finding's stratum of the signed gap: `{0}` for contact, `(−∞, 0)` for interference. A could-not-look finding on a pair is quiet under a holding clearance assertion on it with a positive bound (`Gap ≥ b` or `MinClearance ≥ b`, `b > 0`): the assertion's lane decided what the census could not, on the one stratum with nothing to report. `likely`, not load-bearing.

*Worked example.* The tour's heat sink: five fins seated 1/16" into the base. Today: five `Separation` findings at Warn, then `assemble` refuses `AtRest` on five `InstanceInterference`s, and the viewer draws it anyway under a red badge. After: five loud `Interference` findings, each bounded by a fin's bottom and the base's top; add `Gap(base.top, fin_i.bottom) ≤ −1/16"` over the placed copies' faces and each is listed quiet, naming its assertion; `enforce_checks` at Error passes. Raise one fin to float 1 mm clear: its finding vanishes and its assertion is `Violated`, loud by itself. Make the seat a mate instead: the contact is structural, the gap is zero, and the `≤ −1/16"` assertion is `Violated`, which is the right complaint.

*What it leaves possible.* A pair with no signed primitive (a box against a cylinder) has an interference finding no assertion can quiet; it stays loud until a signed primitive exists for that carrier pair. That is a frontier of `Gap`, not of the rule.

*Reversible?* Yes: `assemble` is sugar over `run_checks_on` plus `enforce_checks` at Error, and can be put back as such.
