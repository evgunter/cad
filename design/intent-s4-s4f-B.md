# FORK-S4F — how a construction's output carriers are known to the door

## For Ev

**Recommendation (likely): nothing states them. The code is the definition.** The door learns what a carrier is by running the construction itself with every variable a symbol, and a recorded coincidence is structural when the margin that decided it is zero as a polynomial in those symbols. No per-verb `CarrierFlow` table, no witness, no separate rung 2. Rungs 2 and 3 of the spec become one rung, and stage 4 unit C shrinks to "every variable a symbol, exact constants, and the record carrying how its Zero was discharged".

**Premise check.** The fork asks how carrier *forms* reach the door. The door never needs a carrier. What it needs is: did this Zero hold for every value of the variables, or only at the current ones? The symbolic tier already answers exactly that question, per decision, inside the kernel (`geom_core::Sym`: a margin whose normal form is the zero polynomial decides `Zero` "for every parameter value, at any box width", and the count of such decisions is `SymCounts::symbolic_zero`). Carrier forms are rung 2's intermediate, invented so the comparison could run without the kernel. That intermediate is what the fork is evidence of: a second representation of what each verb computes, kept in step with the code by hand plus a check. Ev's mitre answer rejected per-node theorems for being "a second hand-written description of an operation [that] can drift from its code". A per-output formula is the same thing one level down.

### The design, as a final state

- **One symbolic lane, already built.** The kernel is generic over its scalar (`extrude<T: Decide + AtRestPolicy>`, the mate solve's `SolveScalar for Sym<T>`, the driver's `Evaluation<Sym<Interval>>` leaf replay in `drive.rs`). Replaying the document at `Sym` with every free variable bound as `Sym::param(var)` runs the real extrude, revolve, boolean, split, blend battery and mate solve. Every carrier they mint carries, beside its numbers, its exact form over the input symbols. An extrude's cap comes out as a plane whose normal and offset mention the frame's symbols, the direction and the depth, and not the profile's; a side wall's form mentions no depth. D10's examples are theorems the replay exhibits, not declarations.
- **The rung.** For a recorded row, the door reads the same decision in the replay (matched as the spec already matches rows across lanes: node, site, ordinal, cell names). Discharged as a theorem (`Discharge::Theorem`): `Structural(Identity)`. Discharged numerically, or unmatched, or escalated: `Unproven`, with the margin's normal form as the residual. Rung 1 (same source, unit B) stays as the free first rung.
- **"Canonical form per kind" is what the verdict reads, not a separate comparison.** The plane ladder decides `‖n₁ × n₂‖` and `d₁ − σ·d₂` over unit normals and offsets, so in-plane motion and a shift along the normal are folded by the predicate itself; the curved ladder does the same for axes and radii. Equality of normal forms is an equivalence relation, so Ev's loop of blocks and the brick across two of them close with nothing said (sure).
- **Soundness is one-directional, in the right direction.** A zero form is zero under every real assignment (the tier's theorem, pinned by `sym_theorem` rows). So the replay can miss a proof (a frozen form, an unmatched row, a rule it lacks) and report noise; it can never report a false "proven". A hand table's failure is the opposite one: a stated `depth` where the code built `|depth|` proves a falsehood on every scene the f64 witness happens to agree on. A point check cannot close that gap; only running the code can.
- **What "states which of its inputs" means now.** Nothing. The support of a carrier's form, read from the code, is that knowledge. A construction never restates its outputs.
- **What rung 2 proved that this must also prove** (likely, each checked against the tier's shipped rules): `h/2 + h/2` against `h` (exact rational forms); a defined variable (bound through `bind_definitions`); a frame from a datum's `u`, `v` orthonormalised at evaluation (`n̂·ĉ₀ = (u×v)·u/…` is a polynomial identity; unit lengths by rule A, `sqrt(X)² = X`); a `Step::Rigid` placement (an angle variable through Rodrigues, closed by rule B, `sin² + cos² = 1`); a `FaceFrame` reading a face (the face's own forms, so "a projection reduces to what it was built from" is automatic); a mate-placed face (the solve runs at `Sym` and its pose satisfies the contact as an identity when the solve is closed-form, which `solve_atan2` with rule D suggests it is: unsure, measure). The spec's pre-stage-3 "opaque placement atom" and unit H's composed frame are not needed: placements already hold `VarId` slots (stage 1), so they are symbols today.
- **Recourse.** The residual is a polynomial over variable ids: `v − w` over two free variables of one kind gives "make the slot reading `v` read `w`"; a residual over the symbols of two placing nodes gives "place one relative to the other"; anything else names the residual and the assertion at its site.
- **Cost at the door.** One symbolic evaluation of the document per lint run, shared by every row, then O(1) per row. The tier's own measurements of one document replay at its nominal, release, shipped rules (`SymRules::shipped`'s table): plate 0.15–0.36 s, annulus 0.3 s, bracket 1.7 s, link 2.4–4 s, pad 10–14 s. Carriers pass through booleans unchanged, so a row's forms are the size its construction minted, whatever the document's depth; vertex-on-carrier rows (a split's pinch) grow with the intersections that made the vertex and may freeze, which is noise, never a false proof.
- **The cache Ev allowed, if measured too slow.** Not a hand table. A per-verb form table *generated* by running that verb once at `Sym` with its slots as symbols, checked in, and verified in CI as a theorem against the replay. Or a content-key memo of per-node symbolic values. Either is derived from the code; neither is authored.
- **Reversibility.** High. Adding a hand table later is one more rung at the same door, and the replay stays as the check that any such table is true.

### The alternatives

- **The spec's unit C: hand-written `CarrierFlow` per verb, `LinForm`/`PoseForm`/`CarrierForm` in `canon.rs`, an f64 witness over the corpus.** Pros: milliseconds; no kernel run; readable per-verb statements. Cons: a second description of every verb, per output role and per decision branch (an extrude's `reverse`, a revolve's on-axis arc); the witness checks the corpus's values, not the family, so drift between those values proves falsehoods silently; H then has to write the frame composition a second time; "Opaque" roles are a third category nothing else needs. Lean against (likely).
- **The spec's table with a symbolic witness** (stated form and minted carrier compared as forms at `Sym`). Drift becomes impossible, but then the table is redundant with the replay at the replay's cost, and its only remaining value is the fast path, which is the generated cache above.
