**What the existing callers actually read** (17 sites; "tag" = a zero the kernel wrote to mean something):
- *Tags in a number* (8): an iso-line's slope being 0 means "this image is a row or column" (3 sites); a ray's start being 0 means "starts at its line's origin" (3); a torus's major radius being 0 means "a sphere" (1); a table's bulge being 0 means "a line" (2, and the bulge can also be a user's computed value).

None asks whether a computed quantity vanishes by identity. Only the direction mint does. (likely; read each site, traced none upstream)

So for a kernel-written literal, "exactly zero" is a fact about structure stored in a number, which `[unrepresentable]` already rules against ("encodings are tagged, never in-band magic values"). It belongs in a variant: a held-row image (which the iso-line fork, PR 6975, already proposes), a ray with or without a start, a stored chart end, a uniform-weights form, a line segment at the table door.

**The three final states.**
- **(a) As in the PR: one function, every scalar answers.** True in every lane: the mint is exact at `Sym`. Leaves possible: any caller branching on a rounding accident at `f64`, with ratified text behind it. Not recommended now.
- **(b) Symbolic only (recommended).** At `Sym`: the axis where the expression is identically zero, at every valuation, eager or deferring. At `f64`, `Interval`, `Dual`: always the quotient, which is what PR 6990 lands; at `f64` that is the axis to the bit, and `Dual` keeps a side's true tilt with no special clause. The question stays "not a decision, no record, propagates with no fallback", because it never touches a number. Cost: with the symbolic tier switched off, a plain-`Interval` side between literal-equal coordinates stays wide; that lane already refuses identities over a box. `is_exact_zero` is not the door and gets no ratified text; its callers move to variants, or to decisions where they change what is built. Until they do, at `Sym` it answers by the same expression test, so nothing there reads zero off a number.
- **(c) At the numeric scalars the question is the band's `Zero`.** This is what PR 6963 did. It is a decision, so it is logged and can escalate, and the two arms stop being one real value: a side tilted by less than ε is stored as the exact axis. That is healing, not a choice of spelling, and at `Sym<f64>` it stores a constant on a coincidence (the five red rows). Right for the callers that change what is built (a bulge of zero is the existing straightness decision); wrong for the mint. Not recommended as the question.

Only a symbolic scalar can answer it: `Sym` answers yes where the symbolic tier proves the expression the zero function, by rules that read no value and a budget no session sets (ERROR-DESIGN E12), so the answer never comes from the value channel and is the same at every valuation. `f64`, `Interval` and `Dual` hold no expression and answer nothing: at a numeric scalar zero is the band's ([verdicts]), and a zero that means something is a variant, never a number read back ([unrepresentable]).
