## What the change is
The sweep doors (`sweep::extrude`, `loft`, `revolve`) were handed their profile's sketch plane as a rigid frame, dropped it, and rebuilt every direction they store from points they had just placed with it: a Newell plane fitted to corners for each cap and line wall, `rim.normalize()` for an arc wall's and a rim circle's reference direction, a normalised difference for a line rim and a strut. Over a parameter box that arithmetic gave stored data width the geometry does not have, and tier 3 could not read the body. The PR makes each door keep the frame: a cap is its station's frame (`swept::cap_plane`); a wall, rim or strut takes the frame turning one 2-D direction decided once in the sketch (`swept::sketch_direction`); the revolve's latitude circles take their reference direction from the revolve's frame. It deletes the fitted-plane machinery and its refusals (`CapPlaneError`, `ExtrudeError::SidePlane`, `cap_plane_orientation`, `register_rim_identity`, `strut_carrier`, `polygon_half_perimeter`, two Python tags), gates `geom_brep::newell` to tests, and re-baselines what moved at `f64`.
