## What the change is
A pcurve that runs along a chart row or column (`Pcurve::IsoLine`) used to store its two ends beside the map that already implies them, kept in step by hand and by checks. The PR makes the image `IsoLine { line: ChartLine { axis, value }, at0, rate }` (and `IsoArc` sharing `ChartLine`), deletes the stored ends and everything that read them (`ends`, `span_ends`, `with_end_at`, `over_span`, `Pcurve::split_at`, `StoredSpan`, `edge_span()`), moves the readers that need a corner onto the lift (the meet of two held lines), states a closed chart's period by its domain's two ends (`Period::of_domain`, `Period::moved`), makes a diagonal iso line unwritable, and removes from tier 3 the reads that existed for the stored ends (`off_map`, the stated ends in the domain gate's reach, the held channel's drift slack and its "moves" decision). 84 files, most of them readers re-spelled.
