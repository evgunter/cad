## What the change is
A profile's line hop records a unit direction. Where one component of the hop's chord is zero, the mint stores the exact axis `(0, ±1)` instead of the quotient `chord / |chord|`. On main the mint learned "zero" from a sign reading of the value, so one document could store the axis at one valuation and the quotient at another. The PR gives `geom_core::Real` an associated `Expression` (a `SymId` at `Sym<T>`, the uninhabited `NoExpression` at every numeric scalar) and asks the expression, through a sealed `Expression::proves_zero`, whether it is zero as an expression. The ask runs the symbolic tier's ladder in a second session state that borrows the installed session's node table and nothing else, with the two value-reading rules off and a budget of 64 terms. The mint stores the axis where exactly one component is proved zero, signed by ONE sign reading of the other component; every numeric scalar stores the quotient. A `line_to` hop now mints once, in the verb, and records that director; the next verb reads it.
