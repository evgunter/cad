## The problem
A drive certifies a document over a box of its parameters by replaying it at a box scalar. Every decision's margin is then an enclosure over the box, and a leaf certifies only where every decision reads a definite answer. E12 added an expression beside each value so that a margin that is zero as an identity decides `Zero` at any box width, because interval arithmetic cannot see that two occurrences of one parameter are one number. For every other margin E12 says the decision is numeric, as before.

Measured (M15; the item has the numbers; re-check what you rest on):
- On a brick whose moving cap is tilted, the embedding census's edge-against-face residual reads a cap vertex against the cap's plane. The vertex's height and the plane origin's height are two stored values that each carry the box's one parameter. The value channel encloses ± 0.094 over a box of ± 0.05. At every member of the box sampled the `f64` margin is ± 5e-17 and decides; at a box of no width it decides. The margin's expression holds the parameter once, and it cancels: the plain form is a constant with no parameter in it, 6.58e-19, because the tilted normal is given as three rounded numbers and the vertex is on the plane to rounding, not exactly. The symbolic tier is asked, and has no answer: the form is not zero.
- A second finding is undecided only because the first is. Three further census readings on wider boxes have the same shape: an enclosure that holds zero, beside an expression that is a non-zero constant or a function with no zero in the box.
- On the square brick the same reading is as wide on the value channel and the form is exactly zero, so the tier proves it. The difference between the body that certifies and the one that does not is 6.6e-19.
- The program has met this before, one reading at a time, and each time re-spelled the reading so the parameter enters once (the side reader's ray, a wall's roots, the one-plane reading). Each took a unit; M13 counted 2,394 of 11,028 builds holding some box-lane escalation at the nominal point alone, of other causes; nobody has counted how many undecided margins over a box of width are of THIS shape.

**What needs deciding:** what a decision at a box scalar is owed when its margin has one sign, or sits inside the zero band, at every member of the box, and the value channel cannot say so because the box enters the margin more than once. Say what that margin IS (a rounding-size constant that is not an identity; a definite function; a coincidence that holds across the box) and what each kind's right answer is over a box, and what the leaf protocol's containment claim needs it to be. Say where the defect starts: at each reader, at what the stored values are, at the body's construction (a vertex that is on its plane only to rounding), at the scalar, or at what E12 lets the expression be used for; and which of those the code should act at so the class cannot recur, as against this instance. Say what a count of the class would have to show for your answer to be right, and how to take it. If E12 as ratified is what makes this hard, say so and say what it should say instead (designer.md §2; check its provenance: who wrote "Everything else decides numerically as today", and on what evidence).

Not yours to design: the census's own algorithm, the driver's bisection, the cost of the symbolic tier's ladder (PR 7712), the per-drive store.
