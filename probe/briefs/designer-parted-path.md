## The problem
The driver certifies a leaf of a parameter box by replaying the document over the leaf at a box scalar and comparing the replay's verdict vector with the vector of the nominal run at `f64` (the witness). E6 gives a leaf three fates: every predicate definite and the vector matching, certified; any predicate indeterminate, bisect; definite on a different vector, refused mass, `FlipCrossing`, "flipped predicates named".

Measured and read on main (the item has the detail; re-check what you rest on):
- A leaf's vector can differ from its witness's with no sign turned. Three such states are reported today as a `FlipCrossing` whose evidence names nothing: the two runs name different predicates at one index of their choices; their choices agree end to end and their check sequences differ; their choices agree and one check has another definite sign. The comparison that names a flip reads choices only.
- One instance was a kernel defect, not geometry: since a recent change one lane logged a premise pass the other lane does not run, so every leaf over a curved closed-form face parted from its witness at fifteen checks, was counted as refused mass, and its refusal named no predicate. Exactly one test on main went red for it.
- `FlipEvidence::is_empty`'s doc states the empty case as intended and honest.

**What needs deciding:** what it MEANS for a leaf's replay to differ from its witness other than by a turned sign, and so what the driver concludes of such a leaf, what it does next, what it tells the consumer, and what the coverage masses then mean. Say what a verdict vector is a record of (which decisions, in which roles, at which scalar) and what "the same vector" is a claim about; whether each of the three states can arise from geometry at all or only from the kernel disagreeing with itself, and what follows for each; what evidence a refusal must be able to name; and what every consumer of the classes reads. If E6 as ratified is what makes this hard, say so and say what it should say instead (designer.md §2; check its provenance).

Not yours to design: the symbolic tier's question (ratified, E12), the per-drive store (a separate unit), which premise passes are logged (ruled for the one instance; say so if you think that ruling is the wrong layer).
