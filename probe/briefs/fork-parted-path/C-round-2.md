**Recommendation (likely): the measurement shows the record of cells is the wrong thing to certify on, and the decision record was the right thing compared against the wrong run.** Keep E6's gate as ratified (a leaf certifies when every decision it logged is the nominal's, in order) and change one thing: the run a leaf is compared with is **the nominal replayed at the box scalar, over a zero-width box**, not the `f64` witness. Then:

- **Certified**: every predicate definite, and the leaf's record equals that reference's.
- **`FlipCrossing`**: at the first difference, one question is answered with the other definite sign. Named by type (node, predicate, role, both signs), so an empty one cannot be built. Today's 660 flips, the planted `notch` and the sign-changing extrusion among them, stay exactly what they are.
- **Parted** (the records differ and no question turned): not proven either way; unsettled, bisected like an indeterminate leaf, never a flip.

**What changed from round 1, and why.** In round 1 I moved to "certify when the leaf builds the witness's cells". The measurement refutes it on its own test: all 661 of today's flip leaves hold the witness's cells, so that gate certifies every one, including the fixture the tree planted as its example of a flip. **Terms.** *Witness*: the nominal run at `f64`. *Box scalar*: the interval arithmetic a leaf is replayed in. *Leaf*: a sub-box. *Record* (verdict vector): per node, every logged definite decision in order: predicate, sign, role. *Choice*: a decision whose other sign can change what is built. *Check*: one whose other sign can only refuse, or a reading taken off the finished body. *Turn*: the same question, reached the same way, with the other sign. *Parting*: any other first difference. *Wall*: the set of parameter values where a decision's margin is zero. *Cell record*: per node its standing and its bodies' faces, edges and vertices by name with their incidence.

**1. Is "same cells across a turned choice" the witness's part? No (likely).** A bump that becomes a notch and a pad that points the other way have the same named cells and are not the part the nominal describes. A tolerance study exists to report exactly that ("topology change under tolerance is usually the reported defect itself" is E6's reason for no-flips, and it generalizes: a change of the part's qualitative shape is the defect). What the cell record lacks is the answers to the build's structural questions: which way a corner turns, which side material lies on, which order things meet in. Those answers are the decision record. They cannot be stated apart from it without naming each question by the cells it is about, which the kernel cannot do for questions asked before cells exist. So the record of a build, for this purpose, IS the decision log, and the fault round 0 and round 1 chased was elsewhere.

**What a certified leaf promises**, in words a study can be held to: *over this leaf the document builds, and every question the build asked is answered as at the nominal.* A turned sign means the leaf lies across a wall from the nominal; every path from the nominal to it passes through values where the kernel cannot decide. No-flips v1 says that side is refused and not analysed. Certifying it because its cells match would be analysing the far side, the v2 door, taken silently.

**2. What a certificate owes, of the things the cell record leaves out.**
- *A crease's convexity; the side an extrusion lies on*: owed, to the study (it is the finding), to sensitivities (E4 calls them chamber-local), to a later blend. Carried by the record's signs, checks included: a convexity read only by the finished-body check is still the part's shape.
- *Positions*: not owed as sameness. Owed as enclosures, true over the whole leaf, to every measure.
- *A measure's configuration*: owed to the stackup; it is a choice in the measure node's record, which the gate reads.
- *A coincidence's discharge* (proven or numeric): not owed. It is how a fact was shown, not what was built.
- *An assertion's verdict*: not owed by the certificate (E10 reports it in its own column). The 82 certified leaves that read `Violated` are that column working.

**3. What `FlipCrossing` is for.** A decided leaf across a wall, the wall named. It is not empty: 660 leaves, 14.92 of mass. A node that fails where the nominal builds is a flip only when the failure is proven definite over the leaf; none is today. The 27,781 leaves with such a node and no logged indeterminacy stay unsettled and bisect (13 of them hold certified mass below, so splitting is doing work). 27,667 are one extrude sentence, "no definite length across the sketch plane": that is an undecided question reported as a failure without going through the decision funnel, a defect of that operation, not a class of the drive.

**4. Why the reference moves** (likely). The two scalars' logs differ at one point in 2,311 of 8,673 decided builds, with the same cells in every one. Any gate that compares a leaf's log with the `f64` log therefore depends on the whole kernel logging identically in two lanes, kept so by hand; the plate was one instance of that. Comparing a leaf with the nominal's own replay in the leaf's lane removes the dependence by construction: both sides are one program, and they differ only by the box. What remains to part a route is box width and real geometry, where bisecting is the right response and where the measurement finds no instance among decided leaves today (the one parting in 2,505 is the plate, which this reference certifies). The round-1 objections to the whole-record gate do not survive: lane parity no longer bears on certification, and a turned check is the part's shape changing.

**The fallback, if the reference cannot be taken** (it must be decided at zero width): compare choices only against the `f64` log, and refuse a parting at once under its own reason, since between lanes every parting measured is a lane artifact no split removes.

**Text for E6**, added to the leaf protocol, nothing else: "The record a leaf is compared with is the nominal's own replay at the box scalar over a zero-width box, which must build what the `f64` witness builds. A leaf whose record differs with no decision turned is not proven either way and is bisected."

**Confidence.** Cells are the wrong gate: sure (measured). Same answers is what a study wants certified: likely; it is your call and the fixture suggests it was your intent. The same-lane reference: likely as a design, unsure until the first measurement below.
