## For Ev

**Recommendation (changed; likely): use the same-lane reference.**
- **The reference:** the nominal document replayed once per drive at the drive's own box scalar over a zero-width box.
- **The tie:** the reference must be fully decided and its choices must equal the `f64` witness's, with no turn and no parting. Otherwise the drive refuses whole, as the kernel's defect, naming the node.
- **Certified:** a leaf's whole record (choices and checks, in order) equals the reference's.
- **`FlipCrossing`:** a question is answered with the other sign. It is named and never empty.
- **Parted:** the records differ with nothing turned. The leaf bisects and is named at the floor.
- **No `f64` fallback:** there is no second reference behind the same word "certified".

**Why it moved.**
- The measurement was meant to choose between this gate and comparing choices only against the `f64` witness. Every input to both came out in the outcome each gate was built for:
  - every reference is decided;
  - no drive that certifies mass today would be refused whole;
  - the two sorts give the same class to all 2,307 decided leaves;
  - no check turns where no choice does;
  - no lane pair is a box-only decision that steers what is built.
- So the gates differ only on cases no one has seen, and what decides between them is which error each makes there.
  - **Comparing checks** risks a refusal that names a "different" part that is in fact the same.
  - **Leaving checks out** risks a certificate on a part that is different, wherever only a finished-body check reads the difference. A crease's convexity is the known case: `dihedral_sign` passes convex or concave.
- A wrong certificate breaks the product's promise; a wrong refusal costs mass. The repair the second gate needs is an audit recording every such shape fact as a choice. The first needs no audit.
- The tie also gives the one measured lane contradiction, `pcurve_chart_placement` on the tour's bracket, the right owner. It is refused whole as the kernel's, where the `f64` gate would report it near the nominal as the document's flip.
- The new gate's only measured cost is one replay per drive.

**The strongest case for the other answer.** A ray instrument's own check can turn while the part is unchanged. The side reader's `side_at_infinity_beside` or `_winding` can do this when a face moves past a ray, and the side it reads, the choice `side_read`, stays the same.
- **The recommended gate** then refuses that leaf as a named `FlipCrossing`, and the range search draws a boundary where none exists.
- **The `f64` choices gate** certifies it, correctly.
- **The reverse case:** a boolean edge whose convexity no construction choice decides, and only tier 3 reads, turns concave. The choices gate certifies the notch; the recommended gate refuses it.

Neither case is observed today. The repair for the first is local: report a turned check of an instrument as not proven rather than as a flip, which is one arm.

**Item 6 is another question (likely).** Two thirds of the named flips turn a choice and change nothing a reader of the body sees except coordinates: `segment_gap` (300), `extrusion_normal_component` (122), `carrier_line_circle` (18). They are not leaves whose form is the nominal's being refused by a bad gate. They are decisions logged as choices whose sign, at that site, does not decide the form. That is a fault in what each predicate asks, or in its role, at its site, the way a lane choice is.
- **If such a leaf certified today,** the certificate would claim "every question answered as at the nominal". That is false: a question was answered otherwise. The only true weaker claim is "the same form", and M13 showed the kernel has no record of form apart from the answers.
- **So these leaves stay refused, named,** until each site is read. For each predicate: what does its other sign change in the build? If nothing, it is re-posed or logged as what it is. A thicker slab flipping `extrusion_normal_component` suggests a margin that is not the extrusion's own.
- This is a row per predicate, not a change to the gate.
