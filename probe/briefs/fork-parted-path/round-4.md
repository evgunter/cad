## The arguments each side must now answer
- **Against the same-lane reference (C's round 3):** a tie by cells is blind to what a choice decides, so it hides a contradiction between the lanes (`pcurve_chart_placement`: Positive at `f64`, Negative at the box scalar, at one point); a tie that does see it compares the two lanes' choices, "which is the lane parity the reference was meant to make unnecessary". A's tie (no parting allowed) refuses whole every drive whose lanes part at the nominal, 24% of builds today; B's tie tolerates partings and so certifies a leaf against a reference whose route the `f64` build did not take. And where the reference is undecided the drive refuses whole or needs a second reference.
- **Against comparing checks (C's round 3):** a crease's convexity read off the finished body is shape; the side reader's ray decisions are not: `topo::side_reader::SIDE`'s doc says of `side_at_infinity_order` that it "is a pair's gap, geometry, and its other sign moves a ray between reading and refusing on that pair", so two unrelated faces can swap order along a ray with the part's form unchanged. A turned check is then sometimes the form and sometimes an instrument's route, and the role cannot say which. Where a build depends on a function of checks the tree records the answer once as a choice (`side_read`).
- **Against the `f64` reference with choices only (A's and B's round 3):** a convexity that only the finished-body check reads turns with every choice equal, and that gate certifies a corner become a notch; and every reason for a terminal `Parted` and for leaving checks out was an answer to the two lanes not logging alike, which is a fact about the kernel and not about the document.
- **C's own reserve:** if a decision only the box scalar makes STEERS what a leaf builds (the candidate is `session_box_reach`, 1,348 of the 2,106 lane partings), then unlogging it hides a structural decision and logging it parts every leaf, and comparing within one lane would be the coherent answer.
