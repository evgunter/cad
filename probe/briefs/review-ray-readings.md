## What the change is

The PR re-poses two of the walk's readings so a moving planar face's box no longer enters them twice: the turn of a face's loops about a crossing (`solid_contain::loop_turn`, `arc_turn`: the dot `u·w` becomes `(n̂ × u)·(n̂ × w)`) and the aim's step into a planar face (`side_reader::loop_aim`, through `clearance_in_plane`). It also changes when two crossings are a pair (`RayHit::pair_as`: only where the undecided gap is in band; otherwise the ray grazes and is set aside). With them an extruded slab of depth `1 ± 0.25` at `Sym<Interval>` reads `Out` by its caps' rays.
