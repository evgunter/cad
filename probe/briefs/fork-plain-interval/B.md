# Designer report: what a leaf at plain `Interval` is (item `a-chains-link-refuses-at-an-edges-stored-curve-with-the-box-arm-off`)

## For Ev

**Recommendation (likely).** Two things, and only the second is work.

1. **Plain `Interval` gets no special leaf. The switch retires at both box scalars alike, and nothing is built to let plain `Interval` answer an identity.** A plain-`Interval` finish of a body whose *shape* the box changes refuses on its identity checks, and that refusal is correct: it is the behaviour ERROR-DESIGN E12 was written to describe ("interval arithmetic cannot see that the occurrences are one number"; the tier "distinguishes an identity from a coincidence, which no enclosure can"). The charter's "tier 3 can read these bodies over a box" is a requirement on `Sym<Interval>`, the scalar the driver replays at.
2. **The gather's finish carries its members' verdicts and asks only what the gather adds.** The chain's refusal is not a sweep door's finish and not really about plain `Interval`: it is the assemble (`⊔`, here the document's product) re-deriving, on moved geometry, facts each member's own finish had already decided and its rigid move had carried. With that fixed, the chain certifies at plain `Interval` again, this time with tier 3 actually run, and no rule about plain `Interval` is needed.

**Terms.** *Box scalar*: a number type that stands for every value a parameter takes over a box of parameters; `Interval` holds an enclosure, `Sym<Interval>` holds the same enclosure plus the expression that produced it. *Finish* / *tier 3*: the at-rest gate that derives a body's verdict (edge certificates, incidence, volume sign and the rest). *Tier 2*: the structural checks before it. *The switch*: `AtRestPolicy::leaf`, which at the box scalars returns a sweep door's or a gather's body at tier 2 only. *Identity check*: an at-rest check whose margin is zero by construction, such as "the edge's stored curve starts at its vertex". *Gather*: grafting several placed bodies into one (`wire_assemble`, through `topo::boolean::graft_placed`).

**Premise check: the body that refuses is the gather's, not a link's (likely; inferred from the tree and the counts, not traced).**
- The refused body has 18 faces, 30 edges, 20 vertices, 18 lines and 12 circles. One bar is 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices; one pin is 4 faces, 6 edges (4 arcs, 2 seam lines), 4 vertices. Bar plus three pins is exactly 18 / 30 / 20 / 18 lines / 12 circles. The 24 refusing edges are the bar's 12 and the two turned pins' 12; the 6 that pass are the target pin's, which no joint turns.
- The tour's `set_product` makes the product an `Assemble` node. `wire_assemble` grafts each placed member into a fresh `Body` and ends in `T::leaf`, which with the box arm off is `T::finish`: the whole of tier 3 on the aggregate, check 2 re-certifying every edge "at every door alike" (`validate.rs`, "Tier 3, check 2").
- Each member's own extrude reads no joint variable, so its finish at `Interval` is over a zero-width box and passes; the placement is a rigid motion, and `FinishedBody::moved` carries the verdict and asks only the session box. The verdict is then dropped one step later, because the graft takes the moved image as construction-state data.
- The `Sym<Interval>` stop on the volume (`[-1.08e-7, 2.53e-7]`) is the same re-derivation: the volume sign of four bodies whose own finishes had each decided it, re-read after a boxed turn (likely).
- The measured margins are otherwise as the item says; I did not re-run the probe.

**What each box scalar's answer claims, and who relies on which (sure).**
- `Interval`: every value encloses the true value at every parameter point of the box, rounding included. A definite verdict holds at every point of the box; an undecided one says nothing about the model and is curable by splitting. At a zero-width box it is the rounding-accounting lane, and identities read at rounding width and decide. Shipped callers at plain `Interval`: `pncad::analysis::clearances_at_nominal` (no box), and the numeric memo prior over the nominal box (`LeafLane::Numeric`). Over a wide box: only `SymbolicDials::off()`, which `drive.rs` calls "the pre-E12 driver, reproduced", used by test differentials and by the tour's chain table. The viewer and the Python bindings set no such dial; the range search, clearance, stackup and report take the drive's lane, default `Sym<Interval>`.
- `Sym<Interval>`: the same enclosure, plus a `Zero` proven from the expression at any box width. Every shipped drive.
- So no shipped consumer asks plain `Interval` to certify a wide box. The sentence test of DESIGN `[unrepresentable]` still gives plain `Interval` over a box a meaning ("an enclosure over the box; it certifies where every fact it must derive has real margin"), so it is not excluded by a bound.

**What an identity check can be at a scalar with no expression, over a box that moves the body: not asked again (likely).** "The curve starts at its vertex" is a fact of the shape up to isometry. It is decided where the shape is built, and where the box only moves the body it is carried, which is already what the move does and what DESIGN's at-rest row says ("an edge's or pcurve row's certificate is carried by every door that keeps its support; a finished body moved by an isometry stays finished and asks only the session box again"). Where the box changes the shape itself (a boxed dimension in the profile), plain `Interval` has no way to know the two occurrences are one number, refuses, and E12 names that as the reason the symbolic tier exists.

**The final states.**

*A. Recommended: switch retires for both; the gather carries.*
- Makes true: a finished member under a rigid motion stays finished through the gather, as it already does through the move. The gather's finish asks the questions between members and the session box, and reads each member's own verdict.
- Consequences: the chain certifies at plain `Interval` at tier 3 with no tier asked (no joint changes a link's shape); the `Sym<Interval>` row no longer re-reads four volumes; the corpus finish count falls at `f64` too, since the aggregate no longer re-certifies member edges.
- Leaves possible, correctly: a plain-`Interval` replay of a document whose box changes a shape refuses leaf by leaf to the budget. That is the tier-off lane's finding, as `m10_3`'s pinned limit row already states.
- Worked example: the bar is finished once at `[x, x]` values; joint 1 turns it by `[-0.03, 0.03]`; its edge's origin becomes `([-4.5e-5, 4.5e-5], …)` and its vertex a separately enclosed point. Today's gather measures the distance between those two enclosures, `[0, 9e-5]`. Under A nothing measures it: the bar's finish read it as zero before the turn, and a rotation moves both ends alike.
- Reversible: yes; it removes a re-derivation.

*B. Plain `Interval` keeps tier 2 at a leaf for good (the switch's `Interval` arm stays).* The tour runs unchanged. But the type cannot tell a boxed `Interval` from the nominal one, where the finish is wanted and works; the charter keeps a permanent exception; and "certifies" at plain `Interval` keeps meaning tier 2 alone, which is what the chain table has been reporting. Not recommended.

*C. Plain `Interval` stops being a scalar a drive can replay at (delete `SymbolicDials::enabled` and `LeafLane::Numeric`).* Removes the lane that can only escalate on a shape-changing box. It also removes the differential that measures what the tier buys (E12's own acceptance evidence), and does nothing for the chain, whose table row calls `evaluate::<Interval>` directly. Defensible later, not needed now, easy to do afterwards.

*D. Give plain `Interval` a way to discharge identities* (provenance tokens, re-association). E12 weighed and rejected these for stated reasons, and took the one it kept in reserve as a `Sym`-only door. Not recommended.
