## The problem
At a symbolic box scalar a decision reads its margin's value first. Where the value reads a definite sign, that is the answer. Where it does not, the decision asks the symbolic tier, whose only possible positive answer is that the margin is zero as an identity; any other outcome leaves the decision undecided, and the caller refuses, sets a ray aside, or the driver bisects.

Measured on main with the switch's box arm off (the item and M11 have the tables; re-check what you rest on):
- On the tour's first drive, 983 s of 1,306 s of summed leaf time is the tier answering asks about four margins. None of the four is zero: the `f64` witness is definite on each and every sub-box narrow enough to decide decides the same way. No ask ends in a budget stop or a poisoned form: each walks every stage and both retries, ends in a small form that is not zero, and stops because it has run out of attempts. A slow ask is 26 to 211 ms and thousands of forms.
- Leaving the tier unasked on those four changes no verdict of the drive and moves much of the cost to the next undecided margin of the same finish: the cost is in forms the undecided margins share, paid by whichever asks first.
- Those four margins are undecided because their readings are wide, which is a defect of its own with its own row. But a margin can read no definite sign for many reasons a wide box makes ordinary, and the same price is then paid.

**What needs deciding:** what an at-rest decision whose value reads no definite sign is asking of the symbolic tier, and what that ask should be and cost when the true answer is "not an identity". Say what the question is a function of and what it is entitled to know; what "no" is, as an answer, and whether today's "ran out of attempts" is one; what each stage and each retry buys and for which asks; where an answer, positive or negative, lives and for how long (an ask, a node, a session, a drive); and what the decision's caller and the driver are owed. Say what your answer makes of the per-drive store's design and of the measured sharing of forms across margins. If E12 as ratified is what makes this hard, say so and say what it should say instead (designer.md §2; check its provenance).

Not yours to design: the four readings' widths (their own row), the driver's leaf protocol, the profile's or the sweep doors' spellings.
