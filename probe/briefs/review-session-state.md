## What the change is
The symbolic tier's `Session` held state for one replay of a document: a registry of stated identities, retry memos with a guard on how many forms a retry may build, memos of forms, atoms and reductions, and counts. A node's answer could therefore depend on what an unrelated earlier node had registered or built. The unit sorts every piece of that state by owner. The drive owns what is a function of an expression's id and the dials (the expression table, the plain forms with their frozen set, the atom records: `sym::memo`, `DriveMemo`), and leaves work in caches of it. A node owns its registry, its retry memos and guard, its door memo and its counts (`sym::NodeState`), and its value or error carries a `NodeRecord` (the identities it registered, what its decisions came to, the roots its frozen column is read over). `eval::eval_node` opens each document node over the records of its transitive upstream nodes. The branches that handled a node missing from the drive's table are removed, behind a `debug_assert!`.
