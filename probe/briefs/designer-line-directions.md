## The problem
A profile validates a loop and records, with each line segment, a unit director (`SegmentKind::Line { director }`). Validation mints it: the exact axis where a component of the chord is zero as a function of the document's variables (asked of the scalar's expression), the chord over its length otherwise. The sweep's traversal carries the record to the doors, and no door reads it: every sweep door re-derives a line's direction and length from the segment's two points.

Measured (the item's sections; re-measure what you rest on):
- With the doors re-deriving, a slab extruded over a box of its width stores its moving walls' normals and reference directions as wide as the box makes the chord's quotient, where the profile's record of those sides is exact. That width is one of the things a box-scalar leaf's at-rest checks then refuse on.
- With the doors reading the record instead (a mutant, 2026-11-22), those walls are exact, and some thirty rows elsewhere go red, of two kinds. (i) Two walls that are the same plane, one built from a side whose director was minted as an axis and one from a side whose director is a chord's quotient, no longer prove identical: the identity needs a quotient of a length by its own magnitude to be one, and the symbolic tier holds no sign. When both walls were quotients the factor cancelled. (ii) The span of a side along its director folds to the chord's length only through a square-root rule that not every walk has, and a chord's length beside an axis does not fold to the component it is.
- Two re-spellings were tried and measured; neither cures both kinds.

**What needs deciding:** what a line side's direction and extent ARE, from the profile's record to the wall a door builds, so that there is one statement of them that every reader reads; so that a side that does not move as a function of the box is stored without the box's width; and so that two walls that are one plane are one plane to the symbolic tier whichever sides they were built from, with nothing about a sign registered that is not an identity at every value. Say what is stored and what is derived, where, and who owns it; what the record means at each scalar the generic code admits; what a door does with a side whose record it cannot use; what happens to a direction a test table wrote; and whether the question is the right one (designer.md §1: is the thing to want a rule for which spelling a door reads, or something upstream or downstream of that?).
