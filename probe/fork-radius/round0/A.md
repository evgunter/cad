**Alternatives.**
- **B. A gate's sign as a fact the tier holds.** The decision door records that a gated quantity is positive; the tier's syntactic sign test treats it as it treats a square root. It is defensible: the tier already uses facts of this class (a denominator is non-zero and a radicand non-negative "wherever the expression has a value"), it reads no value and no bracket, and it is the same in every lane. It closes every reader at once, future ones included. Against: a second source of sign in the tier; it changes expressions document-wide (the tier's reach is not monotone: R1's boss lost ten decisions to an `abs` fold); and it lets stored magnitudes persist.
