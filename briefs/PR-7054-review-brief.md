Background, with the terms defined. A **trimmed face** is a curved face meshed in its chart on the chart lane (`mesh::trimmed`): boundary chord points shared with its neighbours, a lattice of interior points, and a constrained Delaunay triangulation (CDT). A **cone** face is triangulated in the cone's **development** (the cone unrolled flat about its apex; `trimmed::developed`), which cuts each point's slant radius to 27 significant bits and its direction to 26 so that every point of one **ruling** (a straight line of the cone through its apex) lies on one exact line; that is what stops the CDT making a triangle of three points of one ruling, which has no area in space. The price: two boundary points of one ruling closer than 2⁻²⁷ of the slant radius `r / sin α` develop to ONE point, and until this PR the face refused `SphereTrimShape { DevelopedMerge }`. On a slender (draft-angle) cone that distance is 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁷ of the part, reached at the default ε, and since the PR before this one routes every cone face with a split ruling to this lane, main refuses faces it meshed a day ago. **This PR:** a **run** (consecutive polygon points on one ruling that develop to one point) gets one CDT vertex, and the emit pass re-splits it: the triangle on the side to the run's neighbour along the ruling takes the run's near point, the rest of the vertex's fan takes the far point, and thin triangles between the run's points are added. `split_edge` is the test-side door that splits an edge of a finished body at a fraction of its range; a body it leaves a vertex on is not a finished body, which is why the rows read volume off the unsplit solid (`reach_probes::got_enclosing`).
