**In one paragraph.** `present_curved` reads a curved shared edge's image on its face's chart only where that image is a `Trig`: a circle or ellipse on a plane, or a once-winding harmonic row on a cylinder. On a cone, a plane section (an ellipse, a hyperbola or a parabola; the chart's `FocalSection` image) and any other harmonic image that is no coordinate line are `unread`, so the pair refuses `Structural` at depth 0, and a corner such an edge makes at a shared vertex is unread for the same reason. Valid bodies end there today: two faces of one cone split by a plane cut (24 tests of the per-solid census, all f64, traced on `origin/embed/trace-cone-torus-measure`), and the two cone cavities whose cap meets the wall in a hyperbola. The unit makes a cone's chart read those images, soundly, at both scalars: an enclosure of the chart curve and its derivative over a parameter piece, and a separator's value along the face's image of the curve that stays tight at any parameter width (a centred form over the piece is too loose, which is why `Trig::psi` reads coefficients). The row's own sketch: a cone's image `apex + v (cos a axis + sin a radial(u))` is a product of two harmonic channels, a trigonometric polynomial of degree two, of degree four under a quadric separator. Whether that, or re-posing the chart as PR 7113 does for a sphere, or something else is the right form is the first thing you tell me.
