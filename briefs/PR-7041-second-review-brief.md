Background, with the terms defined. The mesher turns each curved face of a solid into triangles in one of two **lanes**. The **rectangle lane** (`mesh::curved`) takes a face whose domain is a rectangle of its chart `(u, v)`, bounded by iso lines. The **chart lane** (`mesh::trimmed`) takes any other curved face: it reads the face's boundary as a polygon in the chart, every loop, and triangulates inside it. On main `tessellate::lane_of` chose the lane by three tests on the KINDS of the outer loop's carriers (an ellipse, spline or spiric; on a sphere a circle with a projected image; a ring), and sent everything else to the rectangle lane, which then asked two **doors** in `geom_brep::props` whether the face is a rectangle (`require_iso_rectangle`, the shape door, and `require_one_chart_branch`, the branch door) and refused if not. So a face bounded by iso lines alone that is no rectangle (a pipe wall notched through its seam) reached no lane. A **stored image** (a pcurve) is an edge's curve written in a face's chart; the mint stores it in one of several **forms** (harmonic for rims, meridians and a section conic on its cylinder; projected, focal section, spiric, fitted, general for curves no iso line is). The **K stream** is the kernel's record of the banded decisions a run made, which a body's replay must reproduce in content and order.

At the fix head the route (`tessellate::route_of`, read once per face per run by `Routes::of`) answers by the first of three facts: a ring → the chart lane; an outer image of a form no iso line takes (`Pcurve::is_non_iso_form`) → the chart lane, no door asked; otherwise the two doors: both pass → the rectangle lane, carrying a `curved::Rectangle` token only the doors mint; a door decides "no rectangle" → the chart lane where `topo::Body::face_lift` reads the domain, else the lift's own refusal or the door's verdict; a door escalates or the data contradicts itself → `UnsupportedCurvedShape` with that cause.
