Background, with the terms defined. A **trimmed face** is a curved face meshed in its chart `(u, v)`: its boundary is the chord points it shares with its neighbours (the **trim polygon**, every loop), its interior vertices are a **lattice**, a rectangular grid of chart points sized from the tolerance δ, and a constrained Delaunay triangulation joins them. Each triangle is held to δ by a per-triangle **certificate**; a face whose certificate passes its bound refuses. The lattice is sized at half of δ on an analytic chart (`UNPROVED_HEADROOM`), so a certificate may reach 2 "cell bounds" before the face refuses.
