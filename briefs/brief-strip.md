## Your unit

**Row:** `work/embed/a-plane-cell-holding-both-sides-of-a-thin-face-is-read-only-below-its-width.md` (P0, M). Read all of it.

**What it is.** `present_plane` presents a plane face's part of a cell only when the cell holds one line edge or two meeting at a convex corner; two line edges with no common end, or three or more, are `Local`. That is right where a split can still narrow the cell onto one edge or one corner. On a thin face it cannot: a face of width `w` holds both long sides in every cell that reaches across it, and its short side with both neighbours in every cell at that end, and the metric split cuts the face's length before its width. At a thin slab's end the pair needs about 52 cuts, past the depth floor of 40 (`Uncertified { Departure }` at an edge, 40 tests, widths 1e-8 to 3e-7); along a needle it takes `L/w` pairs, past the pair budget (`Budget(Pairs)`, 24 tests). **It is the face's aspect ratio, not ε**: rods 1.3 mm wide are among them. About 65 tests. The trace did not flip it.

**What to do.** A strip presentation: a cell clipped between two parallel line edges (and at a short edge, between its two neighbours, the cell holding the face's whole end). The face is convex there, so the affine map that serves a corner serves it. Design it as the general reading it is: a plane face's part of a cell is the intersection of the half-planes of the line edges in the cell wherever the face is convex in that cell, of which one edge, a convex corner, a strip and a strip's end are the cases; say whether `present_plane` should read that one thing, and what "convex in the cell" is decided by (the leans, the edges' order on the loop). Two edges that are NOT of one convex piece (a slit; two sides of a hole; edges of different loops) must not be read as a strip: pin each.

**What you will meet once the strip is presented, and must measure and not touch.** The departure reading multiplies how fast the two faces separate by the width of its own cell, and Ev has ruled that goes: the margin is to be metered at the faces' own reach (PR 7095, still open on its last two decisions; the sliver concession retires with it). On a slab 20 ε thick the face's own reach across is 20 ε, so whether the presented pair then certifies, lands in the band, or refuses is exactly that question's ground.
