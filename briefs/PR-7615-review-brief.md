Background, with the terms defined. The mesher turns each face of a solid into triangles, one **lane** per kind of face: the rectangle lane (`mesh::curved`) for a face that is a rectangle of its chart, the chart lane (`mesh::trimmed`) for a trimmed face, with **triangulating arms** (a constrained Delaunay triangulation of the trim polygon and a lattice) and a **walk** on ruled faces, and the planar lane. δ is the chordal tolerance: every point of the mesh lies within δ of the true surface, and each triangle's **certificate** (`cert.rs`) holds that, triangle by triangle. ε is the kernel's coincidence tolerance; the mesh layer reads no ε in what chooses a mesh (`docs/DESIGN.md` `[tolerance.chordal]`, `crates/mesh/README.md` `[mesh.winding]`).
