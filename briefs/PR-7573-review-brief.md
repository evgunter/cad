Background, with the terms defined. The mesher turns each face of a solid into a **patch** of triangles, one **lane** per kind of face (the rectangle lane `mesh::curved`, the chart lane `mesh::trimmed` with its triangulating arms and its **walk** on ruled faces, the planar lane), and then **joins** the patches into one mesh on shared vertex ids (`mesh::tessellate`). Two faces that share an edge share that edge's **chord** points. δ is the chordal tolerance: every point of the mesh lies within δ of the true surface, which each triangle's **certificate** (`cert.rs`) holds, triangle by triangle. ε is the kernel's coincidence tolerance; `docs/DESIGN.md` `[tolerance.chordal]` and `crates/mesh/README.md` `[mesh.winding]` say the mesh layer reads no ε in what chooses a mesh.

The defect: two faces that share two boundary chains can each make, as an INSIDE edge of its own triangulation, the same straight side between a point of one chain and a point of the other, so the joined mesh has an edge of four triangles. On main three rules answer it, each by predicting the face across: the triangulating arms insert a point and rebuild (`UnclearedSharedSide` past a budget), the walk cuts the side where the face across could reach it (`may_make`), and the rectangle lane has no rule, so the join's census refuses (`OverusedSide`). This unit moves the answer to the join: the census finds each side that more than one patch has inside, and every such patch splits it once at a point of ITS OWN surface over the side's chart midpoint; no lane reads its neighbours; the three rules retire. With it come a floor on chords (two edges joining one vertex pair are never both one chord), a fold cure of its own (a point inside every folded triangle, on its own budget), and a change to what the patch memo stores and what a split patch reports to the viewer's pick memo.

The owner decided the design on its measurements (positive signed volume on a shell thinner than δ, where a straight-midpoint split leaves zero volume). What is yours to judge is whether the build is that design, whether it is sound, and whether its rows hold it.
