## Resolved (PORT, `port/wrap-a`)

**What `node_kind.rs` already closed, and what it did not.** Its
wildcard-free match makes a new `Node` variant fail to compile until it
has a KIND WORD, and `the_node_kind_vocabulary_matches_its_committed_roster`
pins the words. That is the read half. Nothing required a `Node.*`
constructor: a variant could be given a word and no way to author it,
and every census stayed green — which is also the state `Node::Sweep`
was in, unrecorded (the stub's prose names sweep as absent; no census
did).

Closed in `crates/pncad-py/src/surface_census.rs`: `NODE_VARIANTS` and
`DATUM_VARIANTS` are `test_utils::f6_variants!` welds over `Node` and
over the six `Datum` shapes inside `Node::Datum` (one kind word, six
constructors); `NODE_CONSTRUCTORS` / `DATUM_CONSTRUCTORS` give each
variant its `Node.*` spellings or a `NotBound` reason (`Sweep`: the
kernel's sweep door is banked). `every_node_variant_has_a_python_constructor`
holds the keys equal to the welds in both directions and every spelling
declared in `pncad.pyi`; both rosters join `every_roster`, so
`the_not_bound_roster_decays` catches a `Node.sweep` bound under a
standing reason. The class estimate **M** was about right; the shape
did not need deciding beyond reusing the weld macro.

Not covered: the payload vocabularies a constructor takes
(`PatternKind`, `PartSelect`, `BooleanOp`, `MeasureExpr`); and, like
the verb census, whether a spelling means what its variant means.
