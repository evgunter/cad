## Resolved (PORT, `port/wrap-a`): already fixed

The gap this row names was closed by the binding census's **member
rule** (Ev, 2026-09-09, on `work/lib/datum-crosses-name-for-name-as-two-types.md`):
`crates/pncad-py/tests/test_binding_census.py`'s
`test_every_member_of_a_matched_type_is_spelled_or_listed` requires
every arm of a curated type Python spells identically — `Node` and
`Datum` among them — to be spelled on the namesake (an arm as a
snake-cased constructor, `Node::Extrude` → `Node.extrude`) or listed in
`MEMBERS_BOUND_AS` / `MEMBERS_NOT_BOUND`. The six `Datum::*` arms and
`Node::Datum` are in `MEMBERS_BOUND_AS`; `Node::Sweep` is in
`MEMBERS_NOT_BOUND` with its G2 / `wire_sweep` reason; and
`test_the_member_rule_catches_a_spelling_that_moves` is the rule's
falsifier, run against a stub with `Node.extrude` removed.

This row was opened 2026-09-01, predates the rule, and was never
re-read against it. A variant the kernel gains with no constructor
fails that test naming itself, which is the mechanism the row asked
for. The first pass of this lane wrote a second, Rust-side census of the
same fact in `surface_census.rs`; review found the member rule and it
was reverted. `crates/pncad-py/src/node_kind.rs`'s header now points at
the member rule as the write half of its kind-word match.
