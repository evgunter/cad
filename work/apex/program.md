**Sound refusals that sit wider than they need to.** The cone root
lanes (PR 4135) refuse a wide zone about the apex; their depth rung is
levered by the carrier's reach and their lead rung by the segment's
length; the far-nappe tell-off has no row where it decides. At
ε 1e-12 an f64 first-order bound cannot place a shallow circle ×
sphere crossing. The off-face touch lanes (PRs 4128 and 4159) keep the
pierce door for a straddling span whose roots do not settle, for a
touch beside a spline or spiric edge, and for a conic touching a torus
off its face; their four decisions have uncalibrated margins.
`face_extent` misses a sphere's, torus's or cone's interior far point,
and the vertex-vertex lane takes no curvature charge on a curved sector
face.
