## Charter

A legal body splits, and a legal pair combines: no refusal and no
shipped side that the kernel's own gate fails, on an input the operand
gate admits. Where a pose cannot be built yet, it refuses with a typed
construction gap, never a kernel invariant.

## Lanes

- **The split door's poses**: a plane tangent to a hole's or bore's rim
  at a vertex, a split through a pinch corner, a plane through a cone's
  apex, a pocket floor touching the walls, a hole in an elliptic
  section. Measure each on current main first; several may share the
  section-loop cause.
- **The curved-carrier lanes**: the edge-plane copy, the germ segment
  roster and the section copy, and the coplanar conic meeting, each of
  which reads lines and circles and refuses an ellipse.
- **Slits and their records**: the curved slit's tier-3′ record and the
  self-touch pin. These are witnesses for CLEAVE's
  `the-splits-at-rest-gate-reads-tier-3-where-a-finished-body-owes-tier-3-prime`;
  coordinate with it.
