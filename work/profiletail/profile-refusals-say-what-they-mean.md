One lane, one PR: the refusal-text rows PROFILEPROSE held, taken
together because they edit the same renders in `crates/profile/src/path.rs`
and `crates/profile/src/validate.rs` and re-pin the same rows of the
refusal census (`crates/editor-core/tests/refusal_concision_chains.rs`,
its `OVER_BUDGET` and `FILED_NO_RECOURSE` admissions).

## What it carries

Each passenger rides with this unit and is parked on it; each keeps its
own body as the spec for its part, and each closes with its own
evidence when this unit's PR lands:

- `profile-endings-say-lower-the-tolerance-and-route-by-name` — the
  turn refusal and `FILLET_OFFSET_LEVER_RECOURSE` stop saying "lower the
  tolerance"; `PathError::Escalated` stops routing its ending by
  predicate name.
- `constructed-tangent-joint-refusals-are-typed-kernel-defects` — the
  four arms that name the kernel alone, under ENCL's census
  (`a-typed-kernel-defect-ending-may-launder-a-bug-d2-forbids`), with
  the "(a kernel bug)" tags its Absorbed note lists.
- `path-pending-fillet-miss-renders-as-an-overdetermined-junction` —
  `take_pending`'s missing fillet and the two bookkeeping desyncs.
- `profile-arms-the-weld-renders-fall-short-of-the-shape-guard` —
  `ArcBelowSceneResolution` under the word budget, `InconsistentArc`'s
  repair labelled `Recourse:` and its `FILED_NO_RECOURSE` entry dropped.

## Not carried
