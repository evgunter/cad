## What

After PR 5555's ruling, the corpus will report a finding for each pin
or bolt that is seated by number. These are written today as an `Axis`
mate plus a numeric `Slide(Z)` (axis plus plane refuses), and its seat
is "flush by number". The ruling makes that a finding, with
`Pivot` as the recourse.

## Owed

Sweep the corpus, the demos and the fixtures for such placements.
Once `flag-and-pivot-pose-kinds` lands, re-mate each one as a pivot.
List them here first, so that stage 4 I's finding counts move with a
stated cause.
