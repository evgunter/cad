## Closed (2026-09-20, PR 2913)

Built as specified, under the principle the reviews settled: THE
DOORS DECIDE EDITS AND THE SOLVE DECIDES STATES. `admit_mate` is the
solve's own per-mate prefix — the walks, one `check_references` the
solve's first loop and the door both call, `admit_class`, and
`mate_coset` with its lever a closure answering `LeverArm { Formed,
Declined }` at the one row that levers a decision; the insert door
refuses `EditError::MateRefused` carrying the solve's fault
unaltered; the table's static gaps have one home, `mate::table_gap`,
read by the table and the viewer's tool; replay re-decides everything
on the datum alone and declines the rider (`Maintain::reach` states
the two replay rules). What the door does not cover is a state — a
head a rebind or a shrunk pattern strands, a re-pointed `Part`, a
loaded snapshot — which stays the solve's at evaluation; a mate on a
pair the fold never reads (two members over one instance) is refused
on the datum alone all the same. Measured by the correctness arm:
the door's fault equals the solve's to the bit; a rider asks the
reach once per part in document order and every other row asks
nothing; the band's edges agree. Closes half (1) of
`mate-clocking-has-no-gui-path` and records half (2); files
`mate-band-fault-unreachable-on-a-mate`.
