The 2-D fillet inside the PATHS lattice — corner rounding, not the
3-D blend BAND holds — as its own track, because its rows are about
the SUBSYSTEM rather than about the lattice's algebra.

Its spine is that the subsystem does not live where it runs:
`profile/src/validate.rs` carries six `FILLET_*_RECOURSE` consts whose
own docs say `validate.rs` never runs them, which is a quarter of the
fillet subsystem hosted by a file that is not its home. Around it,
`arc_carrier` has three spellings under a comment asserting it has one;
`family.rs`'s `carriers_are_identical` decides a carrier-identity
question the sixth-round ruling RETIRES at the lattice; and the Fillet
chain step takes six door refusals before a reviewer sees it, which is
a discoverability defect rather than a bug.

Four recourse rows ride with these: sentences that meter the wrong
quantity, omit a real lever, or render the shared text where a
fillet-specific one is owed.
