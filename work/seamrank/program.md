**How a union names the vertices on its seams.** EMIT built the
crossing spelling (`EdgeCrossing`), the seam-run ranks along one open
carrier (PRs 5107, 5347) and the flush rule. What it left are typed
refusals and ties, never a known wrong name. The top row is a P2: a
conventional vertex refuses typed (`NamingError::ConventionalVertex`)
on a document that reaches it, the lens ∩ tilted brick cap. Behind it:
an end-touch crossing with no witness, an in-face crossing spelled as a
seam, two seam runs that can read alike, the crossing-sense code's five
copies of one closure, and hygiene on the same files. Two rows (a seam
crossing with no first side, the rank band compared in radians) are
parked on WIRE's build of FORK-ORIENT, which retires ranks along a line.
