What went: `WireStep`, `WireTarget`, `WireArcData` and their
`from_step`/`into_step`, `from_target`/`into_target`,
`from_spec`/`into_spec`, and `WireLoopProgram` with them — with

`ProgramTarget`, `ProgramArcData` and `LoopProgram` where they are

so the document form IS the persisted form. The wire is

`WireSide`/`WireWinding` stayed, as the spec allowed: the orphan rule
puts `profile::ArcSide` and `profile::ArcSweep` out of reach of a
derive in `editor-core` and G1 layering keeps it out of `profile`, so
the move is not free for them. They changed shape rather than
content — two `#[serde(with = …)]` adapter modules,
`persist::wire::arc_side` and `arc_sweep`, named by the five
`ProgramArcData` fields that carry a tag.

`WireProfile` stayed at first and went in the fix pass below: the
door its `plane` field carried rides that field directly, so the
mirror bought nothing.

The census table's `profile Step verbs` row is 6 copies − 1 = **5**:
`profile::Step`, `ProgramStep`, `StepArg`, the content-key tag table,
and `pncad-py`'s PATHS lattice + `.pyi` (LIB's, and forced by the
orphan rule per the steelman). The steelman's verdict for that row —
"`WireStep`/`WireTarget`/`WireArcData` are field-for-field mirrors

**What stands, unchanged by this unit:** the `SegTag` member (needs the

### Fix pass, after the style review (2026-09-16)

Four things the review moved, recorded here because each changes what
the row says rather than only how the code reads:

- **`WireProfile` went too.** The `## Built` text above kept it as "the
  one mirror that carries a door". The reviewer executed the
  alternative — `deny_unknown_fields` on `ProfileProgram` with
  `deserialize_with` naming `plane_ref` on the `plane` field — and got
  the same bytes and a green suite, so the door rides the field it
  guards and the mirror is gone with the deep clone every save used to
  make. **`persist/wire.rs` now holds no mirror of a document type at
  all**: two expression languages that must not deserialize
  field-by-field, one FIELD door, and the two kernel-foreign tags.
- **The pin is two rows, not one.** `PERSISTED_SPELLING` is a SET, and
  a set cannot see a swapped `Ccw`/`Cw` or a `spec`/`spec2` exchange.
  `tests/wire_rv_bytes.rs` (adopted from the review's probe branch)
  freezes the BYTES of a program reaching every member of all four
  vocabularies plus all fifteen `Expr` shapes — thirteen of which no
  row pinned before it.
- **The two tag adapters come from one macro.** Two structurally
  identical `#[serde(with)]` modules is the shape `S4` exists to
  remove; `foreign_tag!` mints both from their module name, kernel type
  and two tag words, so a third pair is one line.
- **The `viewer` spellings, for the recount.** The reviewer found four
  more spellings of this vocabulary in `crates/viewer/` — `PathStep`,
  `PathTarget`, `ArcSpec`, `PathVerb`. They are **outside S4's count
  above**, which is the kernel-side census (`profile` → `editor-core` →
  the binding), and they are not this row's to collapse: the viewer
  authors through the public API and its enums are a chrome vocabulary,
  not a persisted one. Two of them are already carried —
  `PathTarget` by LIB's live
  `both-authoring-surfaces-are-short-of-the-target-vocabulary` (whose
  table this PR corrected from six spellings to five), and `PathStep`
  by the new CHROME row
  `viewer-sketch-claims-a-compile-break-a-probe-verb-does-not-cause`.
  `ArcSpec` and `PathVerb` are carried by neither; nobody has measured
  whether they are short.
