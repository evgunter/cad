## Re-homed from WIRE (2026-09-15): a ratified persisted-vocabulary correction, plus the fixture it was split from

Two rows arrive on EDIT's slate, one carrying a fresh ruling.

**`cut-off-arc-persists-as-a-corner-arc` — RULED, ready to take.**
A ruled band (a cylinder meeting a plane along a ruling — a rod with a
flat) ends at a **transverse cap**, where the band is cut off in the cap
plane's own section of it. Nothing is minted there: no ball turn, no
octant. But `emit_blend` keys that cut-off arc onto
`RoleSeg::CornerArc`, whose doc claims *"where an octant meets one of its
three incident blends"*, and the cap feet and surviving rim piece ride
`FootVertex` and `BandCut`, whose docs say *"corner foot"* and
*"shortened meridian"*.

Ev ruled (in chat, 2026-09-15) **option (b′)**: keep one role, repair
what it claims. Not a new `CutOffArc` variant — that would bake a
geometric classification (`CornerConfig::TransverseCap` vs
`ThreeConvexEdges`, decided by `fillet3_cap_transverse`) into a persisted
identity, so an edit crossing that boundary would re-spell the name of
an entity that is structurally unchanged. `RimSupport`'s own doc already
names that hazard class: *"a pair of structural ROLES, not a geometric
classification."* A discriminator FIELD was considered and rejected too —
unlike `BandSlit`, `(vertex, edge)` is already unique here, so the field
would be derived data inside an identity.

The work: three doc comments state the structural role instead, and
`CornerArc` is renamed to a structural word (`EndArc` suggested, not
ratified — the taker picks). `CornerFace` is deliberately NOT renamed:
it names the octant patch, which exists only where there IS a corner.
The row carries the full argument, the counterargument that was weighed,
and the one thing to check first — `crates/sweep/README.md` V3 keeps
this vocabulary fillet-named on purpose, and a lane that reads the rename
as crossing that fence **stops and reports rather than amending**.

Why EDIT: `role.rs` is EDIT's by `work.py territory`, `emit_blend.rs` is
claimed by no program, and EDIT's charter is the persisted recipe and the
edit vocabulary. WIRE owned neither file — the row reached it at DOCM's
exit sweep on a glob (*"`names/emit*.rs` … are in WIRE's paths"*) that
WIRE's explicit path list does not back. The same error moved
`blend-slit-name-collides-when-two-rims-share-a-meridian`, which has gone
to BLEND.

**`a-ruled-carve-has-no-editor-fixture-…` — filed, and deliberately not
attached to the above.** `editor-core/tests` has no ruled fixture and no
mention of `TransverseCap`, so those three roles have never been minted
through the document layer. The (b′) work does not need it — a rename and
three comments — and making a test the price of a vocabulary correction
would have left it dead had the ruling gone the other way. It is worth
having on its own merits: it is the only row that would have caught the
mis-description, and the only one that would notice if the rename broke
an emitting arm.
