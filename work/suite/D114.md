## Finding

### S168. The recording scalar is asserted to be a wrapper by no test

Three tests in `crates/editor-core/tests/` stand where a differential between the recording scalar and f64 would go: `m5_pr5_corpus_probe.rs`, and `m4_pr8_k_probe.rs`'s `corpus_evaluates_green_at_probe` and `run_doc`.

**All three assert one-sided GREENNESS at `Probe`. None compares a `Probe` result against an f64 one**, and no test in this tree does. Greenness is evidence for bit-identity and is not a check of it — a `Probe` lane that decided differently but still produced a valid body would pass every one of them. Greenness is also **tolerance-dependent**, which a *"bit-identical"* framing hides: it is why the dump is swept at three ε, and it is why the argument *"there is nothing per-ε about this claim"* was wrong.

So the wrapper property — *the recording scalar is a wrapper, never a different arithmetic* — is checked by nothing, and the two tests F8 exists to make run are where the check would sit. That gap is **D114**.

## Closed by the differential (2026-09-15, PR 2630)

`m4_pr8_k_probe.rs::probe_agrees_with_f64_bit_for_bit_over_the_corpus`
evaluates every registered corpus document at `f64` and at `Probe` and
asserts identity across nine of `NodeValue`'s ten fields plus the
result arm, the mass-properties certificate and the boolean payload's
surviving contacts. Every scalar goes through `to_bits`, never `==`, so
`-0.0` and `0.0` and two NaNs are distinguished. It runs at 1e-6, 1e-9
and 1e-12.

**The question the row called open was not open.** `Probe` is
`pub struct Probe(pub f64)`, a transparent newtype, so the wrapper
property is bit-identity proper rather than agreement within a
tolerance. What the row was right about is the trap underneath: ε is an
**input to the evaluation**, not a comparison tolerance, so the row
fixes ε, evaluates both arithmetics there, and compares bits — at each
of three ε. Collapsing the sweep on the grounds that bit-identity is
ε-independent is the argument the row already records as wrong.

**The finding as filed was false, and this is the part to carry
forward.** `S168` says none of the greenness tests compares a `Probe`
result against an f64 one *"and no test in this tree does"*.
`crates/profile/tests/review_m2_pr2_probe.rs` already did — it walks
vertex positions, bulges and arc carriers through `to_bits()` on both
arms and asserts equality, it carries the same recording-scalar
anti-vacuity idiom, and it is rostered and runs under the default
selection. The true gap was narrower and real: **profile's canonical-form
lane was checked; editor-core's evaluation lane was not.** That scope is
now stated at three claim sites rather than in a PR body, and
`work/guard/probe-run-floor-does-not-hold-the-probe-f64-differential.md`
had its cause corrected — it had inherited the finding's sentence as the
reason for a floor raise that is right for other reasons.

**The instrument's own failure mode was found by review, not by CI.**
The first version had three anti-vacuity guards and the review defeated
it anyway: the mass-properties arm — the one arm ε actually reaches, and
whose subject is stored nowhere so nothing else would notice — had no
counter, and disabling it for all 25 documents left the row green. It
now carries `props` and `contacts` counters, asserted positive and
printed, and the same attack panics with *"the one channel here that eps
reaches was compared on nothing while every other guard stayed
satisfied"*. A guard that counts feeding rather than presence is the
difference; both counters increment only after their assertion has run
and only for a non-empty record.

**What it does not compare, stated rather than implied.** Curve
carriers, surface geometry and pcurves are outside the shared feed, so a
divergence confined to a NURBS control net passes. `contacts` and
`carried` are filled on the instantiate path alone and the registry has
no instantiate op, so those two comparisons hold **vacuously today** —
disclosed, not counted as coverage. `placement` has no `PartialEq` and
is compared as `Debug` text, which is a bijection with the bits for
every finite value but prints every NaN as `NaN`. `witness` is a
reserved empty slot. Three documents carry no `result`; 25 take the
certificate arm.

**Red-first receipts, re-taken against the final tree** after the first
set proved not to reproduce: a one-ULP nudge in `Probe`'s `Add` reds the
mass-properties assertion, the same nudge with that arm disabled reds
the node digest independently, and a lane discriminator in the content
key reds a channel the value digest cannot see at all.

**Residue, all with their own files.** `work/tint/`:
`mass-properties-bit-comparison-has-thirty-spellings` (31 files, ~110
occurrences, and the spellings disagree about whether the pads are in
the comparison), `corpus-result-node-loops-skip-silently` (the same
unguarded `result` loop at f64), `value-channel-digest-tag-24-collides`.
`work/ciw/` carries the two prose sites that said no test compares the
two; `work/guard/` the floor raise; `work/instr/` the K-REPORT citation.
