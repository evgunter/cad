## Weighed (2026-10-25) and put to Ev

A designer pair weighed this over two rounds and converged. The current
tree differs from the body above:
- `SAFE_ASPECT` is now `nurbs_cert::ASPECT_CAP`;
- geom's `RATIONAL_METER_SPLITS` was removed in PR 4860, so C23's
  hand-sync no longer exists;
- the 12-vs-6 split is two meters' sample densities, which size
  nothing;
- the missed constant is `nurbs_cert::MAX_RESIDUAL_SQ = 8`, which is the
  halving restated by hand.

Only one kind of constant was ever undecided: the headroom kind (δ/2,
the sphere's 1.25, the 8). The joint recommendation, put to Ev with the
ratified text it changes:
- a sizing rule in a new `crates/mesh/README.md` `[mesh.sizing]`: each
  lane sizes its worst admissible triangle to exactly δ, by a pinned
  theorem, with no global factor;
- rim chords tightened to the face grid, which the sphere and cone lack
  today;
- named headroom on any lane whose theorem is unwritten;
- A = 16 restated as the NURBS split's regularizer in
  `docs/TESS-BUDGET.md`;
- a no-count, no-shape sentence in `[tolerance.chordal]`.

The units that build it are filed once Ev answers. A related defect is
filed on TESS: `a-cone-rim-chord-joined-to-a-higher-row-certifies-above-delta-s`.

## Ruled (Ev, PR 5708, 2026-10-25)

Ev adopted the recommendation, amending the bound: "idk about
*exactly* delta (e.g. if we had some kind of repeated halving it could
be simplest to just require that it's > delta/2), but yes we should
generally aim to never overrefine". `[mesh.sizing]` now states the
rule: each lane's worst admissible triangle certifies within δ and
above δ/2, by a pinned theorem, with no global factor. On A = 16: "it
can also be changed if it ends up not being beneficial; it seems like a
heuristic that might be best replaced with what we actually mean".

The build is `mesh-sizes-each-lane-by-its-worst-admissible-triangle`.
Replacing A is PREDTAIL's `the-aspect-cap-stands-in-for-the-sliver-criterion`.
