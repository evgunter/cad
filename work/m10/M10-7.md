The ceiling, measured (M10-3, pinned by
`crates/editor-core/tests/m10_3_driver_interval.rs`'s
`a_macroscopic_box_refuses_all_of_its_mass_as_budget_today`): the
certification predicates include checked IDENTITIES — an edge endpoint
lies on its carrier (`carrier_endpoint_start/end`,
`crates/geom-brep/src/certify.rs`), consecutive side walls are cosurface
(`side_planes_cosurface`, `crates/sweep/src/extrude.rs`) — whose margin
is exactly zero in real arithmetic for every parameter value and whose
interval enclosure over a box of width w is `[0, c·w]` (c ≈ 2–4), because
the two sides of the identity mention the same parameter and interval
evaluation cannot see that the occurrences are one number. A leaf goes
definite only when c·w < ε, so a certified leaf is a few ε wide; a
±0.05 band on a 1.0 nominal (10^8 ε) refuses all of its mass as Budget.
Three predicates observed, 54 more of the same shape listed in PR #1231.

What is owed: certification that is aware of the parameters — an
identity in p must certify over a macroscopic box. Design conversation
first (with Ev), then a spec. Candidate shapes, to be argued in the
design PR: (a) a dependency-aware lane scalar (affine forms over the
box's axes as noise symbols, interval remainder) so identities affine in
p cancel exactly and nonlinear ones widen as O(w²); (b) discharge by
provenance for same-object identities (the endpoint IS the carrier's
evaluation at t0 by construction); (c) per-site re-association. The
unit's first deliverable either way is the ceiling RE-MEASURED after the
change on the two-hole plate, so the next limit is named rather than
assumed.
