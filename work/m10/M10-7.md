The ceiling, measured (M10-3, pinned by
`crates/editor-core/tests/m10_3_driver_interval.rs`'s
`a_macroscopic_box_refuses_all_of_its_mass_as_budget_today`): the
certification predicates include checked IDENTITIES — an edge endpoint
lies on its carrier (`carrier_endpoint_start/end`,
`crates/geom-brep/src/certify.rs`), consecutive side walls are cosurface
(`side_planes_cosurface`, `crates/sweep/src/extrude.rs`) — whose margin
is exactly zero in real arithmetic for every parameter value and whose
interval enclosure over a box of width w is `[0, c·w]` (c ≈ 2–4), because
the two sides of the identity mention the same parameter and interval
evaluation cannot see that the occurrences are one number. A leaf goes
definite only when c·w < ε, so a certified leaf is a few ε wide; a
±0.05 band on a 1.0 nominal (10^8 ε) refuses all of its mass as Budget.
Three predicates observed, 54 more of the same shape listed in PR #1231.

What is owed: certification that is aware of the parameters — an
identity in p must certify over a macroscopic box. DECIDED in chat with
Ev (2026-09-03), written as ERROR-DESIGN revision E12 (the `[ev]` PR
this item waits on): the SYMBOLIC identity tier — a hash-consed
expression DAG over the parameter symbols beside the lane value, an
on-demand exact-coefficient polynomial normal form with opaque atoms
for sqrt/transcendentals/literals and a freezing budget, and a `decide`
protocol that answers an identically-zero margin `Zero` at any box
width (recorded in k_stats as its own outcome). Affine forms rejected
(√ε ceiling on nonlinear identities); per-site re-association rejected
(sites see values, the dependence is lost upstream); provenance tokens
kept in reserve for a same-object family the census might show. The
frontier — implicit (iterated) quantities — is S-CERT's
`work/cert/param-box-certification-of-implicit-quantities.md`.
Acceptance: the two-hole plate's ±0.05 mm study certifies with
refusals that are flips, slivers or tail; the M10-3 limit row re-cut as
the positive pin; the ceiling re-measured. The E3 amendments (extent
lever, binary `min_clearance`) ride this unit.

**Where the unit landed** (PR 1725, 2026-09-04). The tier is built and
the acceptance clause above is met on the SLAB and not on the PLATE, and
the difference is measured rather than argued:

- The M10-3 limit row is re-cut as the positive pin: the ±0.05 band on
  the 1.0 nominal certifies WHOLE, at the shipped depth budget, nothing
  refused. The widest whole-certifying half-width moved from `ε/8` to
  `0.4883` at the default ε — about `3.9·10^9` — and it is
  ε-DEPENDENT (0.4390 / 0.4883 / 0.4979 at 1e-6 / 1e-9 / 1e-12), which
  is the signature of the mechanism that now bounds it.
- **The two-hole plate's real study does NOT certify, and its ceiling is
  unmoved** (`7.81e-7` of the study with the tier on and off alike). The
  cause is one named family: an arc rim's carrier is
  `Circle { u_ref: (q − c).normalize(), radius: r }`, so its endpoint
  residual is zero iff `‖q − c‖ = r` — a fact about the radius's SIGN,
  not a rational-function identity. That is E12's reserved
  discharge-by-provenance family, and the census names it, which is the
  condition E12 set for taking the reserve.

So E12's own acceptance sentence ("the two-hole plate's REAL study …
returns certified leaves") is NOT satisfied, and this item should not be
read as closing it. What is owed next is a decision on the reserve: a
provenance token verified at the f64 witness point and discharged
structurally, which is a design conversation rather than a follow-on
implementation. The frontier item
`work/cert/param-box-certification-of-implicit-quantities.md` carries
the family beside the implicit ones.
