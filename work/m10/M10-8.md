**The family the symbolic tier misses.** A swept arc's carrier is
built as `Circle { u_ref: (q − c).normalize(), radius: r }`
(`crates/sweep/src/swept.rs`), so the rim endpoint residual
`carrier.eval(t0) − q` is zero iff `‖q − c‖ = r` — a fact about the
radius's SIGN (a square root's positive branch), not a rational-function
identity, and no polynomial normal form or budget reaches it. Measured:
the two-hole plate's real study (±0.05 mm spacing, σ = 0.01 mm radii)
certifies whole only below 7.81e-7 with the tier on or off; first
refusal beyond it `carrier_endpoint_start`, enclosure `[0, 1.25e-9]`.
E12's reserve clause ("discharge by provenance … taken only if the
census shows a family the symbolic tier misses") is triggered by that
measurement.

**What is owed.** A session door through which a CONSTRUCTOR registers
an identity it guarantees — `session.assert_equal(a, b)` on two DAG
nodes (or an equality `NF(a) = NF(b)` as a rewrite axiom) — verified
NUMERICALLY at the f64 witness point where a point residual is tight
(so a constructor that lies is caught), and consulted by the normal
form so `‖q − c‖ − r` is zero over the box. The profile's validated
arc (its radius and center are consistent by validation) is the first
registrant; the sweep's arc carrier the first consumer. Soundness: an
axiom is a theorem about the construction, checked at the witness; the
tier's clause-1 domain gate still applies; the K instrument records
axiom-discharged decisions distinguishably from normal-form ones.

**Acceptance** (E12's own, carried over from M10-7): the two-hole
plate's real study returns certified leaves whose refusals are flips,
slivers or the recorded tail; the tour's stop 1 becomes the certified
study; the M10-7 census's "explicit-but-missed" family is discharged
and re-measured; the ceiling re-measured. Spec follows after M10-7's
reviews conclude (the mechanism may be shaped by them).

## The family is WIDER than "an arc rim's endpoint" (M10-7's reviews, 2026-09-04)

Both reviews exercised the tier end-to-end on CURVED geometry, and the
measurement widens this item's scope. Recorded here rather than left in
the review reports, because the acceptance clause above is written
against the narrow reading.

**R2, a filleted L-bracket with two bores** (an arc stands between the
profile and its extruded walls, which is the ordinary shape of a
machined part): **it certifies nothing at any scale, with the tier on or
off — a factor of exactly 1.0.** The slab's `3.9e9` does not generalise
past a straight-walled prism.

Per-predicate on that document, the arc family collapses wider than the
rim-endpoint reading predicts: `carrier_on_surface_1`,
`carrier_on_surface_2`, `witness_on_surface_1` and `witness_on_surface_2`
decide **0 symbolically**, where the census reports 216/72 for the first
two on the slab. So it is not only `carrier_endpoint_start/_end` that
the missing radius fact blocks — every predicate that has to place a
point ON an arc-derived surface is blocked by the same thing, because
the surface's own parameterisation carries the normalized `u_ref`.

**R1, a parametric rectangle chain**, refuses at the guided lift's step 3
at every width. That refusal is PRE-EXISTING and outside M10-7's diff;
it is noted here only so the two curved-geometry results are not read as
one finding.

**What this changes for this item.** The registrant is not "the swept
arc's rim endpoint" but "the profile's validated arc", and the consumers
are every carrier and surface derived from it. The acceptance clause
should be re-cut against a curved document that certifies NOTHING today,
not only against the two-hole plate: the plate at least moves its
refusal, and the L-bracket does not move at all.

**And a caution the measurement supports.** A provenance axiom that
discharges `‖q − c‖ = r` fixes the endpoint residual. Whether it also
fixes `carrier_on_surface_*` depends on whether those margins reduce to
the same fact once the radius is known, or whether each needs its own
registered identity. That is a question to answer with a measurement
before the mechanism is designed, not after: register the one axiom on
R2's bracket behind a flag and count what moves.
