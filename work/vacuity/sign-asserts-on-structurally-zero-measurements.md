**Refinement to this issue's framing, worth carrying into the sweep.** The mechanisms are not one but (at least) two, and `signed_volume_lifted`'s doc named only the first until PR 1389:

1. **Coplanar boundary** — every fan triangle degenerate (the two-band sphere/cone patches, whose meridians lie in one plane).
2. **Mirror-paired faces** — each face's fan is perfectly healthy on its own; it is the SUM over the body that cancels identically. This is the donut, and it is invisible to any per-face check.

A sweep looking only for mechanism 1 will miss mechanism 2 entirely, which is how the donut survived this long.

**A cheap discriminator for whoever runs the sweep**, which is what makes it mechanical rather than a per-fixture symmetry argument: one face's lifted fan is exactly `(q − o)·A_L`, where `A_L` is that loop's own vector area. So the body's total is `Σ_f (q_f − o)·A_{L_f}` — a structural zero is precisely the case where the `A_{L_f}` cancel across faces, and it can be detected by folding the per-loop area vectors alone, without meshing or lifting anything. (Corollary, also worth recording: because that expression is LINEAR in the lift, "the lift must be an interior surface point" is an overclaim — what a lift must do is separate the mirrored pair along `A_L`. PR 1389 corrects that doc and pins the linearity with a row.)

Measured on `mesh/1-walk-anchor` at PR 1389's fix pass; the census above is a committed row (`r1_sweeps_the_revolve_fixtures_for_more_structural_zeros`), so it fails loudly if the set moves.
