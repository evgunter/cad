## The defect

`crates/editor-core/src/assembly.rs`'s `attribute` resolves a declaration for a `ValidationError::CensusUnsupported` finding from the **one** face the error carries:

```rust
ValidationError::CensusUnsupported { entity: topo::EntityId::Face(f) }
    => named(by_face(*f), Attribution::Declined),
```

The census's subject is a face **pair**. Three consequences follow, and nothing guards any of them:

1. **A face two mates declare answers to whichever declaration comes first in document order.** Realistic: `MateFault::SelfMate` refuses only same-*instance* pairs, so one plate face can be minted as `(f,g)` and `(f,h)` by two different mates.
2. **`topo::census`'s conformal-patch sweep reaches this refusal on an *undeclared* pair.** At `census.rs:994` and `:1024` the `None` and typed-refusal arms of `chart_overlap` fire *before* `declared.faces` is consulted — so a face of an undeclared pair may still be declared against a third face, and the finding is attributed to a mate whose declaration the census never examined.
3. **`sweep_conformal_patches` always carries `EntityId::Face(fa)` — the first face of the pair in arena order, never `fb`.** So when `fb` is the declared one and `fa` is not, the lookup **misses entirely**: the finding becomes `Unattributed` and the whole assembly falls to `AssemblyError::AtRest` instead of `AssemblyError::Uncertified`.

The defect is therefore not *"picks the first of two declarations"*. It is ***only ever consults one of the two faces***, and **which one gets a chance is decided by arena order rather than by declaredness.**

## What is NOT broken, and why — checked, not assumed

The `AtRest` / `Uncertified` split stays **sound**, and structurally so. Both of #833's reviewers verified this independently:

- Every production `CensusUnsupported` push (`census.rs:994, 1024, 1489, 1894, 1943, 1953, 1963`) is a **cannot-decide** path.
- The **refuting** direction at the same door goes to a **different variant**: `census.rs:1955`'s `Some(Ok(ChartOverlap::Empty))` raises `StaleContactDeclaration`, which `attribute` classifies `Refuted`.
- `assemble`'s split reads only the **relation**, never the identity of the declaration.

So `Declined` is safe whichever mate `by_face` names. Today this is a **message / API-fidelity** defect. A GUI highlighting "the offending mate", or any caller branching on `Attribution::declaration()`, would be the first real victim — as would case 3, which changes the *error arm* a caller sees, in the conservative direction.

## The fix

`ValidationError::CensusUnsupported` carries `entity: EntityId`. The face-pair arms know both faces at the push site and discard one. Carrying the **pair** — a new variant, or a widened one — would let `attribute` use `by_pair` and answer exactly. That is a `topo` signature change with its own census rows, which is why it is not in #833.

Also worth deciding in the same change: whether an unsupported *undeclared* pair should reach `attribute` as a declared-face decline at all (case 2), or be distinguishable at the kernel.

## Interim state, landed in #833

Nothing is silently wrong at the site any more:

- the arm carries the width-1 caveat and names the arena-order half;
- `AssemblyError::Uncertified`'s doc, its `Display` string, and `Attribution::Declined`'s doc said *"declining a **declared pair**"*, which the code never established — all three now say *declining to certify a **face** that a declaration names*, which is what `attribute` guarantees;
- `Attribution::Declined` carries the caveat where a caller meets it.
