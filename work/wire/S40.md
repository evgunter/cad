## Deferred, and the M6 citation is stale (2026-09-11)

Read against `8851abb`. Both halves of bullet 1 are **deliberate
forward declarations**, and both say so at the site:

- `WitnessSlot {}` — `crates/editor-core/src/eval/mod.rs:317`,
  *"M6's solved-assignment slot, as a type stub (spec D2)"*, occupying
  `NodeValue::witness` at `:296` (*"RESERVED empty slot: the solved
  witness assignment (M6 fills)"*) and constructed at `:2885`.
- `NodeErrorKind::WitnessBifurcation` — `:1223`, *"NEVER constructed
  before the M6 solver: the arm exists so the solver lands as logic,
  not a schema change."*

**Neither is dead code by accident; both cite a milestone that came and
went without filling them.** `docs/DESIGN.md`'s roadmap records M6 as
complete and as *"the SSI generic-`T` lift, loft/sweep assembly,
composition surgery"* — no witness solver — and says in as many words
that *"the sketch solver is NOT in [M10's] slate and re-opens as its own
design pass when constraint-driven sketches have a consumer."* So the
reservation is real and the trigger it names is wrong: this is Q4's
shape from `docs/prompts/reviewer-style-lane.md`, a premise cited by two
doc comments that the thing they cite has invalidated.

Status **`deferred`** rather than `open`, ratified by that roadmap
sentence: the reservation waits on a consumer, and `work/README.md` is
explicit that a row waiting on a *named trigger* is `parked` while a row
**ratified as not-now** is `deferred` with the ratification cited in the
body — there is no item or PR here that lint could watch fire, so
`deferred` is the honest one. It is the same shape as this program's
`two-verb-seats-do-not-compose`, and for the same reason.

**What is still owed, and is not deferred**: the two doc comments say
"M6" and M6 is over. Re-citing them against the roadmap's sketch-solver
sentence is a two-line prose fix with no design question in it, and it
rides the next WIRE PR that touches `eval/mod.rs` (the
`frame-f64-placement-is-re-evaluated-per-profile` unit). Deleting the
stubs is the thing this row does **not** decide: it would move the eval
value type and the `NodeErrorKind` vocabulary for a reservation that is
genuine, and the roadmap says the consumer may yet arrive.
