The tour has fallen behind the kernel: four of its fudges dodge gaps
that have since closed, three planned stops are unblocked, and a month
of landings (sphere-pair booleans, bored sections, cap engraving, loft
correspondence, joined-rim fillets, gauges) appear in it only as
refusal pins or not at all. SHOW catches it up, folding new
capability into existing montage cells wherever the result is a
better part, so the sheet grows by one cell (the snowman) rather than
by six.

The budget is 35 rather than 30 because every row is one scene in one
file, against the public API, with the kernel's own test as its
oracle: a demo M is cheaper to run than a kernel M, and splitting the
slate would put the one-file serialisation (below) across two
orchestrators. Charter and order: `work/show/plan.md`; narrative in
`work/show/log.md`.
