## Charter

An in-band refusal means the user's geometry is near the decision
refused. Each row is a measured pose that refuses in band where it is
not; each owes the decision that lands there, then the reading that
clears it, or a typed refusal whose lever is true.

## Posture

region-door, curve-pair and section re-pose work, and some may build
now. A row that turns out to be a two-margin or lever-extent shape
moves to HONEPAIR.
