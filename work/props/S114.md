## Re-classed E → M (2026-09-12, the DOOR orchestrator), before dispatch

The row says *"two four-line test helpers in one crate; only question is
the shared home"*, and the cut's class estimate of **E** followed from
that. Measured against the tree, the population is **eighteen distinct
definitions across four crates, three of them in `src/`**. The estimate
was wrong and the row is re-classed **M**.

The shape measured: a named `fn` or `let` binding whose body is the
interval-containment predicate `x.lo() <= v && v <= x.hi()`.

| where | definitions |
| --- | --- |
| `crates/geom/src/curves.rs:1246`, `surfaces.rs:1263` | 2 (the row's own pair) |
| `crates/geom-core/src/spline/net.rs:365` (`holds`) | 1 |
| `crates/geom/tests/` (curves ×5, surfaces ×3) | 8 |
| `crates/geom-brep/tests/` | 2 |
| `crates/sweep/tests/` (`encloses` ×2, `assert_brackets` ×3) | 5 |

**Why the class moves.** With two sites in one crate the home is
obvious and the diff is small. With eighteen across `geom`, `geom-core`,
`geom-brep` and `sweep` — three of them in production code — the home is
a real decision (`geom-core` beside `Interval`, or `test-utils`, which
is not currently a dev-dependency of `geom`), and the sweep lands in
S-TCOST's and S-TINT's declared `*/tests/*` territory. That is an `M` on
this program's own definition: multi-file, plus a small design call
about where a shared thing lives.

**Two caveats on the number, stated because this program has been bitten
three times by counts nobody measured.**

- **It is a floor.** The scanner reads a four-line window from each
  candidate line, so a helper whose body wraps further is missed, and
  one routed through a differently-named wrapper has no syntactic tell.
- **`assert_brackets` is arguably not this class.** The three
  `crates/sweep/tests/fillet_h*_interval.rs` sites are assertion
  helpers that contain the predicate rather than pure predicates that
  return it. Whoever takes this row decides whether they share a home
  with the other fifteen or are a neighbouring shape; counting them in
  is the conservative reading and is what the eighteen reflects.

The lane that takes it states the population its own instrument finds
rather than inheriting either number.
