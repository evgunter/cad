**REACH's P1 slate.** Eight rows, all on shapes a person meets
early. A torus touching a coaxial or axis-normal partner, or touching
at a point that is not elliptic, refuses R-tan; a torus near a tilted
cut's ellipse rim stops at the extent scan. Two sphere faces meeting
in a circle inside both refuse `SpheresMeet`, and a closed sphere
group's escape is answered two ways. `topo::separation` and the census
backstop grant disjointness only along world axes, so whether a verdict
certifies depends on how the parts are turned — the fact PR 4122 fixed
for the operand gate. And the boolean's operand edge gate is to be
deleted once every site behind it refuses a spline or spiric edge
typed.
