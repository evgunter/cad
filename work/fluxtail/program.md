compute the right quantity; the rows here are where a reading under
them loses precision or a refusal. A rim level recovered by a dot
product far from the frame's origin, or by `√(1 − s²)` near a pole; a
planar face summed about a far carrier origin; chord lengths rooted to
nearest and read as pads; span folds through `f64::min` that drop a
refused window; vector-norm bounds read off per-coordinate hulls; an
edge extent with four dispatchers; and a gating corpus that reaches no
collapsed-arm gate.
