The emission half of D5's persistent naming: what a verb writes into the
name table, and the two ways a name disappears from it without the
diagnosis the design promises.

`wire_loft` anchors the WHOLE emitted table on section 0's `LoopAnchor`,
so a section authored rotated or reversed relative to section 0 is
named by section 0's permutation — a silent renaming of everything
downstream. A seam JUNCTION vertex's multi-`Seam` name is a shape
`emit_union`'s collapse simply refuses. And two vanish classes —
a pruned `OrderAlong` pair and a collapsed `SideOf` fragment group —
cannot be shadow-executed and fall to the evidence-free `RecipeEdit`
fallback, which is exactly the "loud typed failure carrying the flip's
diagnosis" that DESIGN.md's naming pillar says must not degrade into a
guess.
