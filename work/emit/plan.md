## Order

`loft-anchors-every-section-with-section-zeros-map` first: it renames
everything downstream of a rotated or reversed section SILENTLY, which
is the widest consequence on this slate and the one a user cannot see.

Then the two vanish classes together
(`order-along-qualifier-records-no-partner-...`,
`side-of-vanish-by-fragment-group-collapse-...`) — both fall to the
evidence-free `RecipeEdit` fallback and the shadow-exec rung is the
same machinery for both, so specifying them apart means specifying it
twice. The two `E` rows are drive-bys in `names/emit.rs`.
