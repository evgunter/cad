**A served verdict rests on one margin, levered at the reach it
moves.** Each row is a split or boolean reading that decides two
terms as two rows and serves when both read Zero (a tilt beside an
offset, a centre beside a radius), composes two readings so the first
escalation wins where the second would settle it, or levers a reading
at an extent other than the one the decision moves (a chord for a
wedge, a whole carrier for a short arc, a Euclidean reach for a
hinge). `work/README.md` bands interval and error propagation P2.

`work/honepair/log.md`.
