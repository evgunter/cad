## Charter

A served verdict rests on one margin carrying every term it depends
on, levered at the reach the decision actually moves, and a
composition of readings answers the same whichever is written first.

## Lanes

- **Two terms, one margin**: tilt beside offset, centre beside
  radius, a tilt beside a position read earlier. The shape is the
  section classifiers' `decide_across` and `carrier_cyl_reach`: the
  zero side on `|datum| + tilt·lever`, a definite side on the datum
  shrunk by the tilt.
- **Compositions**: a conjunction that escalates on its first reading,
  one ray's reading refusing a whole walk, an overruled unit
  escalation left on the log.
- **Lever extents**: a chord for a wedge, a whole carrier for a short
  arc, a Euclidean reach for a hinge, a section's resolution.
