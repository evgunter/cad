### A tension inside the row's own statement, which the taker has to settle first

`S136`'s headline is *"a band-keyed row's NAME asserts one arm, and it is
**the arm the shipped default does not take**."* That is true of the
anchor (`refuses…` at a default that BUILDS). It is **false of the two
`recognize_pins.rs` members the row itself enumerates**: they assert a
first-class import, and the default ε = 1e-9 *does* import first-class —
the arm the default does not take is the 1e-12 one. The operative
criterion the enumeration actually used is the looser *"the name asserts
an arm that is not taken on all three bands"*.

Under the headline, the class has **one** member. Under the enumeration's
criterion, it has **at least seven**. The two readings give a 7x
different unit, and nothing in the row picks one.
