## Charter

Every question the split and the boolean ask of carriers or of
directions — coincidence, coaxiality, a point against a carrier, the
section class, coplanarity, a direction's side of a cone or a sector,
the germ order about a normal, the conic-plane root, the in-plane
frame — has one reading, an exhaustive match with one method per arm
and no fallback, that every consumer reads (`docs/DESIGN.md`, D3
"One reading per question").

## Lanes

- **The audit's four pair questions** (unbanded and unpriced, so
  each is charged the default 3; band and price them before the
  first dispatch): coincidence, coaxiality, point to
  carrier, the section tables.
- **The duplicate readers**: coplanarity, the cone side, `in_sector`
  against `within`, germ orders, the conic-plane root, the in-plane
  frame.
