**The pattern, and what it could not match.** Grep the **shape**, not the symbol: the word `Dual` is exactly what a door of this class never says. The census greps the identifiers `\bBounds\b` and `\bEnclosure\b` over `crates/geom-core/`, `crates/geom/` (and, for the handoffs, `crates/bvh/`, `crates/geom-brep/`, `crates/profile/`), strips leading-`//` comment lines, and reads every surviving hit by hand. **The alias gap is CLOSED for the two `geom` crates by construction** — every declared trait's supertrait list was read (`ControlPoint<T: Real>`, `Real`, `Bounds`, `Enclosure`, `CertifiedEnclosure`, `CertifiedBounds`, `Decide: SpanLocate`, `SpanLocate: Sealed + Real`, `KinkJacobian: Real`, `sealed::Sealed`), so no alias route into them exists. **What it still cannot match**: (1) an alias declared in a **third** crate and used here; (2) a bound reached by a supertrait obligation, `bounds-allowlist.sh`'s KNOWN GAP 2; (3) `crates/*/tests/`, deliberately — a test is not a door. What would close (1) and (2) is a walk of every generic parameter's *resolved* bound set — a `rustc` driver or a `rust-analyzer` query, not a grep.
