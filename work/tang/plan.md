## Charter

A declared kiss or cusp between two curved operands is decided by the
residual-sign story the DEV-1 set names, not by a witness the lane
happens to find; a pierce ring joins on every carrier it can be minted
on; the equal-radius intersecting family (valence 4) has a design.
Every unit here begins as a design conversation with Ev — `[ev]`
spec PRs — because each one either revises ratified ground (DEV-1,
#131's cusp ruling) or opens a shape the kernel has no precedent for.
