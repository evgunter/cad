Filed by the designers weighing `the-line-cone-reads-a-flanks-side-beside-the-one-bound-side-reading` (2026-11-02). A descent along one bound decides that bound's side of a sheet; the line cone reads it as the whole sheet's side. That holds where the faces' mixed second-order term along the line vanishes: true for plane, cylinder and cone, the only analytic surfaces here that hold a line (the line is a principal direction), and a NURBS face reads `Unresolved` → `On`. Owed: a check that trips (refuses) rather than misreads when a future ruled kind breaks the premise.
