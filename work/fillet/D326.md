## What

**The blend surgery establishes "the face `kef` kills is one this surgery
minted" six times, by six unrelated local arguments, and states it at none
of its call sites.** `crates/sweep/src/blend/surgery.rs` hands `kef` a half
at six sites — the edge-strip and corner-strut `kef`s of `blank_phase`, the
rim and rim-strut pair, and the annulus rim and seam-crossing pair. That the
argument is always a surgery-minted face (a carved strip, a corner triangle,
a band sector) rather than a source face is true at all six, but recovering
it means re-deriving which side of each `mef` became the new face, through
`MefCreated`'s *"`he_plus` is in the OLD loop"* contract, and three of the
six are only reachable that way.

**A sibling construction one crate over spells the same invariant into the
code.** `crates/topo/src/shell.rs:1398-1406` does not argue that the anchor
survives — `canonicalize_chart` PICKS `dying` and structurally refuses to
hand `anchor` to `kef`:

```rust
// `kef` kills the face of the half-edge it is given, and
// refuses a dying face that carries rings.
let (dying, he) = if fm != anchor && ring_free(body, fm) {
```

The annulus arm already reaches for this shape in prose (`surgery.rs:3208-3210`
— *"The run that moves to the NEW face is the rim side, so each support keeps
its own key"*) and implements it in `trim_chords`'s `pick(true)`, so the
carrier partly exists and is spelled once rather than at the door.

**What it buys.** `blend::naming::Retired`'s absent face channel is today a
five-paragraph argument at the type (`D323`); with the invariant carried at
the call sites it is a consequence of the code rather than a conclusion of a
doc, and the doc becomes two sentences. That is `plan.md`'s row 0 — *can the
type stop representing the state?* — answered one level up from where `D323`
asked it.

**Held while a BLEND lane is live in `blend/`** (`T-R1`'s class): `surgery.rs`
is the contended file, and two FILLET lanes were editing it when this row was
filed (PRs 1763, 1752). Cheapest taken with `D325`, which opens the corner
fusion for its own reason.
