A cut of `lever-doors-pinned-sites-await-a-geometry-read` (filed by
`oneround/lever-doors`, PR 7143, and on that branch until it merges;
this unit is stacked on it). That row lists the 59 references
`scripts/gates/lever-doors.sh` pins, by what each site's own function
holds. This unit takes the sites under its heading "Geometry in the
function" that lie in this unit's crates: the function already holds a
carrier, a surface, a cover, a `Reach` or a body with the entity, and
still hands a `Lever`, `ExtentBall` or `Reach` door a number or a point
it computed. Each reads what it holds through a door that takes the
geometry, and the gate's pin for its file falls.

Each conversion moves a reading, so each is measured: a differential
against the base over the suites that reach the site, on both lanes,
and every verdict that moves checked for direction.

Not this unit: the row's "Points or scalars only" sites and its wrapper
list, which need a record to hold more than it does (Ev's condition on
records, PR 7141, on `unsourced-levers-await-a-source`); the
`Lever::unsourced` sites.

## Closed (2026-11-23, PR 7228)

The lever sites in topo whose own function holds the geometry read it. `offset_axial`'s frame holds its scope's region and hands each consumer the bounds it is owed; the alignment and axis-parallel sines and the latitude's posture, whose every sign is acted on, read both bounds through `decide_levered` about their datum's pivot; `fate::leaving` reads its edge's cover for a line as for every other kind. A single FULL review (three MAJORs, one of them my own ruling's reach), a fix pass, a delta read, a second fix pass and a second delta read that approves with notes only. What the unit left, each in its own file:
- `a-turns-lever-is-read-from-below-for-every-caller` (P1): the turn-lever doors and their composite consumers, the design fork still in front of two designers; `plane_torus_section`'s bound and pivot wait on it.
- `lever-doors-pinned-sites-await-a-geometry-read` (the parent): the sites still pinned.
- A scope whose only vertices sit at a chart's stored datum reads zero from below and would escalate where the gate took the general door; the reviewer could not build one through a door and no row says what it should do (the second delta read's n1). Recorded here, not filed: nothing reaches it.
