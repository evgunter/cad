## 1. The curved arms — the program's reason to exist

The four PROPS never reached, in the order a lane should take them:

- **`spiric-bounded-face-area-is-unimplemented`** (H). The only row here
  that blocks a user outright: `loop_vector_area` and the torus wall's
  closed-form parse refuse `Unimplemented`, so the hollowed klein elbow
  and the sectioned torus vessel have no mass properties at all. Start
  here — it is the one with a demo behind it.
- **`sphere-flux-arm-refuses-partial-bands`** (H). The coplanar premise
  leaves the lune family outside tier 3. A premise row: require the
  decide, do not restate the premise.
- **`sphere-wedge-arm-does-not-fold-split-meridians-by-lineage`** (H).
  The torus arm already folds pieces by lineage; the sphere arm does not.
  The asymmetry is the whole finding.
- **`rim-side-and-rim-dir-group-signs-are-facts-about-cycle-order`** (H).
  Known and long open. A recorded sign that moves under a pure
  re-anchoring of a loop is the defect; the sphere-pole-side unit's
  amendment pinned anchoring invariance for its own predicate and left
  this one.

Beside them, two arm rows of the same family:

- **`an-ellipse-trimmed-ring-on-a-cylinder-wall-has-no-volume-lane`**
  (P0, H). A tilted bar unioned with a pipe refuses at the volume
  backstop (`RingOnCurvedFace`): the cylinder's Green form reads rings
  bounded by rims and rulings only, and the quadrature lane that reads
  ellipse trims takes the outer loop only. The witness is 72 of 72 ops.
- `stored-spans-read-raw-past-winding-bound` (M). The rim Δu sum reads
  stored spans raw, the same `du_of_rims` ground as the rim-side row,

- `sphere-face-with-a-hole-has-no-closed-form`,
  `sphere-flux-arm-carries-two-closed-forms-for-one-face-kind` (design)
  and `a-sphere-face-whose-boundary-encodes-no-side-is-measured-under-its-bit-alone`
  are one question about the sphere arm. If Gauss–Bonnet subsumes the
  iso-rectangle form, rings and the side encoding follow from it. They

## 4. What PROPS learned that this program should not relearn

- **A spec that asserts a premise hands the unit a defect.** Three
  consecutive failures of one premise, the last reaching main. Require a
  decide.
- **A re-baselined golden is not a re-pointed row.** Twice in one unit a
  row's *subject* moved under a change while its numbers were movable;
  moving the digits would have left it green over a loop it no longer
  tested. Check what a row witnesses before touching its constants.
- **A narrower bound is the expected outcome of a width fix and is
  therefore not evidence it is correct.** Containment is. Test against
  exact arithmetic, with cases chosen to break it.
- **`cost` is effort and `design` is a separate flag**; the legacy `D`
  on five inherited rows means "medium, and maybe a design question" and
  gets re-priced when the row is touched.
