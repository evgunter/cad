Found by G's rule H measurement (`patterns-are-index-variables`), and
not rule H's doing. A face at angle `a`, spun by `b`, against a face
written at `a + b` in another body is left unproven; so is the control
with no angle sum at all, the same face at `a` written in two bodies,
neither turned. With rule H shut both rows freeze on the budget; with
it on both come back not shown zero. The residual says "two
constructions, placed or reflected apart": the door does not settle a
seat between two separate constructions of one carrier. A ring's seats
are between two copies of one construction and prove.

Pinned as a limit by
`g_ring_closure_measure::a_seat_between_two_constructions_of_one_carrier_is_unproven`
(`crates/editor-core/tests/g_ring_closure_measure.rs:379`), which
asserts both seats unproven and reds when the door reaches either.

## Repair shape

The coincidence door settles a seat between two constructions whose
carriers are one by the symbolic tier: the two constructions' carrier
forms compared as forms, not only a construction against its own
copies. The limit row then asserts both seats proven.
