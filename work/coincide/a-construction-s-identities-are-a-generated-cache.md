Ev allowed this on 2026-10-06, as the box mitre's fallback
(`isosceles-mitre-reads-as-an-unproven-coincidence-on-every-box`,
"Answer"): "Per-node theorems … are kept only as a possible cache. One
is added only where the algebra is measured too slow or undecidable,
because a second hand-written description of an operation can drift
from its code." D10 says the same of a table of output forms: "only
ever a cache generated from the symbolic evaluation and checked
against it, never written by hand".

**The measurement that would call for it** (INTENT stage 4 C+D,
`carriers-compare-in-canonical-form`). At the coincidence door's full
pass, a box mitre's margin closes its angle term and freezes on the
feet distance, at every budget from 64 to 4096 terms
(`work/sym/the-mitres-feet-distance-freezes-at-sym.md`). A disc's
blend joints close only from 1024 terms
(`the-blend-joints-prove-only-past-the-door-s-budget`).

Parked on the mitre's SYM row: if the tier closes the feet, no cache
is needed. Whether to build one is Ev's call, put to Ev by the INTENT
orchestrator. The alternative, which the orchestrator ruled out for
now: on-demand symbolic rungs, where the default report stops at
rung 0 and a separate pass runs the symbolic rungs at the full budget.
