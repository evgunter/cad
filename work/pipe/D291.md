## What

`census.rs`'s conic arm (the `reach_box` mirror, near `:1951`) says it reads *"the same construction the boolean lane reads, so the two cannot drift"*. It does not: `boolean::boxes::edge_box` reads the exact arc box (`geom::curves::boxes::conic_arc_aabb`) and the census reads `arc_extent`'s subdivision — by design, because the census scalar carries no ordering. `EdgeBoxRule`'s conic bullet states the rule and `the_two_box_lanes_agree_face_for_face` pins the gap at the subdivision charge. The comment is a stale claim other code is instructed to rely on (S39's shape); rewrite it to name the two arithmetics and the reason.

Two siblings ride with it, both the retired `edge_box` fallback's shape: `census.rs`'s `None => conic_extent(..)` arm (a carrier with no certified parameters keeps the full turn) and `edge_axial_span`'s `params: None` arm ("the full turn, as before") are unreachable by construction — the carrier and its parameters are read from one `EdgeCurve` — exactly as the arm `edge_box` retired under D2 row 0. Delete them with the comment.

## Closed 2026-10-10

PR 4486. The unreachability claim held: `edge_box_rule` minted `ConicAmplitude` only from a `Some` certified carrier, and every caller read the params from the same `EdgeCurve`. The type now says so. `edge_box_rule` takes the certified `Option<&EdgeCurve>`, `EdgeBoxRule::ConicAmplitude` carries `params: (T, T)`, and `AxialCarrier::Conic::params` is non-optional. `census::reach_box`'s `None => conic_extent` arm and `edge_axial_span`'s `params: None` arm are deleted. `reach_box`'s comment now names the two arithmetics (the exact `conic_arc_aabb` box vs `arc_extent`'s subdivision) and why they differ.

The review fix pass carried this through the rest of the rule. Every `EdgeBoxRule` arm now carries what its consumers read: `ConicAmplitude` and `Spiric` carry the carrier for `geom`'s exact door, and `Spiric` also carries its fields. So `edge_box` no longer re-reads the certified curve, and the census spiric arm's silent `None` fallback is gone.
