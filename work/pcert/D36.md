## Re-banded P0, and what the split decides (PCERT, 2026-10-01)

The design pair weighing `S331` read every construction site and found
that `UnsupportedCarrier` means at least three things the mint must not
treat alike: a carrier the chart can hold but the closed-form lane has
no route for (the frontier, DESIGN.md's (c)); a carrier that is not on
the chart at all, or degenerate (a line or ellipse on a sphere or
torus, a cone circle that is not a rim, a sphere circle failing the
polar/meridian centring test); and an image handed to the wrong door
(a `Fitted` or `General` image at the closed-form door). `mint_faces`
swallows the variant whole and moves on, so a body whose edge does not
lie on its face, and a wiring bug, both come back from the mint `Ok`
and from tier 3 clean. That is a live wrong answer, hence P0. Both
designers recommended the split under every answer to the `S331`
question, so it does not wait on that question's ruling.

The `clear_face_caches` call on the swallowed arm is dead (`mint_face`
inserts rows only after `certify_walked` succeeds, and both entries
clear first); it goes with the split. The variant's `Display`, which
says no kernel constructor mints the carrier and routes to
`certify_fitted`, is wrong for the not-on-chart and frontier meanings.
