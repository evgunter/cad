## Re-banded P0, and what the split decides (PCERT, 2026-10-01)

The design pair weighing `S331` read every construction site and found
that `UnsupportedCarrier` means at least three things the mint must not
treat alike: a carrier the chart can hold but the closed-form lane has
no route for (the frontier, DESIGN.md's (c)); a carrier that is not on
the chart at all, or degenerate (a line or ellipse on a sphere or
torus, a cone circle that is not a rim, a sphere circle failing the
polar/meridian centring test); and an image handed to the wrong door
(a `Fitted` or `General` image at the closed-form door). `mint_faces`
swallows the variant whole and moves on, so a body whose edge does not
lie on its face, and a wiring bug, both come back from the mint `Ok`
and from tier 3 clean. That is a live wrong answer, hence P0. Both
designers recommended the split under every answer to the `S331`
question, so it does not wait on that question's ruling.

The `clear_face_caches` call on the swallowed arm is dead (`mint_face`
inserts rows only after `certify_walked` succeeds, and both entries
clear first); it goes with the split. The variant's `Display`, which
says no kernel constructor mints the carrier and routes to
`certify_fitted`, is wrong for the not-on-chart and frontier meanings.

## Closed (PR 3664, 2026-10-01)

`PcurveCertifyError::UnsupportedCarrier` split by meaning, every
construction site classified from the geometry (the per-site table is
in PR 3664's body):

- `UnsupportedCarrier { chart: SurfaceKind, carrier: CurveKind, class: UncoveredClass }`
  — the carrier can lie on the chart and no lane covers the pair yet;
  the only refusal `mint_faces` excuses, each class with its own
  recourse. Where the class has an incidence test (the sphere's general
  circle, the cone's conic sections) the excuse is granted only when
  the test reads on-chart.
- `CarrierOffChart` — a body defect, never excused.
- `ImageMismatch` — an image at the wrong door, never excused.
- `PcurveMintError::UncertifiedImage` for `chart_edge`'s missing
  certificate, classified by cause.

The swallowed arm's dead `clear_face_caches` is gone, and the typed
kinds replaced every hand-written kind string on this ground
(`chart_name`, `certify::carrier_kind` deleted). Two reviews moved it:
the sphere polar arm and the cone gates read an amplified quantity
after an in-band gate and called on-chart carriers off-chart (both now
go through an incidence test), and the cone test's lever is the low
vertex's height over one folded margin, fuzz-checked and red under
mutation. The classes still excused without an incidence test are
`work/issues/uncovered-chart-classes-have-no-incidence-test.md`; Ev's
ruling on PR 3617 retires each class's excuse as its route is wired.
