## What

One decision, the join's class reading in band (a cylinder ruling turned
3ε, pcert E3's `class_zero_rows` fixture), ends two ways at a build:

- the boolean (`crates/topo/src/boolean/edge_join.rs`,
  `JoinUndecided::write`, the `ChartClass(Undecided)` arm,
  `diag.lever_ending(COINCIDENCE_RECOURSE)`): "whether an edge meeting
  another at a vertex on one curve runs along a cylinder's ruling or
  around it is undecided: … Recourse: move the geometry", lever-only;
- the join door (`JoinRefusal::write`, the same arm shares the
  regularity arm's text): "whether two edges meeting at a vertex on one
  curve are one edge is undecided (…). Recourse: move the vertex clear
  of the pole, apex or tangency it sits near, or, if this size is
  intended, tighten the tolerance below 3e-10 m": sized, on the
  regularity reading's lever, which names no ruling.

`[tolerance.recourse]` (i): one message, one recourse per decision.
Ratified D3 `[verdicts.consumers]` says a structural act "in band
refuses with the decision's own lever ([tolerance.recourse]), at the
reading or at the result's finished-body gate". The class reading's
decision is whether the edge runs along its chart's iso family; its
lever is the edge's pose against that family, and it is a form on no
size the user chose, so no tolerance offer belongs to it. On that text
the boolean's ending is right: its subject is the class question and
its recourse a lever (pinned by
`undecided_text::the_booleans_undecided_join_ends_once`). The join door's
is wrong twice: it asks the regularity reading's question, names the
regularity reading's lever (a pole, an apex or a tangency, none of
which a ruling turned off its family sits near), and offers a
tolerance. Row 144's lean ("refuses in band sized at the result's
gate") is not ratified text and does not override D3's sentence.
Neither ending is the kernel's defect.

## Done when

The join door ends the class reading as the boolean does, on the class
question and its own lever, with no tolerance offer.
