## Charter

**An edge lying in a face that the parting does not read.** The parting skips every closed edge, reads an edge lying in a face only where a record names the pair, and reads none in a torus or spline face, so a rider keeps one edge; its rarer configurations refuse typed. Beside them, a fate cut into a complete spline face refuses SplineChart, the plane split refuses a spline edge the ring-composite arm can cross, and the strut prune refuses a curved face where kev mints no chord.

`boolean/slit.rs` is the parting's home (PR 5793 parted by crossings and filed most of these); two lanes on it at once collide.
