**The bodies extrude and revolve build, and the readings they refuse
by.** These rows were CARVE's; they part from its loft and sweep rows
by verb and by question:

- **The cap's plane.** D10 `[coincidence.structural]` says an extrude
  cap is `plane(frame, direction, depth)`; the cap is minted by Newell
  over the profile's points, so at `Sym` its normal is a function of
  every profile variable. Two rows say this for the extrude and one for the
  loft's caps, and the
  guided derived-frame escalation is very likely downstream of it.
- **The full revolve's one band.** Ev ruled on `[ev]` PR 6014 that a
  full revolve's curved wall is one band, its pole reached by a wrap
  tip (PR 6085 kept the tip). Revolve's other ulp and kink rows sit on
  the same build.
- **The readings they refuse by.** An arm escalation worded as a
  sliver, a smooth joint refused for its relative curvature, a wall
  kind refused in band where D3 takes the cone lane.
