**What a creation form answers, and in how many vocabularies.** The
viewer's creation pane grew one form per door, and each form answers
the same few questions in its own words: *what is this button waiting
for*, *is this seat empty*, *how fast does this field drag*, *which
dimensions have a unit*, *how many ways may a circle split*. Nine of
this program's eleven rows are one of those questions with several
homes, and the homes already disagree in places (a count field has two
answers; a circle split cap offers counts the document then refuses).

The other two are the seat gate those forms consult:
`combine::denotes_body` judges a body seat by node kind where the
evaluator reads a value through the placer chain, and names two of its
differences from the operand door when there are three.
