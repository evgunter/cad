`loft_body` refuses with `Pcurve(Certify { IsoUnsupported: "the seam carrier is not the chart's own boundary row (its knot/weight structure differs)" })` at `HalfEdgeKey(11v1)` on generic parameterizations at degree ≥ 2. Reproducer: identical `arc_section` sections stacked at *n* uniform heights — builds at n = 3, 5, 6, 9, **refuses at n = 4, 7, 8**; non-uniform heights refuse at every n ≥ 3. Mechanism: `pcurve_cache.rs:3444` compares knots/degree/weights by **exact** equality, and a degree-≥2 skin fit *solves* for its weights, returning them equal only to rounding — so whether the door opens depends on whether the fit's arithmetic happens to reproduce bits.

The refusal is D2-addendum row-2-shaped (valid input the lane could serve, refused on a bit accident); whoever takes it decides between honest structural comparison at a stated tolerance with an argued soundness story, or making the skin fit reproduce the chart's own boundary row exactly.
