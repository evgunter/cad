Two items, one rung apart: an `EditError` arm that carried a typed
refusal with no word for it, and six discriminants of prelude-carried
profile refusals that a caller could name only by leaving the prelude.

## Delivered

**The `meta_unversioned` arm's inner word.**

- `MetaVersionError` carried at `crates/pncad/src/document.rs` under
  the payload rule, beside `AttrKind` and `ProgramRefusal`.
  `NOT_CARRIED` 85 -> 84; the "types whose curated face is a different
  shape" family's entry for it is replaced by the paragraph that says
  what moved — the reading that held it there was that naming it was
  the per-arm-tag question, and that question is answered.
  `MetaValue` and `MetaError` do not come with it.
- `meta_version_error_tag` in `crates/pncad-py/src/tags.rs`, three
  literals over the kernel's own arms (`not_a_map`, `missing_version`,
  `version_not_int`), with its `TAG_INVENTORY` row and the delegate
  row on `edit_inner_variant_tag`.
- `edit_inner_variant_tag`'s `MetaUnversioned` arm answers that word.
  No shipped `variant` or `inner_variant` value moved: the arm's own
  word was `None`.
- `every_edit_arm_projects_the_payload_it_carries` covers **58 of 58**
  arms. The arm it could not build was the one whose third field the
  façade did not carry, and the projection is unchanged — `name` and
  `key`, the shape refusal riding on `inner_variant`.
- Census: `MetaVersionError` -> `EditError.inner_variant`, on the
  `ProgramRefusal` precedent, with the measurement (no Python door
  mints a `SetAppearanceMeta`, so the reach is the Rust pin).

**The six profile discriminants.**

Five carried onto the prelude with one stanza in `prelude.rs`, one
argued there against carriage with its falsifier:

- `ContactKind`, `EscalationSite` and `SegmentRef` (the rung under it,
  and under four `ProfileError` arms besides) — carried.
- `FilletLeg` and `FilletLegCarrier` — carried; two `CornerReason`
  arms and one `PathError` arm name the first, and the second decides
  the units of the numbers beside it.
- `NoCornerReason` — carried, and decided beside `PathNoCornerReason`
  as the item asked: the pair closes.
- `Step` — NOT carried, argued in `prelude.rs` beside the carrier with
  its falsifier: the recording half of the profile layer is uncarried
  WHOLE, and the element type is the last thing a replay caller needs.
  The carrier rule does not reach it — `ClosedLoop` does not cross to
  Python at all — so the question was the prelude's alone.
- `carried_refusal_payloads_are_matchable_through_the_prelude` gains
  the profile rung, matched exhaustively by bare prelude name.
- Census: five `INTERIOR` by the carrier rule with the measurement,
  `NoCornerReason` `different-shape` with the corner family whose rows
  already carry its two words.

**The re-sweep**, at the merge base, with counts and the drift finding
in `payload-rung-sweep-is-prose-and-a-third-run-disagrees`. The banked
set is empty: no NEW hit.

## Deviations

- `SegmentRef` is a seventh name and no row of its own. The item files
  it under `EscalationSite` ("this one has a rung under it"), and it
  is also the direct payload of four other `ProfileError` arms, so
  carrying `EscalationSite` without it would have been the rung
  stopping one short.
- `ProfileError::Structure`'s payload is named in the same stanza as
  the other side of the fence `Step` sits behind. It is not one of the
  six; saying where it stands is what makes the `Step` argument
  falsifiable rather than convenient.

## Closed

Both items close here.

- `meta-unversioned-arm-has-no-inner-word`: closed as it asked, every
  clause of "what closes it" executed.
- `payload-rung-re-sweep-finds-six-uncurated-profile-discriminants`:
  all six decided. Its methodology finding — the pattern is prose,
  re-implemented each run — is not closed with it and has its own
  file, `payload-rung-sweep-is-prose-and-a-third-run-disagrees`, which
  also records the blind spot this run adds: the sweep's curated set
  is three of the façade's four curated lists, and every one of the
  six was on the fourth.
