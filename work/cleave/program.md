**Split and boolean outputs that are wrong.** A plane split of an
ordinary planar body can hand back a half that fails tier 3: the
U-cutter pockets invert section-loop roles, and a steep cut through a
bored solid leaves a ringed cap fragment touching its outer loop. A
bore's section comes back as a square plus a cancelling disc instead
of one annular face. A flush, fully contained operand refuses
`RayExhausted` although no ray ran out. `topo` mints
`Indeterminate`s outside the funnel after a definite sign. Behind
these are the P1 rows on the same ground: the edge-midpoint copies,
the graft's narrow re-certify lane, `rehome_rings` on arc-bearing
runs, and a split that cannot declare an exact tangency.
