## Why X, and 24 points

the doors that mint them, which nobody has measured: the at-rest check,
the mesher, export and the boolean on a NURBS wall closed in the angle,
with poles as collapsed rows and (with the standard circle) interior
double knots. Three SPLICE rows and one CHARTTAIL row already name
those weak points (the measurement unit lists them). At about one hard
row each (8 points) for the three the designers expect a revolve to
hit, the row is priced 24. It is **re-cut into units once

reported**, and its points move to those units.
