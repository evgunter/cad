## Order

**By cost, and as drive-bys wherever possible.** Twenty of its
twenty-two rows are class `E` and several are one commit; dispatching
them one PR at a time would cost more than the findings do.

The two exceptions lead: `persist-err-projects-fourteen-arms-through-a-fifteen-slot-positional-tuple`
(a transposed pair in a 319-line projection is invisible, which is a
correctness risk wearing a size row's clothes) and
`correspondence-structs-coincide`, which should be read together with
whatever third instance turns up rather than collapsed on two.
