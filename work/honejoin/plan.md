## Charter

The curved join (`boolean::edge_join`, `boolean::join`), the
chord join and the split join carry an arm for every edge their gates
admit, hold their structural invariants by construction rather than by
a distance or a debug assertion, and end an undecided or unbuilt case
as an escalation or a typed "not yet" at the decision's lever, never
as a kernel defect.

## Lanes

- **Arms**: the wrap edge, the ellipse copy, the curved spur.
- **Invariants by construction**: the chart singularity, the kef of a
  completed null face.
- **Refusal class and lever**: the re-mint's kernel-defect ending, the
  adjacency escalation filed as an invariant, `JOIN_LEVER` on a
  chart-class reading.
