**The class's analytic curve-side member is filed, not fixed** — §D row **C24**. `Curve3::deriv`/`deriv2` are the identical shape one file over on the analytic arms, and `topo/src/splitting/neighborhood.rs` calls both at the same `t` under a comment naming the result *"the base-endpoint jet"* — the call sits in the `Circle | Ellipse` arm and repeats an azimuthal frame, two `sin_cos` for one jet. **C-R6 does not reach it** even though it is the same crate: the surface side had a ratified `SurfaceJet` to project onto, and the curve enum has no `CurveJet` at all, so that work is minting a public type — a design element, C-R19 tier two. (The NURBS payload's own jet doors, `NurbsCurve3::ders` and `ders1_in_span`, are what such an arm would have to read; whether it does is the design question.)

**Two shapes on the surface side are noted and deliberately not fixed:** `SurfaceJet` lives in the NURBS arm's module though it is the enum-wide primitive's type, and `crates/geom/tests/surfaces/s32_jet_projection.rs` is the eleventh copy of the workspace's stock `axis`/`u_ref`/`origin` fixture frame.

**The population, measured rather than read.** Each of the five partial accessors discards five of six; `eval`'s NURBS arm is not a member, because `NurbsSurface::eval` is its own cheaper pass. `Surface::normal` has **zero** production callers — every call site sits in a `#[cfg(test)]` module or a `tests/` file — and two crates' module docs say so in prose (`mesh/src/lib.rs`: *"`Surface::normal` is never sampled anywhere"*; `geom-brep/src/implicit.rs`: *"nothing here ever calls `Surface::normal`"*). `deriv_uu`/`deriv_uv`/`deriv_vv` have no caller outside `crates/geom` at all. **Six of `Surface`'s eight public evaluation doors are exercised only by `geom`'s own tests**: `eval` and `jet` are the only two any production path walks, `metric_floor` being the sole production caller of any of the rest and asking for the jet. Recorded because it will decide a future question and nobody will re-measure it cheaply; it is not a mandate to delete them.
