## What

`census.rs`'s `reach_box` re-derives the ARITHMETIC of `boolean::boxes::FaceBoxRule` rather than reading it, because the `Bounds` allowlist is closed to a lane that validates `Dual` bodies (`S16`). One statement of which surface kinds have a cheap sound box exists; this is the arm that still answers it twice.

## Was

## Finding

### S16. The one face-box rule is read by two arms and re-derived by a third

- **Where**: `crates/topo/src/census.rs` (`reach_box`), `crates/topo/src/boolean/boxes.rs` (`FaceBoxRule`)
- **Confidence**: sure
- **Row:** Track Q's **`D280`**

`FaceBoxRule` is the one statement of which surface kinds have a cheap sound box and by what construction — planar faces hull their boundary EDGE boxes, NURBS take the control-net hull, cone/torus/no-surface poison. `face_box` is its `f64`-bracket instantiation and `separation` calls that. **`census::reach_box` reads its ARM from the rule and re-derives the ARITHMETIC**, because the `Bounds` allowlist is closed to a lane that validates `Dual` bodies. So the question *"is this box sound"* still has two implementations, and the soundness argument for the second lives only in the comment that says why it exists.

**Why the third arm is the one that drifts.** The rule was sound when written: at M5 PR 8 the boolean operand gate was planar-only, so a planar face's edges really were all lines and the vertex hull really was a superset. M5 PR 9 retired that gate and, **in the same diff**, added the cylinder arm and the conic `edge_box` — applying the bulge argument twice in one sitting while leaving the planar sentence, whose premise that same diff had just invalidated, untouched. A second implementation is a second place for that to happen.

**Verdict:** ACCEPTED (Ev, 2026-08-18) — *"huh, i wonder how these even happened. they should certainly be unified."*
