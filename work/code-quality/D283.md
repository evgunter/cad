## What

**A TOLERANCED quantity crosses a door in two different units, and the receipt that carries it says which in prose only.** `Exhaustiveness::floor` is `pub` on a `pub` struct and holds meters from `account_r3`, chart units from `account_chart_plane` — the latter divided by the chart speed at `ssi.rs`'s call. `D282` corrected the doc to name both lanes; **this row is the design question the doc fix leaves standing**, and it turns on the floor being toleranced rather than a domain budget: `SsiDomain::floor` is `SSI_FLOOR · band.zero() · floor_scale`, and `seed_floor`'s own doc draws the distinction — *"the ACCOUNTING floor stays tied to ε because that one is a proof obligation"*, while the seeding floor is deliberately tied to the extent instead. So this is not a stopping budget that may live in whatever space the search runs in; it is the proof obligation's own threshold. **The conversion is sound in DIRECTION and lossy in tightness**: `speed` is an upper bound on the chart derivative, so refusing at `floor_m / speed` refuses only once the cell's meters extent is certainly under the floor — conservative where the bound is loose, never the other way. **What is unguarded is the reporting boundary**, and three things say so: every other field of the struct is a unit-free count, so `floor` is the sole dimensional one; the hedge has already replicated to `SsiError::ExhaustivenessInconclusive::cell_width` (*"in meters (or chart units)"*); and `geom-brep/tests/m5_pr7_ssi.rs` carries a helper spelled `assert_floor_is_the_meters_floor_over_the_chart_speed`, i.e. consumer-side knowledge of which door built the value, encoded in a name because no type carries it. **#762 is the demonstration that the conversion has teeth**: a non-finite speed drove the chart floor to exactly `0`, and the caller got `CellBudget` rather than the true diagnosis. The repo has ratified vocabulary for this shape — S22's zero-sized `Tol` witness, and `C13` is the same complaint one crate over (*"ε has no type of its own"*). **The question for Ev: does the ε-typing conversation reach this receipt** — a `Floor` newtype or a lane tag on `Exhaustiveness` — or is a `pub` field documented as two units acceptable for a value that no production caller currently reads back out? Note the answer sets the precedent for `cell_width` too. **Raised by the D282 lane's own reviewer, 2026-08-28; not a lane's to settle.**

**Verdict:** ACCEPTED WITH QUALIFICATIONS, row by row (Ev, 2026-08-18). On `CAD_TOLERANCE_EPS`: *"worth an investigation for good alternatives but I think this state is unfortunately basically correct — we need epsilon to be the same everywhere and so it's risky to pass it individually, but we also want it to be configurable without completely rebuilding. Would be very open to better ways to achieve those goals though!"* So the constraint is **two-sided** — uniformity *and* configurability-without-rebuild. On `k_stats`: *"already tracked but is absolutely a problem that should be fixed."* On `same_chart`: *"good catch as well."*

**S22 row 1 DECIDED (2026-08-19): keep the `OnceLock`; ε gets a provenance channel, not a thread.** Then **S22 row 1 REVISED (2026-08-21): threaded after all — as a witness, not a value.** Ev, 2026-08-21: thread ε, at every call site. This reverses the *"do not thread ε"* half of the 2026-08-19 ruling and nothing else — the `OnceLock` keeps its place and its enforcement job, no session object, no per-model ε, no mixed-ε assemblies, and the provenance channel (#659) is unaffected. Both sides of the earlier argument assumed the threaded parameter would be a `Tolerance` — the *value*; a zero-sized `Tol` witness carries evidence instead — the value never leaves the `OnceLock`. `Tol` has one inhabitant, so the signature promises precisely what it delivers. The objection that survives is churn: ~80 `Band::linear()` and 17 `Band::angular_at()` sites in `src`, their callers up to each operation entry, and ~400 test sites; the 355 functions that already take a `Band` are where threading stops. What it buys: the `no-ambient-env` rule gains an enforceable sibling; `mesh`'s ε inventory (pinned by #872, the subject of #884's open D9 question) becomes structural; `profile`'s double mechanism collapses into one.
