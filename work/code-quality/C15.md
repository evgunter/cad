## Finding

### S73. The budget gate's per-face join has no stable face identity to key on — Track K's `C15` (issue #746)

On `tools/tess-lint`, whose per-face join is by face ORDINAL because the budget CSV carries no other per-face name — the ground where the project's measure-don't-guess rule (`memories/tessellation-budget.md`) is implemented.

**The mis-join is closed.** The join runs under a pointwise precondition over the columns no rule compares — `chart`, the sizing block's presence, the trim box, the whole-patch divisions — a disagreeing ordinal is announced with the column and both readings, and ordinals below it are still compared.

**What survives is what the CSV cannot express.** Two faces of one scene agreeing on every one of those columns and swapping ordinals are indistinguishable, and they are not rare: on the committed baseline several such pairs stand, each two walls of one body with identical trim box and identical divisions.

**The count is not restated here, and that is the correction.** It is a reading of `docs/tess-budget-data/tess-budget-baseline.csv`, so it moves whenever that file is re-cut, and its three hand-transcribed copies each went stale where they could not follow. Every figure they carried was CORRECT when it was written: the baseline as committed at `632e60d76` — the commit that did the `S73` work — has exactly 1327 rows and exactly 22,545 corpus-wide pairs, which is what the copies said. The corpus-wide half went stale the same day, at the teapot re-baseline `ecdd9ec7c` (1315 rows, 22,423 pairs); the sized-row half — 8 pairs, 16 of 64 rows, 6 scenes — survived every cut until `a4eb03a` (CERT-10, 2026-09-01) made it 7, 14 and 5. Re-derive that over every committed BLOB of the path (`git rev-list --all --objects`, eleven distinct row counts from 1025 to 1330), not over `git log --follow` on one branch, which sees six of them and none of the matching one. Its **one home is `tools/tess-lint/tests/baseline_census.rs`**, which states the sweep's definition beside its result and re-derives every quantity — rows, sized rows, scenes, pairs, and the corpus-wide comparison — from the committed baseline on each `cargo test`. A re-cut that moves the census fails there and names what moved.

The restriction doing the work is to the SIZED rows, because an unsized swap costs the slack rule nothing; the same test measures what that restriction is worth. What the cut does NOT move: among the sized rows `chart` is `nurbs` on every one, the trim box is the whole unit square on every one, and the sizing block is "present" on every one because that is what SIZED means, so **six of the eight identity entries discriminate nothing there and the pair actually separating them is `nu`/`nv`** — six plus that pair being the whole list. The test derives that split column by column from the crate's own `identity_readings` rather than spot-checking members of it. Corpus-wide the other entries do discriminate, which is what makes the reroute case real.

Closing it needs a face identity in a column of the sweep's own: the producer-side row is `D201`.
