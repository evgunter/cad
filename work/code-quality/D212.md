## What

**The gate-side half of Track V's `G4`, filed here because it is Track K's
fence.** When `G4` retires `profile`'s `ArcCarrierScalar`, four things in
`scripts/gates/bounds-allowlist.sh` stop being true at once:

- the `family.rs` and `program.rs` file filters, which exist because those
  files write the alias's uses;
- **KNOWN GAP 3's text**, which names this trait as its worked example;
- the `BOUNDS_ALIAS_ROSTER` entry `D68` added, whose census reds the moment
  the declaration goes — by design, so the retirement cannot be silent;
- GAP 3's citation of `D68`'s answer, which is written against a live alias.

`G4`'s own header states the constraint: the retirement "lands as one piece
with K's `D68` answer or an allowlist row filed there (UV-R8)". `D68` has
landed and did **not** mint the entries — it established that the 50 use
sites cannot be checked by a name-shaped matcher and declined the
cry-wolf-then-allowlist close. So what `G4` still waits on is this row: the
gate edit that travels with its diff.

**This row is not takeable on its own.** It has no content until `G4` moves,
and `G4` may not move until Ev's ruling on `S87` (whether the sites are
legal) is in hand. It exists so that `G4` is parked on a live row rather
than on a closed one, and so that whoever takes `G4` finds the gate-side
work already scoped rather than discovering it at the red.
