**What `geom_brep::curve_pair::meet` still cannot answer.** FUSE built
the curve-pair reader (C5, Ev's ruling on PR 5027) and its stretch rule
(Ev, PR 5214), and its last unit merged in PR 5227. What it left are
typed refusals, never wrong answers: two coplanar conics that touch,
curves crossing in nearly parallel planes or below the polish basin's
floor, a first harmonic with both extremes in band, a line beside a
conic as thin as the band, a same-side touch pose that escalates at
ε 1e-12, and spline edges the census cannot meter (a whole-span speed
floor). Beside them: a polish meter that levers
by the line's own span, and a stretch reading with no row of its own.
