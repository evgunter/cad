**What the boolean hands back.** After the join and the zip, the merge
door folds coplanar and cosurface faces and the rebuild decides the
result's posture, its solids and shells, and what its contact records
say. Two rows here are confident WRONG answers rather than refusals:
`subtract-of-a-hollow-operand-files-the-island-under-one-solid` files a
hollow operand's cavity as a second Outer shell of the wrong solid, and
`boolean-rebuild-folds-an-in-band-second-order-into-conventional` builds
a conventional description tier 3 then refuses. The rest are a merge
that leaves faces unmerged or vertices redundant, a lineage walk that
drops a declared contact, and two operands that are one body.
