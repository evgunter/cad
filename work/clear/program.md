ruled units (SHELL-3, SHELL-4) were always about: moving the engine's
body-level half into `topo` behind interval so ONE engine serves two

face scope refuses `NotAFace` without saying what it found; and
`windows_of` asks HOW MANY before WHAT, refusing `Unresolved` for a
tied name before the kind question can be put — the where-before-what
shape the doors rule names.

identity tier and the clearance engine do not compose**: two pieces of
