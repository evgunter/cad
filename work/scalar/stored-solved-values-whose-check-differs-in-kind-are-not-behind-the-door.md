Found by `a-solved-value-the-body-stores-is-admitted-by-its-check`. The door it built, `geom_core::solved::decide_admit`, takes an `f64` candidate and a residual at `T`, admits on a reading in the band's zero, and is bounded `Certifies` (the `geom-core` README, DL5.2). Two storing sites moved onto it (`curve_face::composite::settle`, `curve_face::spline_face::settle`). The sites below store a solved value too, and each one's check differs in kind from "its point reads on within the band", so the README's rule does not settle whether the door holds them. They are listed `owed` to this row in `scripts/gates/from-computed-sites.tsv`.

- **`topo::replace_face::carrier_foot`** (an edge's re-anchored end). Newton's foot from `SplineFoot` (`f64`, `Projection3::t`), reached at `f64`, `Interval` and `Sym` (`SplineFoot::certified` is bounded `CertifiedBounds`; a dual holds `NoLane`). The check is in the caller (`replace_face`'s re-anchor walk): `gap_within_eps(carrier.eval(t_new).distance(point), tol, band)`, the margin `ε − gap` passing unless decided negative. It differs three ways: the tolerance is ε (what a corner is held to), not the band's zero; a gap at ε passes (Zero passes); and a failure is read further, as `ReanchorCollapse` or `ReanchorPastCarrierEnd` from where the clamp stopped, beside the analytic arms (`param_near`) that share the same check. What the door would need: a second form that admits within a tolerance the caller states, returning the gap on refusal so the caller's end reasoning runs, entered from the lane (`SplineFootOf<T>`, where `Certifies` holds) since the walk is generic over `Decide`.
- **`geom_brep::edge_nurbs::measured_foot`** (two calls): the chart foot a plane x NURBS pcurve image is built on. Checked by the image's limbs afterwards, never at the foot. Bounded `Real`.
- **`topo::pcurves::mint::computed_foot`**: a measured chart foot a candidate iso line (`Pcurve::IsoLine`) is built from; the candidate is judged whole at the probe (`probe_pick`), not the foot's point. Reached only where `T::fitted_lane()` is `Some`, so no dual at run time, but bounded `Real`.
- **`geom_brep::pair_section`'s `polished`** (the kept value, one of its three calls; the other two are Newton iterates, places, on `places-held-at-t-re-enter-through-from-computed`): a Newton foot kept by an `f64` window (`moved <= pad + band.escalate()`), a seed that `foot_along` then decides at `T` (`SITE_FOOT_ALONG`, Zero in band). Bounded `Decide`.
- **`geom::Curve3::polish`** (`on_carrier.rs`): the polish iterate re-centred at an enclosure scalar (`T::from_computed(mid_param(lo, hi))`); `Foot::on_carrier` then admits the foot by its gap in the band's zero (`OnCarrier::On`), which `curve_pair` and `topo::boolean::reduce` store (`split_other_at_point`). Same kind of check as the door, but a second admit, at `Decide`: a `Dual<Interval>` reaches the re-centring and takes a zero tangent there.

- **`topo::replace_face::corner_of_root`** (a corner's root along a spline edge, which the re-anchor stores as the edge's new end). The solver is `offset_derive::plane_spline_root`, written at `f64`; its root (`SplineRoot::At`) and a short root's end reach the body with no check at `T`. The check it has differs in kind: the corner's candidates are held to each other pairwise within ε (`corner_apart`), not to the surface within the band. It spells no `from_computed`: the lane's one constructor is `SectionLane::f64()`, so the root arrives as `T = f64`, and the gate cannot see it (named in its header as the blind spot of a lane concrete at one scalar). The type does not hold the `f64`-only fact: `SplineRoot<T>` is a public enum and `corner_of_root` is generic over `Decide`, so a lane built at another scalar would hand it a root it stores unchecked. What it would take: the root typed `f64` until something admits it (the door at a certifying lane scalar, or the pairwise agreement read as the admit), which is not local to `replace_face` because the re-anchor walk is generic.

Decide per site: the door's second form, a move of the check into the lane, or a closed form at `T`.

## What each check bounds and what its readers decide on (2026-11-25, from the crossing-parameter unit; BY-INSPECTION, nothing run)

The door now returns the admitted value with its bound (`geom_core::solved::Admitted`, `Reach`: across, the band's zero; along, the check's own bound), and a reader decides for every candidate (`decide_reaching`, `Reach::bands`). None of the sites below is behind it. Per site, the check's direction against its readers':

- **`replace_face::carrier_foot`** and **`corner_of_root`**: the check bounds the gap from the carrier's point to the corner, across (`gap_within_eps`, `corner_apart`). The stored end parameters are then read ALONG: `re_ask_on_pair` / `must_carry_over_edge` decide a class and a bend at `sample_param` fractions of `(t0, t1)`, which picks the stored description's kind (transverse, jet-determinate, under-determined, mixed); `set_edge_curve` decides the interval forward; on a clamped foot an `f64` comparison picks `ReanchorCollapse` against `ReanchorPastCarrierEnd`. Two feet the gap admits move every station. Whether two definite description kinds are reachable was not determined.
- **`split.rs` `tangent_piece`**: the same reading (`must_carry_over_edge` over `(t0, tm)`), on a child whose `tm` is a stored crossing parameter. The crossing's `Reach` stops at `split_at` today: the child's stations are not read with it.
- **`mint::computed_foot`**: nothing gates the foot. An `f64` branch (`rising = f1.x > f0.x`) picks the row direction, and the foot's coordinates become candidate iso lines that `probe_pick` admits whole by a 3D miss at three stations (across). Two feet flip `rising` only where `f0.x ≈ f1.x`.
- **`edge_nurbs::measured_foot`**: the foot is evaluated at to read a normal's sine, decided pass against `NotTransverse` per interior sample; the same feet are the stored chart image's nodes, checked later across by the incidence limbs. Near a tangency two feet could read the two definite verdicts; not determined.
- **`pair_section`'s `polished`**: kept by an `f64` window, then admitted by `foot_along`, which decides the ALONG distance to the orthogonal foot zero. Its readers in `boolean/join.rs` (`bool_join_foot_heading`, `_ahead`, `_travel`) decide along too, so here the check bounds the direction its readers decide on; its reach is that row's zero.
- **`Curve3::polish`**: a step is kept where the gap across is smaller, and `Foot::on_carrier` admits on that gap, across; `within_span` then decides the place in or past the span, along.
- `sector_face.rs` `spline_normal` and `pcurves/tier3.rs` `decide_at` read an edge's stored END parameters (a chart point, a joint's gap against period marks), not a crossing's; where an end is a re-anchored foot they are readers of the first bullet.
