The rule is `crates/geom-core/README.md` DL5.2.

A bound (DL5.2's first row) decides at `f64` or is an `f64` allowance of the margin; it never becomes a `T`. These do, and no open bound row names them (the others are on `certificate-bound-fields-hold-limb-bounds-as-t`, `projected-hull-bounds-are-operands-of-t-arithmetic`, `lever-arms-read-as-f64-bounds-still-enter-lever-t`, `volume-bracket-ends-are-t-fields-built-from-f64-bounds` and `a-bound-a-sym-identity-is-proved-over-has-no-allowance-spelling`).

- `geom_core::predicate` `Allowance::apply`: the allowance's `f64` bound formed in `T` (`let lift = T::from_computed`) for the reading at `Sym` and the `MarginTerms` decision. It is the allowance mechanism `a-bound-to-decide-against-never-becomes-a-t` built; whether the allowance is decided without forming the bound in `T`, or this is a permanent caller with a class of its own, is the question.
- `geom_brep::pair_section::ruling` `form_ceiling`: a cell's half width as a `T` lever of the ceiling.
- `topo::link` `crossing`: the band's zero as a `T` operand (`rd.band.zero()`).

Cost: E for the last two; `Allowance::apply` is a design question first.
