# SCALAR — the plan

The scalar doors and the readers over them: the certified-door family,
margins and their recourse, unit directions, and the box driver's
readings.

The slate is the board's (`python3 scripts/work.py status --program
scalar`); this page says what order it is taken in and why.

## The sitting's rows (P1)

Two design forks, each weighed by designers before it goes to Ev
(`memories/orchestration-model.md`):

- `dl5-has-no-arm-for-a-selection-whose-result-is-the-payload` — what
  the delegation rule is. Its two statements disagree:
  `docs/DUAL-DESIGN.md` DL5 has no locally-constant clause, and
  `crates/geom-core/src/real.rs`'s has one, which the census's
  `Why::Selection` calls load-bearing and at least one roster row says
  does not carry it.
- `patherror-display-renders-float-noise` — what a person sees when a
  sentence quotes a number, and what owns saying it. Three rules
  exist (`profile::path::num`, `viewer::readout::number`,
  `geom_core::Readable`) and 150 to 200 `Display` arms use none. The
  per-crate sweep is mechanical once the rule is decided, and
  `user-facing-number-format-is-not-one-across-payloads-endings-and-escalations`
  (P4) is the same question's tail.

Two rows are parked on ONEHOME's
`carriers-hold-their-frame-as-unitvec3-and-rigidmotion-and-check-1s-frame-half-goes`:
`measure-carrier-holds-a-unit-chart-normal-by-prose` and
`tangent-perp-takes-an-unchecked-unit-direction`. Each is one signature
once a carrier holds its direction as a `UnitVec3`.

## Review posture

The review tiers of `memories/orchestration-model.md`, named with the
reason at dispatch; implementers and reviewers are drawn under
`docs/MODEL-COMPARE-PROTOCOL.md`. A spec requires a decide where it
would otherwise assert a premise, and a narrower bound is not evidence
a width fix is right: containment, tested against exact arithmetic, is.

**Re-measure a row before dispatching it.** Most of this slate was
filed in September and October against a tree that has since moved,
and of the six P0 and P1 rows this sitting opened on, two were already
fixed and two were waiting on a trigger no header named.
