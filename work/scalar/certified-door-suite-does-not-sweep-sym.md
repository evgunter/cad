## The finding

Raised by ENCL's H11 sweep. `geom_core::CertifiedEnclosure`'s trait
docs (`crates/geom-core/src/real.rs`) rest the postcondition *"a
`Some` never carries a NaN end"* on an implementor list, and
`crates/geom-core/tests/certified_door.rs` is its executable form
("written once, generically ... swept per implementor"). The list
omitted `Sym<T>` (`crates/geom-core/src/sym.rs`,
`impl<T: CertifiedEnclosure> CertifiedEnclosure for Sym<T>`), which
H11 added as a bullet. The suite still omits it: its corpora are
`f64`, `Interval`, and `Probe` under `--features probe`.

`Sym` delegates to its numeric channel today, so it honours the
postcondition by construction. What a row would catch is the day that
impl stops delegating (a symbolic refusal of its own, or a certified
answer the DAG computes): the per-member agreement assertion — `Sym<T>`
refuses exactly where `T` does — is the property to pin, over a corpus
generated by `Sym` arithmetic rather than by wrapping `T` values.
