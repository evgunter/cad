## What

**A TOLERANCED quantity crosses a door in two different units, and the receipt that carries it says which in prose only.** `Exhaustiveness::floor` is `pub` on a `pub` struct and holds meters from `account_r3`, chart units from `account_chart_plane` — the latter divided by the chart speed at `ssi.rs`'s call. `D282` corrected the doc to name both lanes; **this row is the design question the doc fix leaves standing**, and it turns on the floor being toleranced rather than a domain budget: `SsiDomain::floor` is `SSI_FLOOR · band.zero() · floor_scale`, and `seed_floor`'s own doc draws the distinction — *"the ACCOUNTING floor stays tied to ε because that one is a proof obligation"*, while the seeding floor is deliberately tied to the extent instead. So this is not a stopping budget that may live in whatever space the search runs in; it is the proof obligation's own threshold. **The conversion is sound in DIRECTION and lossy in tightness**: `speed` is an upper bound on the chart derivative, so refusing at `floor_m / speed` refuses only once the cell's meters extent is certainly under the floor — conservative where the bound is loose, never the other way. **What is unguarded is the reporting boundary**, and three things say so: every other field of the struct is a unit-free count, so `floor` is the sole dimensional one; the hedge has already replicated to `SsiError::ExhaustivenessInconclusive::cell_width` (*"in meters (or chart units)"*); and `geom-brep/tests/m5_pr7_ssi.rs` carries a helper spelled `assert_floor_is_the_meters_floor_over_the_chart_speed`, i.e. consumer-side knowledge of which door built the value, encoded in a name because no type carries it. **#762 is the demonstration that the conversion has teeth**: a non-finite speed drove the chart floor to exactly `0`, and the caller got `CellBudget` rather than the true diagnosis. The repo has ratified vocabulary for this shape — S22's zero-sized `Tol` witness, and `C13` is the same complaint one crate over (*"ε has no type of its own"*). **The question for Ev: does the ε-typing conversation reach this receipt** — a `Floor` newtype or a lane tag on `Exhaustiveness` — or is a `pub` field documented as two units acceptable for a value that no production caller currently reads back out? Note the answer sets the precedent for `cell_width` too. **Raised by the D282 lane's own reviewer, 2026-08-28; not a lane's to settle.**

## Put to Ev (2026-09-12, SCALAR's first `[ev]` sitting)

**What the tree says.** `Exhaustiveness` (`ssi/exhaust.rs`) has one
writer, `sweep`, generic over the cell type — `Box3` is the ℝ³ lane in
meters, `UvRect` the chart lane in chart units — and `floor` is its
only dimensional field. The chart lane's floor is `domain.floor(band) /
speed`, where `speed` is a sup bound on the chart derivative computed in
`plane_nurbs_ssi` and stored nowhere. Nothing in production reads
`floor` or `cell_width` except `Display`, which prints no unit word; the
one test that reads them re-derives the lane and the speed by hand
through a pinned literal (`WALL_CHART_SPEED`) because the public API
cannot say either. #762's guard is a class test (non-finite or ≤ 0);
issue 1238 (CURVED) records that a finite huge speed still lands the
floor at ~1e-152 and the caller still gets `CellBudget`. The in-tree
receipt that already solves this shape is `offset_meters::PatchRegularity`:
a raw `floor` beside `speed_u`/`speed_v`, meters derived by a method.
Evidence: `work/scalar/log.md`, the D283 entry.

**Recommendation: no dimensional newtype; the receipt carries its
lane and, on the chart lane, the speed that translated the floor.**
`Exhaustiveness` and `ExhaustivenessInconclusive` gain a lane tag
(`R3`, or `Chart { speed }`), keep `floor` and `cell_width` in the
lane's own units (the units the `cell.width() <= floor` decision was
made in), and derive the meters reading by a method (`floor · speed` on
the chart lane); `Display` names the lane. That is the ε-typing answer
at this receipt: **provenance, not dimension** — the same idiom as
`SsiLimb` naming which limb refused and `Margin` carrying its dimension
in the constructor door rather than in the type. It makes the #762 /
1238 misdiagnosis diagnosable (the receipt shows a floor and the speed
that produced it), retires the test's pinned speed, and settles
`cell_width` in the same stroke because the error carries the same tag.

**Rejected:** a `Floor` newtype with `meters()`/`chart(speed)`
constructors — a dimensional type inside the kernel, against D6's "raw
`T` in canonical units" and against the 2026-09-04 ruling that ε has no
type of its own (`work/exch/epsilon-has-no-type-of-its-own.md`); and it
would exist only to be unwrapped, since no production caller reads the
value. Also rejected: a pub field documented as two units — the hedge
has already replicated once (`cell_width`), and a consumer that has to
encode which door built a value in a helper's NAME is the row's own
evidence that a type is owed.

**Cost:** ~10–12 sites in `geom-brep/src/ssi*` (TRIM's ground, announced),
one small unit. **Question:** (a) ratify lane-tagged receipt, meters by
method, no dimensional newtype; (b) it runs here with TRIM announced,
unless you want TRIM to take it.
