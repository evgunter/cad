INTENT stage 6, PR B. Spec: `docs/INTENT-STAGE6-SPEC.md` §3.

D10 `[coincidence]`, "Constructing a coincidence": "Coaxiality is one `Axis` read twice, directly or as projections of one construction". Stage 3 lets a document write that (`Axis`–`Axis` mates onto one `Axis` variable; a revolve's `axis` output), but the door reads none of it: rung one's `Origin` is the cell itself (`coincide.rs` `one_construction`), `stated_by_a_mate` reads only `mate::planes_equated`, and rung two's `carrier_verdict` answers only one-carrier relations. So every `Coaxial` row, and the sphere-centre `OnCarrier` rows at `CoaxialSphere` and `SphereOnTorusAxis`, is proven by rung three or not at all.

`design: true` for **FORK-S6-1** (spec §7): whether the door reads an axis's provenance (rung one, with a generated per-construction cache), answers coaxiality at rung two from the symbolic carriers, or reads mates only; and whether a press-fit seat (one axis, one radius) is structural.
