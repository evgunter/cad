Raised by Ev in chat (2026-10-15) while reviewing FORK-MOVE (PR 5189): settling on slide + spin as the only way to relate two poses may be "another instance of 'family of ways to represent the same thing'", a principle that "comes up frequently as a solution to keeping exact authored values while presenting a clean unified api", and that is written nowhere. A search of `docs/`, `memories/`, the crate READMEs and PR comments found it stated nowhere; the closest instance is #3218, where Ev proposed a three-point arc form ("a whole infinite family of ways to represent the same circle-with-vertex") and `circle` came to read back as `circle` while lowering as `circle_split(c, r, 1, 0)`. Several places say the opposite in passing ("a second spelling D10 forbids", `docs/INTENT-STAGE3-SPEC.md` §Q, Q6), so the paragraph also says what a forbidden second spelling is. The `[ev]` PR adds the paragraph to D10.
