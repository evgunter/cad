The measurement half of
`sense-inversion-is-invisible-to-tier-3-on-arc-capped-lofts`, which is
cost `H` because nobody yet knows what it is. Its own body ends in
three questions and `memories/refusal-text-is-not-cause.md` makes
answering them a checkpoint rather than a preamble: the payload and
the raising site are the instrument, and a refusal's text is not
evidence of its cause.

So this unit MEASURES and does not fix. It answers the three, pins
each answer as a row that goes red if the answer changes, and hands
the orchestrator what the remaining change is — which may be a check,
may be a door, and may be that the whole thing is a test-door artefact
worth one sentence and a closed row.

Spec: `docs/ATREST-2-SPEC.md`. Outside protocol v7 (opus/opus): a
measurement produces evidence, not a design, and the design call it
feeds is the orchestrator's. Review is the style lane plus one
correctness claim — that the measurements say what the report says
they say.
