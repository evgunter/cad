**The join, between the section and the zip.** Once intersection has
found the section curves, the join matches their chords into arcs,
pairs every loose end, and decides which loop of each completed section
polygon is the IN copy. Every row here is a refusal the join raises on
geometry the kernel should build — four of them on normal verbs over
normal shapes — and most of them in the join's own kernel-bug words
(`JoinDesync`, `UnpairedLooseEnds`, `SectionLoopMixed`).

`verbs-1031b-assigner-checker-divergence` is the winding predicate
spelled at three sites that no longer cover the same carriers, and
settling which reading is right is the likeliest explanation of
`join-desync-on-the-star-fixture`.
