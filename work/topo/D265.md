## What

**Is `euler.rs`'s tier-1-corruption row complete?** `D38` widened `merge_coplanar_faces`' arena-fault escape from two variants to **nine**, taking membership from the seven `EulerOpError` variants whose own docs say *tier-1-invalid input* — and the class now lives on the enum as an **exhaustive match**, so a new variant does not compile until someone places it. One name was **declined on evidence**: `NotSameLoop` is not in that table, though at this door `merge_group` verifies `hp.loop == hm.loop` itself before calling `kemr`, so a `NotSameLoop` from there does contradict a fact the call established. **The site-level argument sweeps in `FaceHasRings`, `SameLoop` and others** — i.e. it is the claim that at this door nearly every `EulerOpError` is unreachable except by corruption, which needs the table restructured rather than one row added. Measured harmless today: zero arena faults in 3,033 door decisions. **But if the table is incomplete the door's published sentence is false for at least one variant**, and the sentence is what callers read.

(`D38` is a landed row cited as provenance only.)

## Closed

Merged as **PR 2013**. The door's published sentence was false because
it asked the ENUM a question the enum does not answer.
`EulerOpError::reports_tier1_corruption` says *this variant means the
body was already torn* — a property of the variant. The door's sentence
is about THIS door, where `merge_group` verifies facts before its
operator calls. The two questions now have two homes and the door asks
both.

**What landed** (`crates/topo/src/merge_faces.rs`; `euler.rs`'s only
diff is a shared test constructor):

- **The door's own classification.** `OpPlacement`, an exhaustive match
  over all 27 `EulerOpError` variants with two arms —
  `TheEnumsVerdict`, which delegates and keeps no copy of the operator
  layer's list, and `Contradicts(EstablishedFact)`, this door's half.
  A new variant does not compile until it is placed here as well as on
  the enum's line. `reports_tier1_corruption` and its body are
  untouched.
- **`EstablishedFact`**, six variants naming the facts the surgery
  owns: `RingIsNotItsFacesOuter`, `RingAndSurvivorShareAShell`,
  `AbsorbedFaceIsRingFree`, `DyingHalvesAreInDifferentLoops`,
  `StrutHalvesHaveDistinctEnds`, `DuplicateHalvesShareALoop`. The
  `debug_assert!` re-checks, the `OpPlacement` arms and the coverage
  row all name the same value.
- **Re-checks at the call.** A fact the door established earlier is a
  fact only until the door's own next mutation, so each fact is
  re-derived from the arena in a `debug_assert!` immediately before its
  operator call, with no production code between. The test-only tear
  points (`TearPoint`, armed through the RAII `ArmedTear`) sit after
  the re-check and before the call, which is exactly the window the
  re-check cannot cover and the only way to reach these arms at all —
  the entry gate refuses a torn INPUT before a group is staged.
- **The (R) correction.** The dispatched head claimed the (R) column
  was empty. It is not: `kef`'s `SameFace` is reachable on a
  tier-1-valid body, because the absorption's own `ring_move` drain
  re-homes the dying loop onto the survivor, so a nested group — a
  membrane face covering a ring of its neighbour — legitimately leaves
  `kef` reading one face on both sides. That is an inventory refusal
  and the regime records it; escaping on it turned a legal skip into a
  refused call. Found by the R2 reviewer, executed, and adopted with
  its fixture and rows.
- **Executed coverage.** Five of the six contradicted facts run
  end-to-end through the public door under BOTH regimes, on
  plane-recipe fixtures whose regime is asserted first (no fixture
  rides `D263`'s placeholder-reads-as-curved):
  `RingIsOuter`, `CrossShell`, `SameLoop`, `FaceHasRings`,
  `NotSameLoop`. `SelfLoopEdge` is argued, not executed, and the code
  says so and why: `kev` runs only on a straight-seam strut, and no
  fixture in the corpus both reaches that repair and admits a plane
  recipe.
- Nine raising sites in `merge_group`, not two, including
  `loop_winding` through `merged_outline_ring`; the rule stated once,
  in the public door's rustdoc, with five sites pointing at it; two
  stale testability claims rewritten.
