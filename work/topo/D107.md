### S161. Two of `review_d18`'s seven hammered operators never reach the arms under attack

Found by lane F-d while replacing S76's floor, and by F4's adversarial review one step further.

`crates/topo/src/review_d18.rs` says its sweep *"drives every operator that reaches `link_half_edges` over every key"*, at the module doc and again on `hammer`. The per-operator exposure the S76 fix added measures that sentence for the first time, and it is **true of the calls and false of the arms**:

- **`kemr` enters a mutation phase in NEITHER row** — 0 of 96 calls on the spent-graft destination, 0 of ~1900 across the torn sweep. Its plan phase refuses on every input either row produces, so every `link_half_edges` site below it is attacked by nothing here.

**Two different defects wearing one sentence.** `mfkrh_plug` is a *classification* error — the file counts an operator that is not in the class its prose names, and the S76 fix therefore excludes it from the floor (`LINK_OPS`) while still driving and printing it. `kemr` is a *coverage* gap: it is in the class, and nothing this file does gets it past its plan phase. Only the first is closed by #825.

**What D107 owes.** Either a fixture on which `kemr`'s plan phase succeeds — its preconditions are a ring-merge shape neither `ops_cube()` nor a torn cube presents — or the written finding that `kemr`'s row-4 arms have no input witness from this door, which is a claim of the same kind the D2 addendum's row 1 already makes elsewhere and would be worth as much. What is not admissible is the current state, where the prose asserts the coverage and the exposure line disproves it on every run.

The exposure prints `kemr: 0` before the floors, so this is derivable from any `--nocapture` run of the row rather than only by reading `euler_ring.rs`. ## Closed 2026-09-14

**The first half of the alternative was taken**: `kemr` now has an input
witness from this door, so the finding is answered by coverage rather
than by a written concession.

**The fixture.** `crates/topo/src/fixtures.rs`'s `ops_ring_bridge` —
`ops_holed_box` plus one `mekr_chord` joining the top face's hole rim
back into that face's outer loop. `kemr` wants the two halves of ONE
edge lying in ONE loop; every edge of `ops_cube`, `ops_holed_box` and
`ops_genus2` borders two distinct faces, so its halves sit in two loops
and the plan phase refuses at `NotSameLoop` on every pair those bodies
present. **That is structural, not a sampling shortfall** — which is why
~1900 `kemr` calls over torn cubes found nothing — and
`kemr_reaches_a_mutation_phase_on_the_ring_bridge_and_on_no_cube_edge`
asserts both halves of it. The bridge edge's halves share the merged
loop, both sides of the split are non-empty, so both of `kemr`'s
`link_half_edges` splices run rather than the one a strut kill reaches.

**The enumeration.** The fixture alone was not enough: the hammer drove
`kemr` at arbitrary key pairs, which it refuses at `NotSameEdge` or
`NotSameLoop` before reading anything else. The mate pair — an edge's
own two halves, both argument orders — is the enumeration that can
reach the mutation phase, and it was ADDED beside the arbitrary pairs,
not substituted for them, so the refusal paths keep their attack. The
enumeration and the fixtures are each independently load-bearing, and
the controls say so at the level each is floored: point the mate-pair
calls at a half's own key instead of its mate and `kemr` goes to 0 with
both fixtures still swept; drop either fixture and the arm only it
reaches goes to 0. All three controls run.

Sampling row, at the floor of the effort dial: `kemr` 0 → ~90, calls

table on the deterministic row, and on the sampling one a floor under
EACH of `LINK_OPS` — `require_each(&LINK_OPS, 1)` — replacing the

let `kemr` sit at 0 for the row's whole life. The call count is exact
there too (`TORN_SWEEP_CALLS_PER_ROUND`, times the dial and the tear
kinds), since a tear never adds or removes an entity.

**Both arms of `kemr`'s mutation phase, and each body by name.** `kemr`
splices twice when both sides of the split are non-empty and once when
the ring side is empty; the two are counted apart and floored apart, so
the fixture family carries both — `ops_ring_bridge` for the first and
`ops_strut_cube` (the cube with a pendant strut, the state
`ops_holed_box` passes through at its hole anchor) for the second.
`FIXTURES` is floored per body the way the tear kinds are, so no body
can leave the sweep under the cover of the others.

**The `mfkrh_plug` classification.** Already excluded from `LINK_OPS` by
the S76 fix; what was still false was the module doc's headline, which
claimed the sweep drives *every* operator that reaches
`link_half_edges`. It drives one that does not (`mfkrh_plug` — it calls
`link_half_edges` nowhere, as this row's `## Finding` says) and it drove
none of `mekr`, which reaches the arms at twelve splices. The sentence
is narrowed to the driven set and both facts are stated where the lists
are defined.
