## What

**`merge_faces`' four predicate helpers answer on an unresolved lookup instead of announcing — eleven discard arms and one value substitution.** After `D38`/`D88` the door's call chain announces every failed lookup and the helpers it consults do not. `planes_declared_equal` returns `Ok(None)` on four lookup arms, silently dropping a mergeable adjacency; `redundant_subdivision_vertex` returns `Ok(false)` on **seven**, each of which routes the seam repair from `kev` to `kemr` and therefore **changes the group's Euler delta**; `merged_outline_ring`'s `_ => Ok(None)` conflates *not a plane* with *surface key does not resolve* and means *"roles already correct"*; `loop_winding`'s `all_lines` chain conflates a torn half-edge, edge or curve with a curved carrier. The substitution is `edge_chord_len(edge).unwrap_or_else(T::one)`, feeding a unitless `1` as a **length** lever arm into `oriented_plane_eq`'s `decide` site — so a failed lookup does not merely answer the question, it **re-scales the margin**. The closing lane's own disclosure named three of these; the file holds twelve.

(`D38` and `D88` are landed rows cited as provenance only.)

## What holds now (PR 3532)

No failed lookup in the three helpers is answered as a value. Each one
left refuses naming what it could not read, and the rest are gone:

- `planes_declared_equal` takes the shared edge's two halves as the
  adjacency scan resolved them (`HalfEdgeFacts`) and reads each face's
  record and surface from the kind census, which resolved them in the
  same call (row 0: its surface lookups are gone). A face the census
  did not meet live refuses `MergeCoplanarError::Op` naming it
  (`StaleKey`); "not a plane" is still `Ok(false)`.
- `edge_chord_len` reads the two start vertices the scan resolved
  (row 0: the edge and half-edge lookups are gone) and returns the
  vertex or point that does not resolve. The `unwrap_or_else(T::one)`
  substitution is gone: no failed lookup levers the declared rung.
- `merged_outline_ring` announces its surface lookup and keeps "not a
  plane" as `Ok(None)`. `loop_winding`'s torn walk names what it could
  not read rather than the loop it started from: the half-edge a stale
  link names, or the edge, curve, vertex or point (`StaleKey`,
  `StaleGeometry`); a half-edge its own edge does not claim
  (`UnclaimedHalfEdge`, through `Edge::claim`, the one reading of the
  pairing that `Body::mate`, `outermost_survivor` and `edge_mate` also
  use); and, for a walk whose links all resolve but never return,
  `LoopCycleBroken` naming the loop, since no key is at fault there.
