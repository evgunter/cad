## Finding

### S94. Two hand-maintained `VARIANTS` ladders, with the disclosure copied verbatim

`crates/topo/src/euler.rs:3220-3251` and `crates/topo/src/validate.rs:4079-4140` both carry a `const VARIANTS: usize` and a wildcard-free `variant_index` match restating the enum's declaration order by hand, and both carry the same four-sentence *"what it does NOT enforce … when you add an arm, its index is the new `VARIANTS - 1`"* paragraph word for word — a hand-kept mirror of a compiler-known fact, self-declared as such at both copy sites. The disclosure names `strum`'s `EnumCount` as the way out and then declines it, which owes a schedule and has none. Worth checking whether `MassPropsError`, `PcurveMintError` and `BooleanError` grew the same ladder or lack it.

## Closed (rides with S330)

Both ladders are gone. `ValidationError` and `EulerOpError` each carry
a `strum::EnumDiscriminants` companion derived under `cfg(test)` —
`ValidationErrorKind`, `EulerOpErrorKind` — and the two Display-coverage
rows take the index from `Kind::from(err) as usize` and the array length
from `Kind::COUNT`. Neither the count nor the declaration order is
written down a second time, and a variant added without a sample now
fails the row by NAME rather than by index. The pairing of `iter()`
with the discriminant index is sound only because the two derives agree
on order, so each row asserts that explicitly rather than assuming it
(added at review). Both copies of the
four-sentence "what it does NOT enforce" disclosure are deleted; the
route it named and declined is the one taken. `strum` is a
dev-dependency of `topo` only, and the derives are `cfg_attr(test)`, so
no production build graph carries it.

The three sibling enums the finding asked about:

- `BooleanError` (`crates/topo/src/boolean/mod.rs:555`) grew a
  **hand-written** companion instead — `BooleanErrorKind` at `:1161`,
  one fieldless variant per error variant, each documented, plus an
  exhaustive visit that names a phantom at compile time. It is the same
  fact restated by hand that `EnumDiscriminants` derives, and it is a
  larger ladder than either of this row's. `topo/src/boolean/*` is
  S-BOOL's ground (`work/topo/program.md` `keep_out`), so it is
  reported and not touched.
- `MassPropsError` (`crates/topo/src/props.rs:103`) and
  `PcurveMintError` (`crates/topo/src/pcurves.rs:168`) **lack** it: no
  count, no index ladder, and no Display-coverage row at all. Nothing
  to convert; whether they want the row is their owners' call
  (`props.rs` is code-quality Track M's, `pcurves.rs` is TRIM's).

**Verdict:** fixed, in the two files the finding named.
