## Closed (PR #1923, 2026-09-05)

Tier-3 check 1 reads a `Nurbs` face surface's **state** and answers each
one. `geom::NetState` (`crates/geom/src/surfaces/nurbs.rs`) names the
three — `Placeholder`, `Described`, `Poisoned` — and its docs are the
single home of the distinction; `NurbsSurface::net_state` is the door,
and check 1 matches on it exhaustively, so no call site carries a guard
order and no second copy of the table exists. `Poisoned` reports the new
`ValidationError::PoisonedSurfaceDescription`, naming the face;
`Placeholder` keeps `UncertifiableSurface`; `Described` passes. Every
remaining `Surface` variant and `None` is a named arm with a true
reason: **no wildcard survives in the match.**

Measured before the change and recorded in the PR body: a described net
poisoned in one channel drew NO check-1 verdict, and the refusal it did
draw — check 2's two `DescriptionNotAdjacent` findings — was identical,
key for key, to the one an honest finite net draws. Nothing but check 1
separated the states.

Rows in `crates/topo/src/tier3_tests.rs`: the red-first row (verified
red against the arm-less build), the finite control, and
`the_net_state_ladder_decides_check_1s_verdict`, adopted from the R2
review — eight rungs whose near misses are what pin the boundaries (a
hand-built all-poison net is the placeholder; poison in every channel of
ONE point is not; `+∞` is not `f64` poison). The interval twin is in
`crates/topo/tests/interval_body.rs`.

`S94` rode along and is closed on its own file.
