## Brief (TOPO, 2026-09-14) — block TOPO-B3 slot 2, dual at review

**The answer to give.** `mev`'s fan site and `kev`'s fan merge re-base
a run of half-edges onto a different vertex; today each carries a
paragraph telling the caller to re-describe the moved run's carriers
"whenever the two points differ", and nothing enforces it — an edge's
stored curve is routinely stale against its own endpoints, `seqgen`
filters around it, and `split_edge` refuses on it. After this unit no
public operator leaves a re-based edge with a carrier certified
against a point that is no longer its endpoint: the operator either
re-certifies every re-based edge's carrier against the new endpoint in
its plan phase (through `EdgeCurve::recertify`, the door `split_edge`
already certifies through — `T: Decide`, no bound moved) and REFUSES
typed, body untouched, where a carrier does not pass through the new
vertex; or, where the two points coincide within band, carries the
certificate as it is. Phase 1 decides whether the re-certification is
exact or a re-fit per carrier kind and says why; a re-description the
operator cannot make honestly is a refusal, never a stale carrier.

**Rows.** Red-first: `mev` at a fan site with a moved run whose
carriers do not pass through the new vertex — on the merge base `Ok`
with stale carriers (assert `split_edge` then refuses on one of them,
the `seqgen` symptom); at the head a typed refusal naming the edge.
`kev`'s fan merge the same way. Control: the coincident-point case
carries every certificate byte for byte. `seqgen.rs`'s
re-certification filter ("an edge's stored curve is routinely stale")
retires with the reason it existed, and its proptest/roundtrip rows
stay green.

**Receipt.** Every site that re-bases a half-edge run (`mev` fan,
`kev` fan merge, `kemr`?, `split_edge`'s children, the merge door's
absorption) with whether it re-certifies, carries or refuses; the two
prose obligations deleted with the mechanism that replaces them. Both
files are TOPO's.
