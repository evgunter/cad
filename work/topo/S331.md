## What

**`validate_pcurves` answers a clean bill on a body whose mint just failed (filed by CERT-N2; found by the dual review, executed).** `topo/src/pcurves.rs:1602` returns an empty error list for a body where `mint_pcurves` refused a face and `clear_face_caches` removed its caches — absence of a cache is not a claim (`validate_pcurves`' own rule), so zero errors is the correct *letter* and a vacuous green through a **public** door: a caller that validates after a failed mint is told the pcurves are fine.
