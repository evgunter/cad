## Brief (TOPO, 2026-09-05)

Dispatched as the liveness sub-lane's unit, with `D260` riding
(`rides_with: D50`). Branch `topo/d50-live-guard`. Runs outside the A/B
experiment (single style review; no row). Survey re-derived on main
after `D261` landed (`d9b7b26d`):

**What the compiler already guards, and what it does not.**
`crates/topo/src/live.rs:49` declares `pub(crate) struct Live(HalfEdgeKey)`
with a PRIVATE field, so the tuple constructor and `Live::new`
(`:56`, private) are unnameable outside the module — `grep -rn 'Live('
crates/topo/src` finds no construction outside `live.rs`, and one could
not compile. What nothing checks is the module header's actual claim
(`:6-8`, `:26-30`): **every door in `live.rs` that hands out a `Live`
performs the lookup first.** That is a fact about four function bodies
(`of` `:62`, `require_live` `:102`, `resolve_half_edge_live` `:116`,
`loop_cycle_live` `:133`) and about the declaration staying private,
and a `compile_fail` doctest cannot name a `pub(crate)` type. A
source-level guard can, and D261 just landed the tools: the shared
lexer `test_utils::source::code_only`, `rust_sources`, `crate_dir`, and
the item carve `item_body`/`ItemBody` (`crates/test-utils/src/source.rs:412-451`).

**The guard (one `#[cfg(test)]` row in `live.rs`'s own test module, or
beside `source_walk.rs`'s door walk if it shares that file's helpers —
your call, say why):** over the `code_only` view of `live.rs`,
(1) the `struct Live(` declaration carries no visibility on its field
and `fn new` carries none; (2) every item whose head mentions `Live`
or `Self` in a returning position (`-> Live`, `-> Option<Self>`,
`-> Result<Live, …>`, `-> Option<Vec<Live>>`) has, inside its
`item_body` carve, a lookup call BEFORE any `Live::new(` / `Self(` —
name the lookup vocabulary explicitly (`get_half_edge`, `require_key`,
`.get(`, `loop_cycle`) and make an unknown spelling a loud failure,
not a pass; (3) `Live::new(` and `Self(` construction sites in the file
number exactly the ones the header lists, so a fifth door arrives as a
red row rather than silently. Over the `code_only` view of every other
`crates/topo/src` file: no `Live(`/`Live::new(` at all (the compiler's
fact, restated so the privacy claim is executable if someone ever
widens `new`). **Plant the mutants and record them in the PR body**: a
door that returns `Some(Live::new(he))` without looking up; `fn new`
made `pub(crate)`; a construction moved above its lookup. Each must red
with a message naming the door.
