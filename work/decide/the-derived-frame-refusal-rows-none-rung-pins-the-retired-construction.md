## What was measured (SYM-10 Phase 1, 2026-09-21)

`editor-core/tests/m10_derived_frame_interval::m10_the_derived_frames_refusal_is_not_a_freeze`
is one of the three rows red on `main` merged with `props/sign-hull`.
Its first assertion — every rule off (`SymRules::none`), the widened
box at `5e-2`, `Pinned` — is that the ONE refusal is
`carrier_endpoint_start` and the plain form freezes. That was true of
Duff's construction: the gate's residual froze (12) and refused. On the
sign-hull construction, rendered with the per-predicate split
(`sym10_phase1_the_derived_frame_rows_refusal_rendered`):

| rung | `carrier_endpoint_start` | `newell_plane_residual` | frozen | first refusal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | `[32, 0, 0, 0]` — 32 THEOREMS | `[28, 0, 0, 1]` | 24 | the boss's SIDE plane, `margin is invalid` |
| A0 replacing | `[24, 0, 0, 1]` | `[24, 0, 0, 0]` | 10 | the gate, `[0, 0.32]` |

Under `none` the gate is a plain theorem — the opaque `Select` atoms of
an axis-aligned frame cancel as `X − X` — so the document goes on to
the boss's side planes, where the value channel's `newell_plane_residual`
is `Invalid` (`work/frame/a-widened-derived-placement-normalises-a-straddling-newell-sum`'s
class, the cap's site under Duff). `Decide for Sym<T>` never asks the
tier about an `Invalid` margin (`domain_violation → symbolic = None`),
and at `none` no rule runs by the tier's own contract. **So no fold at
any dial can make this rung green**: the assertion asks the `none`
tier to REFUSE a residual it now proves, and the only plants that
"restored" it (`123zp`, folds in the plain walk) did so by opening the
atoms until the gate's form froze (8) and the theorem was lost — the
wrong direction.

The A0 rung is a different matter and is
`a0-leaves-max-and-min-of-constants-opaque`'s: with `max`/`min` of two
constants folded under A0 the rung reads frozen 0, one refusal, the
newell `Invalid` — the row's second assertion verbatim.

## What this row asks

The `none` rung is a golden of the plain form's first refusal on a
construction that no longer exists. Ev's #2728 rejected re-aiming
SYM-5's acceptance row; this row is not that row — it pins the
`none`/A0 DIAGNOSTIC of the height document, and its `none` half
cannot be met by the tier at all. Whether it is re-aimed to what the
`none` tier now does (the gate a theorem, the boss's side plane the
value channel's refusal) is put to Ev with SYM-10's Phase 1 tables.
