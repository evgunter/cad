Filed by BOXFINISH (2026-11-15) from the design fork on
`work/boxfinish/a-verb-stores-a-radius-as-a-magnitude-and-the-symbolic-tier-cannot-use-the-sign-its-gate-decided.md`.
**Not to be built as it stands**: both designers weighed it and
recommend against it today; it is here so the next reader that needs
it finds the argument.

**The idea.** When the numeric channel decides a margin definitely
positive or negative inside a `Sym` session, the session records that
the margin's form has that sign, and the tier's `abs` fold, `√(f²)`
and `atan2(0, f)` simplifications read the record beside their
syntactic "manifestly positive" test. A definite verdict at
`Sym<Interval>` is a proof about every point of the leaf's box, the
same class of premise as the quotient rule's "wherever the expression
has a value", so the record is sound and is no registration.

**Why not now.**
- No measured reader needs it: the arc's readers each hold something
  sign-free to read (the stored radius, the recorded direction, the
  rationalized gap).
- What it proves depends on which decisions were taken before the
  question is asked, so eager and deferring sessions can differ in
  reach (never in soundness).
- It adds tier work to decisions the numeric check already settles,
  unless made lazy.
- It changes E12 and what the `unproven-coincidence` lint stays quiet
  about, and it would have to be listed among the rules the structural
  question (`[ev]` PR 6993) switches off.
- It is the family Ev declined on #3283 (a read of a certified sign),
  narrower in what it reads (verdicts taken, not bounds) and the same
  in effect.

**Open it if** a reader appears that needs a decided sign and holds
nothing sign-free to read. Then it goes to Ev with its measurements
(what it proves and re-labels on the measured documents, its cost,
eager against deferring), as #3186 and #3283 were put.
