## Charter

The split's and the boolean's finish keep lineage, remove only
what they have proven theirs, make an unrepresentable state
unrepresentable in the type, read half-edge ownership from the edge,
and take both orientations of a configuration they take one of.

## Lanes

- **Lineage and removal**: the twice-crossed edge, carve's walks,
  the null-pair cycle, the redundant null-face clear, the unclaimed
  half-edge.
- **Reach**: the shoulder's other orientation, the wall on a fillet's
  tangent line.
- **Reads nothing reaches**: the `param_near` ellipse sites, the sweep
  trace's face-free record.
