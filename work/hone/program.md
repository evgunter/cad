**Interval and error propagation on the split and boolean ground.**
A shared rim's identity is three ε rows of one displacement; the
circle and sphere closed forms cancel at unequal radii; the circle ×
torus clear margin reads the floor; the half-angle ladder escalates in
its own metric; the subtract volume backstop charges no rounding for
flush operands; `section_sense` drops a zero winding's margin; a
vertex near a tilted face's carrier refuses where the face is far; a
sphere seam in a plane face loses its re-cut; a sphere cut-in survives
unglued; a short-ruling ring run refuses as a desync; and the reach
refusals state no recourse. One row is parked: the curved join's Chart
arm on spline and fitted iso images.

## From HONEPAIR (merged 2026-11-20)

HONEPAIR — one verdict on one margin at its own reach: split and boolean readings that decide two margins one at a time or lever a reading at the wrong extent

**A served verdict rests on one margin, levered at the reach it
moves.** Each row is a split or boolean reading that decides two
terms as two rows and serves when both read Zero (a tilt beside an
offset, a centre beside a radius), composes two readings so the first
escalation wins where the second would settle it, or levers a reading
at an extent other than the one the decision moves (a chord for a
wedge, a whole carrier for a short arc, a Euclidean reach for a
hinge). `work/README.md` bands interval and error propagation P2.
