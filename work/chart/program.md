**The chart boundary and its consumers, after the 2026-09-20 cut.**
What a chart's boundary IS — the polygon, the metring arms, the
clearance window that reads it — and the places where the boundary is
missing rather than merely loose.

Its sharpest row is that **a chart singularity has no boundary polygon
at all**: sphere and cone faces meeting at a pole, which is every
revolved body with a closed top, give the clearance window nothing to
read. Beside it, the cone/sphere/torus clearance windows still keep
M10-5's whole-carrier rectangles, the exact-region cells that would
retire `Certified::LowerBoundOnly` do not exist, and a circular hole's
two half-arc envelope boxes cover the whole disc, so no cell is ever
excluded.
