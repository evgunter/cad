**A body built by loft or sweep can enter every boolean.** Today
none can: every loft or sweep has spline (NURBS) seam edges, and the
boolean's operand gate (`boolean::reduce::gate_operand_edges`)
refuses an operand with any spline edge, whether or not that edge is
anywhere near the other body. Three demo joins stop there: klein wall 3
(the loop onto the bulb), lily wall 8 (the leaf's sheath onto its
blade) and teapot wall 3 (the spout onto the pot).

Deleting the gate alone builds nothing (PR 3984's backed-out attempt:
every outcome refuses typed). The chain behind it is this slate: the
sites the gate protects refuse a spline edge typed at their own site,
the gate goes, a spline edge crossing a face gets its crossing rung, the
join runs along a spline carrier, and the flush readings follow a NURBS
edge. Those links were filed across eight tracks; they are one thread.

Slate, order and posture: `work/splice/plan.md`; narrative in
`work/splice/log.md`.
