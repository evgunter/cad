Opened 2026-10-25 by a refold approved in chat. Every row here moved by
`git mv` with its id and body unchanged, and each carries a dated
`## Moved to SPLICE` note saying where it came from and why its band
changed. Nothing is dispatched.

## Why one track

The boolean's operand edge gate refuses any operand with a spline or
spiric edge, so no loft or sweep can enter a boolean. The rows that
make one able to were filed on eight tracks, mostly by REACH's
priority-stratified split, so each link of one dependency chain sat in
whichever band it happened to carry: the gate deletion at P0 on ORBIT,
the sites it waits on at P2 (ROOTS) and P3 (GAUGE), the crossing rung
parked on REACHHOLD and the spline join parked behind it on HONE. The
measurement that started this refold: the klein loop's contact rims are
`Circle` edges and the gate refuses it on a longitudinal NURBS seam
(`delete-the-boolean-operand-edge-gate.md`, 2026-10-25 section).

## Order

1. **The sites refuse typed.** `join-and-continuation-sites-…` and
   `a-nurbs-edges-sector-departure-…` first: they are what the gate
   still protects. PR 3984's branch carries a backed-out attempt

   `delete-the-boolean-operand-edge-gate.md`): re-derive it, do not

   `the-touch-and-cross-doors-…`, `carrier-walk-…`,
   `a-touch-beside-a-spline-…`) ride with this step or the next, so
   no spline edge reaches a site that reads it wrong.
2. **Delete the gate.** It owes the end-to-end row the emit note in
   its body names (a face crossing one NURBS edge twice with one
   sense). After this step every demo join still refuses, typed, at a
   named site: klein wall 3, lily wall 8 and teapot wall 3 each
   re-pin on the refusal they reach, with the scene's findings text
   updated in the same PR.
3. **The crossing rung**, then **the spline join** built on it.
4. **The flush readings** (the two SEAMRANK rows): two lofted bodies
   on an exact planar mate read flush along their NURBS edges. The
   sweep row `a_sheath_grafted_on_its_blade` is that contact; lily wall
   8 was too, until 2026-10-29.

**The seam with SECT.** Klein wall 3 (the loop's NURBS walls through
the bulb's cone) and teapot wall 3 (the spout's walls through the
