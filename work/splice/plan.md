## Why one track

The boolean's operand edge gate refused any operand with a spline or
spiric edge, so no loft or sweep could enter a boolean (it was deleted
on 2026-10-25). The rows that made one able to were filed on eight tracks, mostly by REACH's
priority-stratified split, so each link of one dependency chain sat in
whichever band it happened to carry: the gate deletion at P0 on ORBIT,
the sites it waits on at P2 (ROOTS) and P3 (GAUGE), the crossing rung
parked on REACHHOLD and the spline join parked behind it on HONE. The
measurement that started this refold: the klein loop's contact rims are
`Circle` edges and the gate refuses it on a longitudinal NURBS seam
(`delete-the-boolean-operand-edge-gate.md`, 2026-10-25 section).

## Order

1. **The reds and the wrong volume first**: the far union of two lofted
   cylinders at ε 1e-6 is a wrong answer, and the endpoint census drift
   is a red row.
2. **The carrier's parameter inverse.** One mechanism sits under the
   join on a spline carrier, the split parameter, the flush readings and
   the touch floor, so its shape is settled once, at the P0 join row,
   and the other rows read it.
3. **The face on its own sheet**: the pierce-point reading (P0), then the
   creased spline's second-order clearing.
4. **The kind-based readings**: the E rows can go to one lane as a
   bundle; each names its own reproducer.
5. **The P2/P3 tail** by thread, after the P0 and P1 rows are done.
   `the-interior-loop-guard-has-no-spline-by-curved-analytic-arm` pairs
   with SECTARMS's NURBS × implicit build: coordinate there first.

**The seam with SECT.** A NURBS face against a curved face is the
section object's rung-3 arm and SECTARMS' to build. Klein wall 3, teapot
wall 3 and lily wall 8 each need both tracks; at the 2026-11-01
hand-back lily wall 8 stops at SECT's
`c5-plane-torus-cone-cylinder-arms` and klein wall 3 at CARVETAIL's
`the-skin-cuts-an-arc-wall-at-each-sub-arc-joint`. Each join's current stop is kept in
`work/splice/curved-demo-joins-build.md` (2026-11-03).

Spiric operand edges are not on this slate: they stay deferred
(`reachhold/spiric-operand-edges-reopen-with-their-first-producer`,
