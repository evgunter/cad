the standing goal's blocker. `topo::split` refuses a box with one
cylindrical feature in BOTH orientations, and the second refusal
reports `CircularAxes` where the closed form gives a - b = 0.049 m.

because the arcs' carrier circles are not bit-identical. A user who
cannot split a box with a hole in it does not care which door
refused.
