## Closed

PR 5579. The span search itself was already shared (NURBS's span-locator unit, PR 4442). What remained were two clamps around it: `raw_span` and `Dir::const_index`. They existed because `KnotVector` refuses the two shapes a derivative ladder reaches: interior runs longer than the degree, and degree 0.

`geom_core::spline::LooseKnots` is the validated type for those shapes. It shares `KnotVector`'s search and its end checks (`end_clamped`, and `end_runs_clamped`, which `KnotStructure::clamped` now shares too), so both clamps are deleted. Bit identity was shown against the deleted functions over about 4.4M probes and an adversarial table. `loose_knots_span_fuzz` now pins the locator's contract. The `knots-f64-interim` rise (geom-core 27→28, total −1) was accepted by the orchestrator.
