**What is measured.** On a three-face revolved circle's finish that
refuses nothing (1,429 decisions, 1.0 s once the check is gone), three
predicates are nearly all of the time, and each slow decision is on the
DECISION path: the numeric margin is indeterminate, the tier is asked
once (`decision_ladders=1 contradiction_ladders=0`), and the outcome
stays `Indeterminate`.

| predicate | decisions | time | per slow decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `curve_pair_span_end_chord` | 100 | 454 ms | 8 of 46 to 67 ms: 150 to 390 forms, largest 1,970 terms, no `Refusal::Ring`; 41 to 62 ms in nodes that made a form, 4.5 ms in the 15 or 16 refused on budget |
| `bool_torus_region_component` | | 310 ms | 131, 55, 38, 37 ms: 280 to 1,744 forms, largest 988 to 1,890 terms; the 131 ms one has 32 `Ring` freezes, the first at 14 ms; 121 ms made against 7.9 refused |
| `bool_torus_region_corner` | | 116 ms | 54, 62 ms: 111 forms, largest 2,089 terms, no `Ring`; 44 to 52 ms made, 10 refused |

So the time goes in building large forms BELOW the frozen nodes, forms
of one to two thousand terms that are kept, and not in products that
are then refused. A pre-bound that refuses before multiplying
(`the-substituted-numerator-is-built-before-the-quotients-pre-bound-refuses-it`
is that shape) would not reach it.

**Why it matters.** It is what a curved leaf pays on every margin that
is numerically indeterminate, with the retry ladder on top, and it
holds `sweep`'s `a_revolved_circles_latitude_over_a_centre_box_is_exact`
at 1.33 s against a 1.0 s budget once BOXFINISH's switch is off
(`work/boxfinish/tier-3-at-every-box-replay-leaf-multiplies-the-decisions.md`).

**Not measured:** form degree (terms only); which ring guard refuses;

know to stop); any of it on a switch-on tree.
