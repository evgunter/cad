## What

(DOCM's two red rows ported as evidence, the freeze profiled by origin
and cause with SYM-1's instrument, the normalisation chain rendered
and the lost identity written as a form) and then remedied inside the
tier by the mechanism the measurement picks — a unit-vector atom with
two rules (`Σ U_i² = 1`, `U_i · sqrt(S) = a_i`), the common factor
cancelled before the square, or a degree-resetting `Sqrt` — behind a
dial, argued as equalities of reals under clause 1 like rules A–D,
shipped on only if affordable on the five measured documents. No door,
no `linalg`, no budget moves; if the tier cannot reach it without a
door change the unit stops after the measurement and says what the
door would have to hand it. Block SYM-B1 slot 1; the full v6 dual.

## Outcome (2026-09-14)

measurement says the tier is not what stands between a derived frame
and its authored twin: M10-8's constant fold already discharges the
document (with A0 alone it freezes NOTHING and certifies at every
width the parity row asks about up to `1e-3`), and the one refusal
left — at `5e-2`, at all three ε rows — is a clause-1 `Invalid` margin
on `newell_plane_residual` whose early form the tier has already
proved to be the zero form. No rule of the atom algebra can lift a
refusal the value channel makes before the identity test is asked.

(`crates/editor-core/tests/m10_derived_frame_interval.rs`), one of
