## (b) `EdgeEdgeCross` / `EdgeFacePierce` are called "categorically undeclarable" on a boolean-lane premise

`census.rs` (the `EdgeEdgeCross` push in the edge-edge lane, and the `EdgeFacePierce` push in `sweep_edge_face`) rest on 3′ "allows touching, never crossing" — which reads as a statement about interpenetration. At rest, two coplanar boundary edges *crossing* is not interpenetration: it is what a seat that overhangs its support looks like from the census's side.

**Executed, plain geometry, nothing exotic** (verified in this lane, independently of the review): post cap `[0.30,0.60] x [0.20,0.42]`, shelf underside `[0,0.9] x [0,0.30]`, cap straddling the shelf's `y = 0.30` edge, declared `post_cap ~ shelf_underside`:

```
[EdgeEdgeCross { a: 10v1, b: 15v1 }, EdgeEdgeCross { a: 12v1, b: 15v1 }]
```

Two hard `Unattributed` findings ⇒ `AssemblyError::AtRest` for a seat a user would draw by dragging a part until it overhangs. The declaration says the faces rest; the census refuses the document.

Whether these two classes SHOULD be backable is the design question, and it is genuinely open — a crossing is more than a touch, and the honest answer may be "these stay categorical, and the docs should say so on an at-rest premise rather than a reduction premise". What is not open is that the current justification is inherited from the lane that refines, exactly like D4's was.
