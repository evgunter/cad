## What the clause says

The rule, its argument and the classified population of 35 sites are in
`work/vnews/a-disabled-control-says-why-in-four-shapes.md`. The clause
the README owes is the rule and its sweep rule, present tense:

> **A control a reader cannot use says the sentence a click would have
> been answered with.** The question at the control is: if the reader
> got past this gate and the operation ran, what sentence would come
> back? If one would, the pre-click sentence and the refusal are the
> same sentence and have one composition — read off the refusal value,
> or off a wording helper the refusal's `Display` also calls. If none
> would, because no operation can be formed yet, the control gates a
> draft and a literal at the control is correct.
>
> **The population is that disposition, not the egui call that usually
> carries it.** The sweep is `add_enabled` / `add_enabled_ui` over the
> crate, unioned with the branches that draw a sentence where a control
> would be; `on_disabled_hover_text` finds a strict subset and cannot
> see a control that is disabled in silence or one whose reason is
> drawn beside it.

The second paragraph is the part this program's rows exist for: VIEW's
register records the call-shaped sweep as the eighth entry in its
proxy-failure table (#2320), and a universal in prose owes the sweep
rule that produces its population *at the sentence*.

## Why it is not already there

The README already carries the neighbouring clauses — *"A missing
file-chooser backend is not on the line at all"* (cited by
`platform::NO_CHOOSER_BACKEND`'s doc comment) and the rule about the
sweep for that fact ranging over reads of the field rather than readers
of the string. `refuse.rs` states the general rule three times in
passing, at `Refusal::self_instance`, `Refusal::affordance` and
`Refusal::exists_wording`, but no page states it once for the crate. The
VNEWS plan's exit shape names this clause as a precondition of that
program closing.

## Sequencing

The clause describes what the code does. Three sites do not obey it yet
(`work/vnews/undo-and-redo-are-disabled-in-silence-over-a-refusal-that-
has-words`, `.../the-range-button-re-mints-the-ratified-affordance`,
`.../the-new-document-button-states-its-refusal-twice`), so a present-
tense clause written today is false at three controls. Either the
clause waits for those three, or it lands with the exceptions named —
VDOC's call, and the VNEWS plan's exit shape assumes the former.
