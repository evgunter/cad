**The curved operand arms, after the 2026-09-20 priority-seam cut**:
the six rows where a boolean on an ordinary curved shape simply
refuses. `sphere ∪ sphere` refuses `CurvedPierceUnsupported` although
`intersect::route` mints the exact circle; a union with a definitely
tilted cylinder boss refuses `ClassificationInvariant`; `topo::split`
refuses EVERY cone face, though the elliptic plane×cone section is a
closed form; a slab cut through a cylinder wall refuses
`CurvedSectorSideUnsupported`; a declared cylindrical Rest on a
full-period wall has no verdict the chart door can give; and two
stacked parts cannot both have a rounded outline, because cosurface
disjoint walls glue as planar.

Six refusals, six shapes a person would expect to model on their first
afternoon. That is the whole charter now.
