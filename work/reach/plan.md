## Review posture

Protocol v7 (`docs/MODEL-AB-LOG.md`, Ev 2026-09-19): a unit ENTERS the
v6 dual iff its logic is especially tricky or it makes a decision that
is broad or hard to reverse — the orchestrator's call at spec time;
every other unit runs an opus implementer and an opus reviewer outside
the protocol (style review by default, full review where correctness
is meaningfully at risk); a mechanical change merges on the
orchestrator's own read. Blocks and slots are spent by protocol units
only, drawing from 6000 on. The lily's retirement and the touch-kinds
census unit are protocol candidates; the singles are not.
