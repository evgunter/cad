Claims to falsify:
1. **The certificate is sound.** The argument is the identity theorem: on one Bézier piece, the carrier's implicit equation composed with the rational patch, cleared of its weight, is a polynomial in (u, v). So one point decided off means the piece lies on the carrier over no open set. Attack each step:
   - Is the "off" that is decided off the whole algebraic carrier, or only off part of it: one nappe of the cone (the PR says the cone's `implicit_residual` reads `|h|`), the face's trimmed region, a cylinder's finite extent, or one sheet of the torus? Can a piece lie identically on the part of the variety the decision does not see, and still read Distinct?
   - Are the five points strictly interior to the piece, so that a point on a breakpoint cannot certify the wrong side?
   - Do the pieces follow the actual knots for every multiplicity, including a closed or periodic net, a net whose u and v knots differ, and a degenerate (zero-width) span?
   - Does the margin on "decided off" scale with ε, so that a point within the band never reads off? Run the 1e-6 and 1e-12 rows.
2. **The reading is one-way.** It answers Distinct or unread and never "same". No caller treats unread as Distinct, and no caller reads Distinct as anything stronger than "not cosurface over an open set". A sheet that touches the carrier along a curve, for example tangent along the rim arc as in klein, still reads Distinct. Check that this is all `parents_distinct_from` needs at both rungs the PR changes (the uncovered `(Zero, Zero)` arm and the covered-arc `LiesOn` rung), and that a wall tangent to the carrier along the arc does not mislead the arc's side reading downstream.
3. **The scope is confined.** The new reading runs only on `parents_distinct_from`'s `OutsideInventory` branch. `carrier_pair_relation`'s inventory is not widened, and none of glue, census, merge, holdings, `contact_verify` or `slit` sees it. Check the sweep table's dispositions, especially `holdings::on_carrier` and the tangency screen.
4. **The rows can go red and pin by identity.**
   - Kernel: off on every piece → Distinct; an exact cylinder patch → unread; a partial piece (on for `v ∈ [0, 0.1]` at a non-uniform knot) → unread; the crease pair. Re-run the three mutants and try others: points not interior, the margin dropped, the cone residual on one nappe.
   - Sweep: the flush tube on a cylinder, pinned per ε (`EdgeUndescribed { Crease }` at default ε and 1e-12, `CurvedPierceEscalated` at 1e-6).
   - Klein wall 3's re-pin, by face keys and kinds, at three ε.
5. **The new stops are honest.** At default ε and 1e-12 the sweep row stops at `EdgeUndescribed { edge: 10v1, failure: Crease }`: the describer reads the arc's ends on the tube wall's `u = 0.5` double knot (two rational arcs) and on its closure line as a crease.
