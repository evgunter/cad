The curved join (`topo::boolean::edge_join::joined_spec`) states the joined edge on the kept edge's carrier, run on to the killed edge's far end. The PR adds a first rung for EVERY carrier kind, **carried** (`carried_far`). Where the killed edge's carrier equals the kept edge's by content (exactly: kind, knots, control points, weights, frame, radius), and both stand at the same parameter at the vertex (exactly), the killed edge's own far end is the joined far parameter. It needs no inverse and no period. The old period / `param_near` readings remain as the fallback for distinct carriers of one locus. `kev_describing` certifies the joined spec at its merged ends. A spline beside a different carrier still refuses `JoinCarrierUnsupported`. The orchestrator's phase 0 ruling asked for exactly this design, uniform across kinds; whether it is RIGHT is yours to falsify.

Claims to falsify:
1. **The carried interval is what the two edges cover.** Attack orientation and direction: the kept and killed edges with opposite senses along the carrier, a killed edge whose interval runs back over the kept edge (overlap), a far end on the kept side (the PR says it "carries nothing"), and a vertex at either end of the kept edge. A wrong far parameter builds a joined edge that covers the wrong arc, which is a wrong body, not a refusal.
2. **Exact content equality is the right identity, and never a wrong carry.** Can two content-equal carriers yield an incoherent carry, e.g. a closed carrier whose two pieces meet at the vertex at parameters a period apart (the PR says it falls through to the period arm), or a reversed restatement? Can a split ever restate its parent's carrier NOT verbatim (any normalisation, re-knotting or reparametrisation in `EdgeCurve::split_specs` or downstream), so the carry silently stops firing where it should? The latter is only a refusal, but say so.
3. **Closed joins.** Two pieces of one closed carrier sharing both ends join over the union of their intervals: check a closed circle and the PR's closed three-span spline, including the seam, and that the joined interval spans exactly one period with the right start.
4. **The crease class.** The PR argues no crease can lie inside the joined interval (`crease_at` picks the kept edge; [ev] PR 5913 makes carriers C¹ inside cells). But the tier-3 check enforcing PR 5913 at rest (`measuredoor/tier-3-refuses-a-carrier-with-an-interior-c0-knot`) is still PARKED. Can a body at rest today carry a spline with an interior C⁰ knot such that a carried join spans it, or such that the vertex sits on a crease that `crease_at` misses? Build one if you can.
5. **Certification is sound and not weakened.** `kev_describing` still certifies the joined spec at both merged ends on every rung, and a carried spec that does not certify refuses.
6. **What moved.** On analytic carriers a split pair's joined interval is now the parent's exactly; on a spiric it moved by 1 ulp. Check that no golden, frame or slow-set row moved unreported, and that the rows can go red: the PR says the Circle, Line and Ellipse sweep rows stay green with the rung disabled, so only the topo `carry` unit row pins the rung there. Is that enough? Try mutants on `carried_far` (drop the parameter-equality check, drop the content check, swap ends).
7. **Producer.** No public boolean reaches the door today (the lane's phase 0); `Body::split_edge` + `Body::join_edges` is the public producer. Check the claim, and whether the sibling split-parameter lane's halves (PR 6441, not merged) would carry here.
