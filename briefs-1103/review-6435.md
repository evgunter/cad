The PR moves `Scaffolding { Skipped, Refused }` and a new `is_placeholder(body, edge)` to `topo::null`, gives `pcurves::loop_lift` / `Body::loop_lift` a `scaffolding` policy, makes the spline trim (`spline_region::Trim::of`) the one `Skipped` caller, and leaves every other caller `Refused`. Claims to falsify:
1. **The skip swallows only placeholders.** `is_placeholder` is true exactly of null scaffolding whose two ends are one point, and the skip leaves out only such a half's rowless side. Try: a null edge the sweep has since parted or given length; one whose ends are distinct points at the same location within the band; a placeholder that later acquires a carrier; and a non-null rowless half (it must still gap). Is a structural test (one `PointKey`) the right identity, or can two coincident-but-distinct vertices fool it?
2. **The skipped lift is the bare lift.** With the placeholder's joint element still summed, every other member's deck and start point equal the bare sheet's. Attack it where decks matter: a closed (periodic) chart, a placeholder sitting on or next to the chart's seam crossing, a hole loop with a placeholder, and the trim's winding number. A wrong deck reads a point In that is Out.
3. **Every `Refused` caller is right.** The PR claims the clearance, mesher, `lifted_images` (boxes, chart_region, quad lane) and tour callers read only at-rest bodies, which tier 2 keeps free of null scaffolding. Check that no `Refused` caller runs mid-boolean, and that `sphere_region` (still refusing, filed on CHARTTAIL) and `boxes::face_window_steps` (a wider box, never a wrong answer) are as dispositioned.
4. **The new readings are right.** `OffCarrier` at the wavy loft's pierce points (1, ∓1, 3) and at the saddle's corner pierces (±1, 0, ±1) is correct, and no wall that does hold a point is now passed over. Look for an input where a wall does hold a pierce point and the new path reads it Out.
5. **The re-pins are by identity and can go red** (the wavy loft row, renamed in `.config/nextest.toml`; the saddle row, either way round).
6. **The `placed: true` half** is unreached as claimed, the attempt was fair, and the filed row (`a-spline-wall-holding-a-pierce-point-has-no-operand-that-reaches-it`, P2) states what would reach it.
