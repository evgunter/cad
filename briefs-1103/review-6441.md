Teapot wall 3's meridian meets the spout's NURBS wall on the wall's seam. The split asked `Curve3::param_near` for the seam's parameter, which a spline has none of, so the union refused `PointSplitCarrierUnsupported`. The PR carries the parameter the boundary pass already held: `geom_brep::curve_face::Boundary::Meets.on_edge` → `carrier_cross`'s `held_on` (only when the pre-pass's `OnEdge(ey)` names the same edge) → `BoundaryCrossing::At` → `CurvedEvent::Pierce.on_edge` → `reduce::split_other_at_point(p, held)`, which splits at the held parameter on ANY carrier, keeping the new vertex exactly `p`. `sheets_part` now reads the tangent at the held parameter too.

Claims to falsify:
1. **The carried parameter is the meeting's point on that edge.** `on_edge` is the parameter of the same point `p` the split mints, on the same edge, to within the meeting's certificate. Can a held `t` ever be from another edge, another span, or another sheet (the edge's pcurve on the other face, a closed carrier's other period branch), or stale after an earlier split of the same edge renumbered it? Note that `held_on`'s same-edge guard compares keys: is a key the right identity after earlier splits in the same pass?
2. **Splitting at `t` while minting the vertex at `p` keeps the body consistent.** The split's two pieces end at `carrier(t)`, and the vertex is `p`: the PR measured 1.6e-17 m apart on teapot. What bounds that gap in general? Is it checked, or only assumed, and what happens when `t` is near a crease or a knot?
3. **No analytic path changes.** The lane's temporary probe found no analytic boundary edge reaching the held branch in the topo suite. But the PR says it splits at a held parameter "on any carrier". Construct an analytic case that does reach it (a line or circle boundary edge met by a curved span on its face), and check its output against the param_near split it replaces.
4. **A meeting at or near an edge end** carries `None` and falls back to the carrier. That is correct on an analytic edge and the loud refusal on a spline. Check the band-of-the-end logic at all three ε (ε-scaled).
5. **Teapot wall 3's re-pin** (`teapot::wall3_pinned`): a different refusal per ε row, each matched by geometry, not key. Check that each pin would go red if the stop moved (Q3), and that the 1e-6 stop (`SplineCrossingUnsettled { why: Floor }`, newly filed) is genuinely a different stop rather than flake or ε-unaware geometry.
