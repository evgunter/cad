## Doc-gate change discipline

`scripts/doc-gate.sh` has a `--selftest`; any gate change carries a
selftest arm proving the new arm fires (a planted `not(feature)`
doc error goes red) and the clean tree passes. Mind the gate's
measured cost history (`docs/CI-MINUTES-2026-08.md` F6): say in the
PR what the widening costs on the fmt job (the selftest cases are
mktemp-fixture-based and cheap; a second full workspace doc pass is
NOT — prefer a targeted `not(feature)` pass over the crates that
declare such modules, derived not listed, if you widen).
