At `f64`, `Interval` and `Dual` the allowance measured bit-identical to the lifted spellings, and code outside `geom-core` could not use it to turn an `f64` into a `T`. Of nine claims, the `Sym` half of verdict equivalence and the floor reorder were falsified.

**MAJOR:**
- **DEMONSTRATED (measured `proven()` and sign differences against the lifted spellings): at `Sym` an allowance changes what is proved, both ways.** `Allowance == NONE` was a float comparison, so a bound of exactly zero read as "no bound" and proved a `Zero` the lifted spelling did not; the scaling doors made a form-zero margin opaque, so the five mate sites lost proofs they had.

**MINORs:** the `spline_trim` floor reorder turns a clean "says nothing" into an in-band refusal (demonstrated at the formula); `UnitVec3::new_scaled` decides differently for a negative, non-finite or huge arm (demonstrated); DESIGN `[tolerance.margins]` and `Margin`'s doc misdescribe the new type; the disposition of `from_computed` lines has holes (two bounds left lifted that fit the form, one line missing).

**Spec deviations.** Reported: five kinds of bound not moved, each with a filed row; the reviewer judged the obstacles real and two rows' "the rule is underspecified" wrong (restated in the fix pass). Silent: the `Sym` change; the floor's verdict change, described in the PR body as harmless.
