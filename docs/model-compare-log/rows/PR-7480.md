# PR 7480: the side reader's turn and its aim's step are as narrow as their inputs over a box

**Unit:** BOXFINISH, `an-aimed-rays-other-readings-still-carry-a-moving-faces-box-twice`: over a parameter box the side reader's in-plane turn and its aim's step carried a moving face's box twice, so a slab boxed wider than about ± 0.215 of its depth lost its rays. The turn is read in the face's plane with no frame chosen, the step reads the in-plane clearance, and a pair of crossings a box cannot hold apart sets the ray aside. The door's axis-aligned slab reads its side by its caps' rays at depth 1 ± 0.25.

**Letter and tier:** M, dual (sequential): it changes what a ray reads on every body the side reader walks, and a wrong read is a wrong side. Both were logged in `work/boxfinish/log.md` before the draw.
