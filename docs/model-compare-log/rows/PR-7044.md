# PR 7044: the cylinder and sphere certificates read their true distance on every triangle

No MAJOR at either head. The reviewer built an exact-rational oracle and ran about 480,000 triangles through the new distance: on triangles with their corners on a sphere or a cylinder it is within 0.31 of a rounding allowance of the truth, and the same to the bit in all six corner orders. It reproduced the PR's count of misread triangles on the `mesh` suite exactly (6,291 on the rectangle lane, 94,386 on the chart lane) and found every mesh of that suite byte-identical with main's certificate swapped in.

**The implementer's measure-first pass corrected the unit's spec on three points**, and the review confirmed each: both halves of a lattice cell are misread, by corner order, so a triangle's sibling is no safeguard; the misreading is not only a NaN (127 emitted triangles read a finite distance too long, up to 11 % under the truth); and the chart lane's cylinder arm, which the spec had not measured, holds most of them. 689,547 emitted triangles were certified at 0 or short across the four corpora, none over its δ at its true value.

The MINORs of the first review, by defect:

- **The normal's choice of sides was pinned by no row, DEMONSTRATED:** a fixed pair in place of the two-shorter-sides select passed all 32 rows, and the PR's mutant table listed a mutant as red that was not.
- **A wrong finite answer off the unit direction, DEMONSTRATED:** an axis of norm 10¹⁵⁵ answered 2.449 for a true 0.5385 (too long, so the certificate reads 0), while the doc said the direction need not be unit. Coordinates under 10⁻⁷⁷ underflowed to an answer 2·10¹⁴ allowances long (a NOTE).
- **The PR body's reason for "no mesh over δ" was false on the chart lane, DEMONSTRATED:** that arm has no theorem, and the reviewer measured the misread triangles there at 0.4 to 0.5 of δ with a tail to 0.652.
