## Honesty rows

   plainly.** M9-PLAN's item 7 spoke of "the declared-contact joins
   the lily can now spell"; that set was measured and it is EMPTY.
   The governing sentence is the plan's own exit shape
   ("dispositioned honestly"), which the M9-5 spec says explicitly
   beats the optimistic item-7 clause — so this is a finding, not a
   shortfall. But a reader who remembers only the plan's optimism
   should read this row: **no lily wall flipped in M9.** Wall 1 is
   banked (#968), wall 2 stays deferred by ruling with its
   germ-chord half named, wall 7 is handed to the VERBS breadth
   slate, wall 8 rides the post-M9 pcurve migration.

2. **The join lane shipped with a limitation nobody had stated, and
   its own milestone found it.** A purely cylindrical declared
   `Rest`, with no planar `Rest` beside it, does not reach the rest
   lane (**#1032**) — the refusal survives full engagement, partial
   engagement, and the two-peg fixture's own 3-arc face structure.
   M9-3's acceptance fixture works because it carries a planar
   `Rest` at the rim plane, and nobody noticed that was
   load-bearing until M9-5 tried to build without one. Recorded
   because a milestone that closes over an unstated limitation
   teaches the next reader the wrong thing.

   7 blamed the lantern's spherical zone ("two half-bands on ONE

   Consequence, written into the probe and into VERBS-PLAN: **a
   sphere×sphere germ arm alone will not flip wall 7** — a planning
   error caught before the arm was scoped rather than after it
   failed to move the wall.
