PROPS recourse grammar — the eleven rows PROPS closed on (PR #3942).

Deleted at PROPS' close, 2026-10-03.
Recover with `git show 63df2069c:docs/PROPS-RECOURSE-GRAMMAR-SPEC.md`.

**Spec note worth keeping: the ruling that shaped the unit was "a twelfth
spelling of a recourse is the defect this unit closes, so minting one is
the one thing it must not do."** ENCL and TOPO had built the homes
(`KERNEL_LIMIT_RECOURSE`, `KERNEL_DEFECT_ENDING`, `geom_brep::recourse`,
`sized_recourse`) while PROPS was parked, and the row set was eleven
sites that had each spelled their own. Naming the anti-goal rather than
the goal is what kept a sweep over eleven refusals from becoming a
twelfth convention: the unit consolidated two existing spellings into
each of its two new helpers and retired a third constant outright.

Two clauses earned their place. **Row 7 was named as "a typing question
wearing a wording question's clothes"** — an edge off its own surface is
a kernel defect, a valid face outside the inventory is not — and the unit
answered it with two arms and a per-site premise, refuting a sibling
program's hand-kept census of predicate names in the process. And **row
9, Ev's own concision request, was required to be measured rather than
estimated**, which is how the unit found that one of the five sites had
already been reworded by another program and that two arms legitimately
GREW because they gained an ending they had lacked.
