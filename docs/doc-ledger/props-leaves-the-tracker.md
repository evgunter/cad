## The lesson this program paid for three times

**A spec that asserts a premise hands the unit a defect.** One premise
about `du_of_rims` — that it already sums a full rim to `2π` — was
asserted in `docs/PROPS-SPHERE-POLE-SIDE-SPEC.md` rather than required to
be decided; the sphere unit inherited the assertion and shipped a MAJOR
through it; the fix added a guard that checked the **sum** where a
**cover** was needed, and that shipped to main, where a blinded reviewer
found a half rim stated twice measuring a whole cap. It is carried into
`work/flux/plan.md` §4 so the successor's specs require a decide.

Two smaller ones, same file: a re-baselined golden is not a re-pointed
row (twice in one unit a row's *subject* moved while its numbers were
movable), and a narrower bound is the expected outcome of a width fix and
therefore not evidence that it is correct — containment is.
