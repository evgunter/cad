## What it left

- reads a per-solid signed volume (check 7) and the sign level through
  one `plus_v_by_sign`, with a `SignCertificate`;
- refuses a solid whose shells do not bound winding number 0 or 1
  everywhere (check 10);
- winds line, circle and ellipse planar loops exactly (check 6,
  `loop_winding.rs`);
- nests rings through the carrier walk, with contact arms that see a
  crossing and a tangency (check 9);
- refuses poisoned, unrepresentable and infinite surface and curve
  datums, and bad frame directions (check 1).

roster.
