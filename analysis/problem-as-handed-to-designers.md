## What needs deciding

`crates/geom-core/README.md`, "Float rounding", the lever bullet, is ratified text: "a verdict that claims a definite sign reads the extent from below, one that acts on its Zero reads it from above, and one that acts on every sign reads both and acts only where they agree", and of the consumed extent, "any point is sound provided the datum and its lever are read at the same one" and "a record that verdicts read from different points holds its region, not a lever".

Until now every caller of the turn-lever doors (`topo::pcurves::decision::edge_reach` and `stored_reach`; `topo::wedge_reach::{wedge_reach, edge_wedge_reach, WedgeReach}`) took one lever, a bound from below (the carrier's ends and the faces' boundary vertices). A unit in flight (`oneround/turn-lever`, PR 7262; its tree is checked out read-only for you at `/root/.local/share/cad-work/turn-lever-read`) has made those doors return both bounds (`geom_brep::LeverBounds::Between`): from below as before, from above the farthest of the faces' covers, the edge's own span cover, and those points. About 41 callers must now take the bound each needs. The lane checked each caller against the code and stopped, because the callers are not what the row filed them as:

- Only two kinds of caller make one kind of decision on the lever: an edge's certification (`EdgeCurve::certify` / `recertify`: definite sign throughout) and `pcurve_singular_sheet`.
- Every other consumer is a COMPOSITE verdict: it makes several decisions on the one lever, of different kinds. The lane's reading, which you should check: `geom_brep::must_carry_over_edge` and `must_carry_description` (a Smooth premise that acts on a first-order Zero; a second order that is intrinsic where its jet is determinate and conventional where it is not); `classify_shared_rim` (a Smooth premise, then second order and seam or cusp); `edge_wedge_over_as` (Corner → Crease, Tangent → Seam or End); `classify_seam`; `dihedral_wedge` then `tangent_second_order`; the joint turn (`Period::decide`'s on-mark slit against k); `boolean::join::stands_on` and `boolean::reduce::sheets_part`, which compare a sign with Negative and act on `false` unrefused; `slit::walls_inside`, which levers two readings on one arm.
- For a scalar decision the tree has a home for "read both bounds, act only where they agree": `topo::validate::decide_levered`, with a straddle row per predicate (`bool_fate_corner_straddle` is one). For the composite verdicts it has none: nothing in `MustCarryVerdict`, `PairRefusal`, the wedge fold's verdict, `RimRouting`, `SeamUnread`, `PinMiss` or `SingularMiss` says "the two bounds disagree".

So: what should be true of the code so that every decision made on a turn's lever reads the bound its own action needs, and a disagreement between the bounds is refused loudly, once, in one spelling? What do the composite consumers take, where is the choice of bound made, what does a disagreement become and for whom, and what does that bind for every later consumer of a lever?
