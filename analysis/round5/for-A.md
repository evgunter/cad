## For Ev

**Recommendation (likely).** A sector bound is a unit direction plus what stands behind it, never a vector scaled by a lever. One departure record serves the star, the bound-side read, the cut and the link.

**Terms.** A *bound* is one side of a face's corner at a vertex: an edge leaving it, or a *divider*, a direction a wide corner is cut at. A *lever* is the length a tilt is multiplied by to give a displacement. *Reach* is how far an edge gets from the vertex. `LeverBounds` (exists today) is a lever known exactly, or between a bound from below and one from above. A *straddle* is the two bounds giving different signs; it refuses.

**Premise (sure).**
- The cut already has this shape. `boolean::fate::corner_holds` takes a unit direction, a reach from the vertex known at both bounds, and a bend.
- The tree has four forms of one concept, with different levers.
- `geom_brep::sector_shape` forces the packing: it asks for scaled chords and reads its arm from their lengths.

**Worked example (sure).** An ellipse with semi-axes 10 and 1, and an edge leaving the end of the minor axis and running nearly all the way round. Its diameter is 20. Its far distance from the vertex is about 10.05. Today's lever is a lower bound on 20, on the side that claims a definite sign. So a tilt between ε/20 and ε/10 is called definite where the edge reads within the band.

**The final state.**
1. **One departure record, in `topo`, built by one door from the body.** It holds:
   - a unit direction;
   - a reach, as `LeverBounds` read from the vertex: a line's chord, exact; a circular arc's, exact; for an ellipse, spiric or spline, the edge's farthest sampled point from below and its cover's far distance from above;
   - what stands behind it: a line's far vertex; a curved edge's bend and, for a circle or an ellipse, its arc; a divider's face section and its corner's arm.
2. **A corner's arm is the lesser of its two bounds' reaches, at each bound.** It is exact where both reaches are, and derived where the two bounds are held. `SectorShape.arm`, `FullTurn.arm`, `ApexShape.arm`, the stored `star::Sector.arm` and `metering_arm` go. A divider is levered at its corner's arm: a sector's Zero claims only its neighbourhood, and no reader takes it as two faces coinciding without the glue door's verdict.

A site reads one bound only by writing `.below()` or `.above()`, with a row that goes red when the bound is swapped. At a slit's tip, only an agreed Zero reads as the whole turn.

5. **The link.** A ray of the gathered cone is a real point (a line's far vertex) or a departure read at both bounds. Where the cone's reading needs a position in its own picture, it forms the ray's displacement, direction times reach, at each bound. That is read only against a plane, never as a place on the body. The link's face-tie fan stays a named construction: a polygon past the face, sized by the face cover's far distance, read as a length.
