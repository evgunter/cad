The fork went to Ev as an `[ev]` PR with your final texts (three README edits, identical in both). Ev has commented, quoting the PR body's account of the alternative both of you rejected for edit 2:

> > levering a divider at its face's far distance from the vertex. That is owed only if some reader takes a corner's Zero as two faces coinciding; both designers read the consumers and found none does without the glue door's whole-face verdict, and it would refuse many straight corners.

Ev reads carefully and probes fudged invariants; Ev favours structural enforcement of an invariant over discipline, and "define what the thing is" over a rule that happens to hold for today's callers. The question is not whether today's consumers are safe (you both read them). It is whether edit 2, as a ratified sentence, states something that would be FALSE for a consumer that could legitimately be written, and whether the design rests on a fact about today's readers that nothing holds.

Edit 2 as sent: "A turn between two directions out of one point, each with its own extent, is consumed where both run: its lever is the lesser of the two extents' far distances from that point, each read from below and from above. A direction drawn between them has no extent of its own and takes that lever."

The README bullet it sits in opens: "The consumed extent: the region a verdict is used over." And the lever bullet (as Ev has just approved it on PR 7619): a lever read off a consumed extent is known from both sides; a definite sign is served at the bound from below and a Zero at the bound from above.

My reading, a hypothesis for you to check and not a position to adopt: the bullet defines the extent by what the verdict is USED over, and edit 2 fixes it by what the DATUM is (two directions out of a point), so the sentence is true only of a consumer whose Zero claims the neighbourhood out to the shorter direction's reach. A consumer that took a corner's Zero as a statement about a whole face (two faces coincide; a divider's side governs its whole sector of the face) would consume the face and be under-levered at the lesser reach: its Zero would be served at a bound from above that is not above what it is used over. Both of you found no such reader today, and that the one reader that claims a whole face reads the face itself. But that is a fact about the readers, held by nothing.
