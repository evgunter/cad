BACKGROUND. A datum read at one point `p` of a span (a sine, a slope, a coefficient centred there, a root's transversality) moves over the span by at most the datum times the span's reach from `p`, `R(p)`, which is at most the diameter `D`. A lower bound on `D` (`edge_extent_inf`, a chord, a ball's radius about another point) is not a lower bound on `R(p)`, so a definite sign levered at it can be claimed where the honest lever reads in band. The unit converts the callers that hold their read point, and leaves for a later unit the spline certificate's per-sample levers. Two lessons from the sibling units' reviews, which found defects exactly here: (1) which bound a read needs (from below, from above, both) is decided by what each CONSUMER does on each sign of the verdict, read in the consumer and not in the site's comment, a sign compared and returned as a bool included; (2) "the read point" has to be the point the datum is actually evaluated at, in the code, not the point the comment names.
