THE UNIT. A datum read at one point `p` of a span (a sine, a slope, a coefficient centred there, a root's transversality) moves over the span by at most the datum times the span's reach from `p`, `R(p)`, which is at most the diameter `D`. A lower bound on `D` (`edge_extent_inf`, or a chord between the span's ends, or a two-point read through the point doors) is NOT a lower bound on `R(p)`: a definite sign read at it can come from a datum that `R(p)` reads in band (a segment met at its middle reaches half its chord). From above `D` is sound and loose. The row lists the callers that lever a point-read datum by a diameter or a chord. Each is converted to read the span's reach from the point its datum is read at, through the reach doors that exist (`edge_reach_bounds`; in `topo`, `cover::edge_reach_bounds` and `cover::piece_reach_bounds`), taking the bound its verdict is owed. `edge_extent_inf`'s own doc states the levering-at-`D` as its contract for two point-read readings: that contract is the second thing that changes.
