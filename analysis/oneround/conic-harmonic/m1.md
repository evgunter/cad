# M1 — first-degree roots, three ways

Branch `oneround/conic-harmonic-probe` (scratch, base `bd07a867`). Run at
`CAD_TOLERANCE_EPS` 1e-9 and 1e-12, `Band::linear(Tol::witness())`, K = 10.

## What decides

- **Posing the root about the stored point is what recovers the arc's
  scale; taking only the constant from it is not.** Near the anchor, (ii)
  lands 3 to 10 times nearer than (i) and still at the semi-axis's
  rounding; (iii) lands at the parameter's own last bit.
- **No first-degree door certifies its root.** The plane door and
  `ring_path::plane_roots` answer an uncertified closed form;
  `curve_pair`'s transverse arm hands it to the Gauss–Newton polish,
  which replaces it. So nowhere does "(iii) certifies where (i) refuses"
  apply to a plane; it applies only to `harmonic_roots_as`.
- **`harmonic_roots_as` refuses, it never answers wrongly**, in (i) and
  (ii) alike, and the refusal is not chiefly the turn charge.
- **A separate defect, on main, reported on its own**: `curve_pair::meet`
  certifies "no meeting" beside the anchor. No variant here repairs it.

Variants: (i) the branch as it stands; (ii) the harmonic's constant read
`(S − o)·n − (p·k_c + q·k_s)`, the solve unchanged; (iii) the two roots
of `h − 2P·sin²(φ/2) + Q·sin φ`, `φ` measured from the anchor.

**The truth.** The curve the stored numbers state (anchor, axis, seam,
semi-axes, phase; `v = axis × seam` exactly), in 60-digit `mpmath`, from
the harness's printed `f64`s read back exactly. Roots are solved in
`mpmath` and checked by their own residual (below 1e-40). No kernel code
is called. A root's miss is the chord between the curve's points at the
kernel's parameter and at the true one.

**The carriers.** Real `plane_cone_section` ellipses, cone half-angle
1.3, anchor near `(1.3, −0.7, 0.4)`, nothing axis-aligned. The mint
allowed the whole ladder `a` = 1e4 … 1e9 at both ε, in two shapes: fat
(`b/a` = 0.3) and thin (apex gap 1 m, `b` = 186 … 5.9e4 m). Poses: a
target 0.3 m and 3 m of arc from the anchor ("near"), 1 km ("mid"), and
at `θ` = 0.5 and 2 ("far"); crossing angles 90°, 30°, 5°. 54 poses per
ladder step.

## Door D — `conic_quadric_roots`' first-harmonic lane (`harmonic_roots_as`)

An arc-held ellipse reaches this lane only where its second harmonic
reads in the zero band. D2 is the one such pose I could build: the wall
along which the minted ellipse projects to a circle, radius `b`, its axis
1, 6 or 200 m beside the ellipse's. D1 is the lane's ordinary customer,
a circle of radius `a` (stored at its centre, as every circle is)
against a unit sphere beside its arc.

| pose | ε 1e-9 | ε 1e-12 |
|---|---|---|
| D1, radius 1e2 | certifies, 3 of 3 | refuses, `curve_face_pose_noise` |
| D1, radius 1e3 … 1e7 | refuses, `curve_face_pose_noise`, 15 of 15 | the same |
| D2, a = 1e4, (i) and (ii) | refuses, `conic_quadric_first_root_slack`, 6 of 6 | refuses, `curve_face_pose_noise` |
| D2, a = 1e4, (iii) | certifies 3 of 6, misses 4e-12 … 4e-11 m, each inside its slack | refuses, `curve_face_pose_noise` |
| D2, a = 1e5 fat | all three refuse, `conic_quadric_first_root_slack` | ladder lane: refusals |
| D2, a = 1e5 thin, a ≥ 1e6 | all three refuse, `curve_face_pose_noise` | ladder lane: refusals |

No wrong root in 144 runs. The slack `harmonic_roots_as` read, D2 at
1e4: the noise over the root's slope is 1.7e-13 … 4.8e-10 rad, the turn
charge `rounding_charge(τ)` 1.1e-14 rad; times the speed 1e4, 1.8e-9 …
4.8e-6 m against 1.1e-10 m. So at this size the refusal is the uniform
noise `rounding_charge((|C₀ − o| + a)²)/2r`, and (ii) reads the same
slack to 15 digits, because the noise is a charge and does not see where
the constant was read. The turn charge alone passes ε at `a` = 1e4 and
reaches it at `a` ≈ 1e5 (1.1e-9 m); from there every pose would refuse
on it, but `curve_face_pose_noise` refuses first. These are refusals of
valid geometry: the true roots exist and (iii)'s land within 4e-11 m.
(iii)'s slack is my own (the form's rounding at the root over its slope,
plus `rounding_charge(|θ − θ₀|)·speed`, each root decided alone); past
1e4 it refuses too, on the wall's own `r = b`, which says nothing about
the conic.

## Door E — `topo::ring_path::plane_roots`

It takes a `CircleArc`, never an ellipse: the harmonic it solves is the
path piece's circle's. The conic's centre enters as the *plane's*
origin (`LoopArc::plane`, `c.conic().frame().origin()`), and the far
centre is covered by a charged `slack` (`carrier_resolution`). So (iii)
does not apply; (ii) is the plane stated through the stored point. A
unit circle dipping `gap` through the plane of the fat ellipse, 0.3 m
from the anchor; the truth is that plane through the stored anchor.

| a | slack | worst root miss on the unit circle, (i) | (ii) | first gap refused (both) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1e4 | 7e-11 | 1.5e-9 (1.4e-8 at ε 1e-12) | 3e-13 | 3e-9 (ε 1e-9 only) |
| 1e5 | 7e-10 | 1.8e-8 (6.4e-8) | 4e-13 | 3e-9 (ε 1e-9 only) |
| 1e6 | 7e-9 | 2.6e-7 | 3e-13 | 3e-9 |
| 1e7 | 7e-8 | 7.8e-7 | 1e-13 | 3e-8 |
| 1e8 | 7e-7 | 1.5e-6 | 2e-13 | 1e-7 |
| 1e9 | 7e-6 | 1.6e-6 | 3e-16 | 1e-6 |

No wrong verdict in 96 runs per ε: every "meets" and "misses" agrees
with the truth, and the slack refuses the rest (graze in band). The
verdicts of (i) and (ii) are identical, since the slack does not change;
under (ii) the refusals at a gap between Kε and the slack are refusals
of valid geometry, as its roots there would be good to 1e-13. The roots
(i) does answer are uncertified and up to 2.6e-7 m off at `a` = 1e6
(1e-9 m at a gap of 1 mm).
