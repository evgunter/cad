# M2 — the sphere beside the anchor

Branch `oneround/conic-harmonic-probe` (scratch, base `bd07a867`), at
`CAD_TOLERANCE_EPS` 1e-9 and 1e-12, K = 10, `f64`.

## What decides

- **(ii) certifies at the arc's scale with the existing subdivision.**
  At ε 1e-9 it certifies 456 of 480 poses against 261 as the branch
  stands, and every fat ellipse (`b/a` = 0.3) at every size to 1e8.
- **The whole-turn `|F⁗|` bound does not stop it.** The bound is
  enormous (1.5e13 at `a` = 1e6, `r` = 1) and the walk still settles.
- **The whole-turn quantity that does bite is `speed_hi`**, the
  carrier's top speed `a`, in the root-slack meter: every refusal left
  at ε 1e-9 but two would pass at the arc's own speed.
- **The ladder's own expansion loses the digits**: told the harmonics
  carry no noise, it escalates spuriously (M3).
- As the branch stands the refusal is `Unsettled::Tangency`, not a noise
  refusal by name. No variant answered wrongly.

## Built

- `ProbeOffsetJet` (`curve_face/conic_quadric.rs`): the residual as a
  polynomial in the offsets about the stored point,
  `q(θ) = e + U·(cos θ − k_c) + W·(sin θ − k_s)`, `e = S − o`,
  `F = (|q|² − r²)/2r` read `(|q| − r)(|q| + r)/2r`. Its `jet` gives
  `F … F‴` at a parameter, each with a bound read **at that point**:
  `rounding_charge` of the magnitudes its terms are summed from. Its
  `fourth` bounds `|F⁗|` over the whole turn.
- The door's ladder arm, under the switch: variant 1 hands that jet to
  `half_angle_roots` as `HalfAngleFrame::jet` (`Terms::Local`), nothing
  else changed, the centre harmonics and their noise as the door forms
  them. Variant 3 also zeroes `walk.noise`, so the ladder runs.
- Log lines in `half_angle.rs` (the slack meter, the piece a tangency
  refusal stops on).
- `probe_conic_harmonic/m2.rs` (`m2_sphere_beside_the_anchor`),
  `analysis/oneround/conic-harmonic/oracle_m2.py`.

**Poses.** The minted ladder of M1, `a` = 1e4 … 1e8, fat and thin. A
sphere of radius 0.01, 1 or 30 m whose centre stands 0.2, 0.7, 0.99 or
0.9999 of its radius off the arc, at 0, 0.4, −3 and 300 m of arc from
the anchor: 48 poses per ladder step, 480 in all. Each truly crosses
twice.

**The truth.** The curve the stored numbers state, in 60-digit `mpmath`
(M1's oracle). The roots of `|P(θ) − c| − r` by a sign scan of the span
widened threefold, bisected in `mpmath`. The scratch jet is checked too:
its four values against the same derivatives formed in `mpmath`, at
every root, both span ends, the target, and `θ` = 0.5 and 2. Its worst
error is 0.36 of its bound, so the bounds held everywhere I read them.

## Why (i) refuses

The door's noise is `rounding_charge((|C₀ − o| + a)²)/2r`: 3.6e-3 m at
`a` = 1e6, `r` = 1, and it falls as `1/r`, which is why the 30 m sphere
mostly certifies. The noise meter keeps the ladder from running; the
subdivision charges the noise to each Taylor term, so no piece about a
root reads monotone, the pieces shrink to the width row, and the walk
refuses `Unsettled::Tangency`. On the piece it stops on (fat 1e6,
`r` = 1) the harmonics read `F` = −1.5e-4 ± 3.6e-3 and `F′` = −8e-3 ±
7e-3 at a crossing whose true slope is of order 1e5 per radian: the
digits are gone from the centre harmonics themselves, as the designers
said. The caller I read (`topo::boolean::reduce`) takes a door's limit
to its localized reading against the face; I did not follow it.

## Why (ii) certifies, and what still refuses

The jet's bounds are read at the point, so beside the anchor they are a
few `u·r`. The one whole-turn number it hands over, `fourth`, enters as
`M₄w⁴/24` and `M₄w³/6` on a piece of half-width `w`, and the walk
halves `w` until those vanish. I did not count pieces; no run reached
the piece budget (`Unsettled::Exhausted` never appeared).

What is left:

- **The root-slack meter reads `speed_hi`.** `arc = speed_hi·reach/lever`
  with `speed_hi` the semi-major axis, while the arc beside the anchor
  runs at `b`. Re-reading the logged `reach` and `lever` at the span's
  own top speed, `√((a·sin θ)² + (b·cos θ)²)` at its far end: all 22
  slack refusals of (ii) at ε 1e-9 pass, and 164 of 253 at ε 1e-12.
  This is the thin shape's loss (`a/b` = 54 … 1700); fat ellipses lose
  nothing at ε 1e-9.
- The other 89 at ε 1e-12 are the residual's own reading at the root
  against a 1e-12 band (a 30 m sphere at depth 0.9999 refuses 40 of 40
  in both variants): a shallow crossing, not the conic's posing.
- 2 `Tangency` at ε 1e-9: thin 1e4 and 1e5, `r` = 0.01, depth 0.9999.
  Not traced.
- 11 escalations on `conic_quadric_sub_side` at ε 1e-12, the same in (i)
  and (ii): a cut read in the band's gap.

So the windowed bound that has to belong to the polynomial is the
**speed** (and with it the monotone and width rows, which read
`speed_hi` the same way); the Taylor bound may stay whole-turn.

## The ladder (variant 3)

With the jet as in (ii) and `walk.noise` set to zero, the ladder runs on
the centre harmonics. It escalates where (ii) certified: 23 poses at
ε 1e-9 (all `conic_quadric_disc`), 65 at ε 1e-12 (59 `_disc`, 4 `_odd`,
2 `_split`). Every one is spurious: (ii) certifies those poses and the
truth has two clean crossings. The ladder's expansion about an anchor
therefore does lose the digits when it is fed centre harmonics, and the
door's noise meter is what keeps that from showing today. M3 measures
it.
