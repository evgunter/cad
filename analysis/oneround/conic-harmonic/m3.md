# M3 — the ladder's anchors

Branch `oneround/conic-harmonic-probe`, ε 1e-9 and 1e-12, K = 10, `f64`.

## What decides

- **Re-basing the constant does not work.** With `c₀` re-read so that
  `F(anchor)` should come out right, the quartic's low coefficient is
  still wrong by up to 64 m (median 3e-6 m): `c₀ + c₁ + c₂` rounds at
  the harmonics' own size whatever `c₀` holds.
- **Forming the five coefficients about the anchor from the offsets
  does**: `F(anchor)` to 4e-16 m in the median, the depressed quartic's
  `s` to 2e-12 relative (2e-8 at worst), against errors of order 1 to 75
  from centre harmonics.
- **The ladder's escalations are unsound either way, and at ε 1e-9
  rounding is not why.** It escalates at 14 of 270 clean crossings with
  centre harmonics, with the re-based constant, and with exact
  coefficients alike: the same 14. That is its own metric (the filed
  `half-angle-ladder-escalates-in-its-own-metric`), not the posing.
- So the polynomial owes the ladder nothing it cannot give cheaply (two
  jets), but giving it does not make the ladder's escalations sound.

## What the ladder needs, and the two ways

`ladder_roots` tries anchors `θₐ` = the span's middle, then every
`τ/32` either side (32 candidates), takes the first whose pole
`θₐ + π` is decided off a root and well conditioned, and forms
`F·(1 + t²)²` in `t = tan((θ − θₐ)/2)`:
`a = [c₀+c₁+c₂, 2s₁+4s₂, 2c₀−6c₂, 2s₁−4s₂, c₀−c₁+c₂]` from the harmonics
rotated to the anchor, then monic in `τ = 2ρt`, then depressed.

In these terms `a₀ = F(θₐ)`, `a₁ = 2F′(θₐ)`, `a₄ = F(pole)`,
`a₃ = −2F′(pole)`, `a₂ = F(θₐ) + F(pole) + F″(θₐ) + F″(pole)`. So:

- **(a) re-base the constant**: keep the centre harmonics, set
  `c₀ := F_offsets(θₐ) − c₁ − c₂` (variant 4).
- **(b) form the coefficients about the anchor**: the five numbers above
  from the offsets jet (M2's `ProbeOffsetJet`) at the anchor and at its
  pole: two jet evaluations (variant 5).

Both under the switch in `half_angle.rs::ladder_roots`, reading the
door's `HalfAngleFrame::jet`; variant 3 is the centre harmonics
untouched. In all three the noise is zeroed so the ladder runs, and the
subdivision reads the offsets jet as in M2 (ii). Harness
`probe_conic_harmonic/m3.rs` (`m3_the_ladders_anchors`), oracle
`oracle_m3.py`.

**Poses.** The minted ladder, `a` = 1e4 … 1e8, fat and thin; spheres of
radius 0.01, 1, 30 m at 0.4, −3 and 300 m of arc from the anchor (so the
anchor the ladder uses, the span's middle, stands up to 1.8 rad from the
stored point); the arc dipping 0.8 r, 0.3 r and 1e-4 r into the sphere
(clean crossings, 270), and 0.3 ε, 4 ε and −0.3 ε (in the band, in its
gap, a miss in the band, 270). 540 runs per variant per ε. The first
candidate anchor answered in 522 of 540; the second in 18.

**The truth.** The five coefficients formed in 60-digit `mpmath` from
the stored curve and the sphere by the identities above, at the anchor
the kernel logged; the depressed `p, q, s` from those in `mpmath`.

## The rounding

Error of the coefficients handed to the ladder, over the 540 runs (the
same at both ε to the digits shown, save `a₀`'s relative figure):

| | centre harmonics | (a) constant re-based | (b) about the anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a₀ = F(anchor)`, absolute, median | 9e-5 m | 3e-6 m | 4e-16 m |
| `a₀`, absolute, worst | 2.2e2 m | 6.4e1 m | 5.5e-7 m |
| `a₂`, relative, worst | 1.3e-7 | 2.7e-8 | 2.7e-8 |
| `a₃`, relative, worst | 1.5e-9 | 1.5e-9 | 5.0e-9 |
| `a₄ = F(pole)`, relative, worst | 2e-15 | 2e-15 | 1e-15 |
| depressed `s`, relative, median | 3e-7 | 6e-8 | 2e-12 |
| depressed `s`, relative, worst | 75 | 7.6 | 1.9e-8 |
| depressed `p`, `q`, relative, worst | 6e-8, 6e-7 | 3e-8, 7e-7 | 4e-8, 2e-8 |

The depression itself is clean: done in `f64` from the true
coefficients rounded once, `s` is good to 1.5e-15, and its terms are at
most 7 times `s`. So the ladder's later stages lose nothing here; what
it is handed decides.

**The cost of an anchor away from the stored point**, (b): `F(anchor)`'s
error by where the anchor stands.

| anchor from the stored point | worst `F(anchor)` error |
|---|---|
| 0.4 m of arc (to 2e-3 rad) | 3.9e-15 m |
| 3 m (to 1.6e-2 rad) | 5.7e-15 m |
| 300 m, fat (to 0.1 rad) | 2e-15 … 4.5e-14 m |
| 300 m, thin, `a` ≥ 1e5 (to 0.5 rad) | 7e-16 … 4.5e-13 m |
| 300 m, thin 1e4 (1.6 rad) | 1.8e-10 m at `r` = 30, 5.1e-9 at `r` = 1, 5.5e-7 at `r` = 0.01 |

It grows as the offsets do: at 1.6 rad on a 1e4 ellipse the offsets are
of the semi-axis's size and the point itself is held to `u·a`. That is
the angle parameter's own limit (M1's "far" rows), not the re-basing's.
The pole's jet is always that far out, and it costs nothing: `F(pole)`
is of order `a²/r` and is read to 1e-15 relative.

## Are the escalations sound?

The ladder's answers are dropped; only an escalation is kept. Two ways
to be unsound: escalating at a clean crossing, and (harmless here) not
escalating at a graze, since the subdivision then refuses.

Ladder escalations (`conic_quadric_disc`, `_split`, `_odd`) at the 270
clean crossings, every one spurious by the oracle (two crossings, the
arc dipping at least 1e-4 r):

| ε | centre harmonics | (a) | (b) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-9 | 14 | 14 | 14 |
| 1e-12 | 37 | 28 | 17 |

At ε 1e-9 the 14 are the same poses in all three (fat ellipses at 1e4,
1e5, 1e7, 1e8; `r` = 1 and 30), and under (b) their `s` is right to
between 2e-15 and 1.5e-11: the discriminant the ladder reads really is
inside its band in `τ`. Rounding adds escalations only at ε 1e-12 (20
with centre harmonics, 11 with (a), none attributable under (b), whose
17 have `s` right to 8.5e-9 or better).

At the 270 poses in or beside the band every variant refuses, always:
the subdivision escalates `conic_quadric_sub_side` (or refuses on the
slack meter at ε 1e-12). With centre harmonics or (a) the ladder gets
there first on 29 and 21 of them at ε 1e-12 (`_disc`, `_split`); under
(b) on 3. No variant certified anything at a graze.
