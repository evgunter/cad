**Terms.** *Role*: whether a shell's stored normals point away from the region it encloses (outer) or into it (cavity). *Aimed ray*: a ray from outside the shell through a point certified inside one of its faces, read to its nearest crossing; the sign of ray direction against the crossed face's normal is the role. *Order margin*: the distance along the ray between its two nearest crossings, which must be definite for the nearest to be certain. *In band*: a margin inside the sliver band at this ε.
