## For Ev

**Recommendation (likely): a shell's role is the side at infinity of its faces, and it is read by the one reader the kernel already has for that question.** That reader is the missed-ray read in `solid_contain.rs` (PR 4725): the shell's certified signed volume where its sign is definite, otherwise a ray cast from a point outside the shell, aimed at a point certified inside one of its faces, reading the outward normal of the nearest crossing. Infinity outside the faces' material means `Outer`; infinity inside means `Void`. `props::shell_role_read` becomes that reader applied to one shell's faces. Every reader you listed keeps its question and changes only what answers it. V/A stops reading roles except where no ray can be cast.

**Terms.** *Side at infinity*: whether a point far from a closed set of oriented faces is inside or outside the material those faces bound. *Aimed ray*: a ray from such a far point through a point strictly inside one face, read to its nearest crossing. *Reach box*: a certified box around one face; a point outside every reach box of a shell is outside the shell. *In band*: a reading whose margin lies inside the sliver band at this ε, so a tighter ε might decide it.

**Premise check.** The brief asks what a role should be "read from", as if a new instrument were needed. The code already states what a role *is*: `SolidFaces::with_role` maps `Outer` to infinity-outside and `Void` to infinity-inside, and `at_infinity_side` calls the role walk and maps back. So the kernel holds one fact under two readers: `shell_role_read` (volume only) and `missed_side` (volume, then a ray). The defect is that the first stopped at the volume. The answer deletes a reader rather than adding one. The same split exists a third time, between check 7 (D1's orientation invariant, which exempts the band) and the operand gate `gate_sliver_orientation` (which reads the same orientation by ray at the next operation). That is the coupled question below.

**Why the ray is the right ruler (sure).** V/A is the shell's mean half-thickness: a slab t thick reads t/2. A ray through the slab meets its two faces t apart, and the nearer face's orientation decides the side. That is the pair reading, at the pair's own margin t, the convention you ratified for every other pair in PR 4335. The role reader reading t/2 while the pair test reads t is the one inconsistency left after PR 4783.

**Final state, reader by reader.**
- `shell_role_read`: volume first (unchanged; it decides every thick shell at no new cost), then a far point outside the hull of the shell's faces' reach boxes (the operand gate's rule today, made one shared function) and the aimed rays. `Out` is `Outer`, `In` is `Void`. Where neither decides, the refusal carries the reading that bound: an in-band aimed reading first, else the volume's, the ranking `missed_side` already has.
- Check 10 (`validate.rs`): unchanged logic. It still needs every role of a several-shell solid to compute the winding, and refuses only a role no reading decides. `ShellRoleUndecided` carries the binding reading instead of the separate `sliver` certificate, which existed only to say "the walk's words are not the certificate's". The role reader cannot tell a cavity wall from an inside-out outer shell; neither can V/A today. The winding tells them apart, as now.
- Pieces sort (`pieces.rs`): unchanged. The slab now reads `Outer`, so `slab ∪ brick (5,6)³` sorts into two solids and builds.
- `shell.rs` voids, the census's `gate_role`, `classify_shells`: the same call, now answering. `classify_shells` still reports each shell's volume and area as measured properties; its refusal collapses `Escalated`, `ZeroVolume` and `Straddles` into one variant naming the shell and the reading, and editor-core's three-arm match becomes one.
- The door: `BooleanDecision::ShellRole` keeps its subject (the shell, its solid, how many others are undecided) and the lever text. Its doc says "its side at infinity" instead of "its volume over its area". The predicate named is the ray's (`bool_point_in_solid_*`, a length margin, the gap along the ray) or, only where no ray could be cast, `chk_shell_volume_sign` with V/A.

**Worked example (likely).** `brick (−1,2)³ ∖ slab(15ε)` at ε 1e-9. The cavity shell's V/A is −7.5e-9, in band. A ray from a point outside the cavity's reach boxes, aimed inside the cavity's top face, crosses that face first; its outward normal points into the cavity, away from the ray's origin, so the origin reads `In`: the shell is a `Void`. Its only encloser is the brick's `Outer` shell, winding 1, as a void requires. The result builds. The same read makes a lone 15ε slab classify `Outer` for editor-core's component count.

**Does the "in-band shell" half of D10 reduce to pairs?** In substance, yes. An aimed ray that reads in band has its two crossings within the band's width, and the two cells it crossed are at most that far apart, so the pair test refuses the same body. The clause therefore adds no thinness judgement. It stays in D10 only as check 10's completeness condition: a several-shell solid whose winding cannot be read is not certified. I would change one phrase of the sentence PR 4690 added: "whose role is certified in band" becomes "whose role no reading decides", and add "which, where a ray can be cast, happens only beside an in-band pair". That text is agent-written and you approved it on PR 4690 in passing ("sounds good!"); the decision it carries, pairs judge thinness, does not change.

**Residue (sure).** `aim_points` offers planar faces only, so a thin shell with no planar face (a thin lens of two spheres) still refuses on its V/A, named as the volume's reading. That is CLEAVE's open row on aiming at curved faces; it closes the residue without touching this design. Until then that is the one place V/A still reads a role, and the refusal says so.

**The coupled question: check 7.** D1's invariant reads a solid's orientation off V/A_total and exempts the band, so an inside-out slab 10–19ε thick finishes `Validated` and is caught only when the next operation's operand gate reads it by ray and blames the operand (RESTFRONT's P1 row; FLUSH's inside-out-sliver-result row). Two options:

- **A (lean, likely): check 7 uses the same reader.** The invariant becomes "a solid's faces leave infinity outside", read by volume where definite and by ray otherwise. The inside-out slab refuses at rest as `NegativeVolume`; `AtRestBody::slivers`, `PlusVVerdict::Sliver` and `gate_sliver_orientation` go, since a finished body's orientation is read at its own door. D1's text changes: "zero and escalated exempt" becomes "a solid whose side no reading decides refuses". That clause's stated reason, that ε-tightening never flips valid to invalid, is already false under V/A (a −7.5e-9 slab passes at 1e-9 and refuses at 1e-10), so the ray read makes the invariant honest rather than stricter. Three readers of one fact become one.
- **B: leave check 7 as it is** and take only the role reader. Consistent in itself, keeps the operand gate, leaves RESTFRONT's row open. Easy to revisit, because A is the same function called from one more place.

Both are reversible; A removes more code and I lean to it.

**Confidence.** Recommendation: likely. Role is the side at infinity: sure (the code's own mapping). An in-band aimed ray implies an in-band pair: sure for planar faces, likely for curved. The worked example: likely, from PR 4725's pins (a thin hollow reads `Out` far away and `In` in its cavity, through the same selection), not measured by me.
