## The clearance

`trimmed::lay` decides, once and before the retry loop, which lattice nodes are laid: a node is laid only where the box `BOUNDARY_CLEARANCE` of its own step each way, per chart axis, meets no side of any loop and no crease line but one it is laid on as that crease's own point. Nothing is merged or moved. The lane takes its clearance as a value from `tessellate` (`TrimmedFace::clearance`); no test switch sits in the production path.

- **"Its own step" is stated once**, on `trimmed::Node`: on an analytic chart, the realized column step and the row's own gap; on a spline chart, its band's steps. A location two bands lay is two nodes and is laid only where both clear, so the larger box counts; a crease's own point on a grid line is laid by each band on that line under the same rule. Row: `a_location_two_bands_lay_is_laid_only_where_both_clear`.
- **A crease's own point is known by provenance** (`Node::on_crease`, set where `Creases::cut_candidates` lays it), not by looking its coordinate up among the creases. Row: `a_node_is_laid_just_outside_a_creases_clearance_and_not_just_inside` (nodes at 0.99 and 1.01 clearances either side).
- **Measured against** every side of the trim polygon, rings included. A period seam the loop runs along and a singular row are sides like any other. A side with an end at a cone's apex is searched at every azimuth, since every sector that reaches the origin holds the apex (`Side::window`; row `a_side_from_the_apex_is_searched_at_every_azimuth`, which reds under the earlier window).
- **In the plane the CDT runs in** (`Side`). In the chart that is the chart segment against the node's rectangle. On the cone it is the developed segment against the annular sector that is the rectangle's image.
- **Two cone choices no measured body distinguishes**, each said at its site: a graded row's step is the smaller of its two gaps (`lattice_candidates`; the permissive choice, and the larger moves no mesh or digest); the box is read in the development and not the chart (`Side`; they differ by the side's bow on either side of it, so neither is the permissive one, and the chart reading moves no mesh or digest). `in_the_development_a_nodes_box_is_its_sector` holds the second at unit level, to the side sampled 200,000 times and a rectangle grown by a thousandth, with the reason for the thousandth at the row.
- **Ear and refinement points are not held to it.** Each is inside a kept triangle in exact arithmetic; how far f64 holds that is now measured (below).
- **A face may lay no node** and keeps its sizing by its certificates: a rod's wall between two parallel tilted cuts 0.2 down to 0.002 apart, δ 10⁻² to 10⁻⁴, meshes at every width (measured at 1/4, worst 1.96 cell bounds). `winding_rows::a_band_narrower_than_its_clearance_meshes_within_its_certificate` asserts that such a face meshes and its mesh is the body's, and its doc says no more than that.
- **Nothing but the constant and the goldens depends on the value.**

## The winding

One definition, `mesh::validate::resolved_normal`: a triangle is resolved when its cross product exceeds `cert::rounding_allowance` at `cert::coordinate_scale` (the largest of its corners' norms) times its longest side. It answers the normal, or an `Unresolved` naming the corners, the height and the allowance. Three readers:

1. **The join**, `tessellate`'s placement fold: every triangle of every face, whichever lane made it and whether the patch is the lane's or the memo's. A face with an unresolved triangle refuses `TessellateError::UnresolvedWinding { face, unresolved }`.
