    ## The change
    - Routing: lane_of sends a curved face holding a ring to the trimmed lane, ahead of the carrier predicates; every such face refused on main, so nothing that meshes changes lane. The curved lane's ring guard goes.
    - The domain is one value, read once: tessellate_trimmed reads Body::face_lift at the run's band first and takes the outer lift and the placed rings from it (domain_loops); it places nothing itself. A door miss is DomainUnread{face, cause}, the door's cause whole: the lane's one refusal of a loop that does not close, a ring with no one place, a loop with no lift, a ringed spline face whose trim is unread. RingOnCurvedFace goes.
    - TrimPolygon holds every loop, outer first, with each point's successor on its own loop; constraints, pinch sides, the ear test, pole sides and singular_row_shape step by it. On a ringless face it is (i+1)%n, the step each took before.
    - Inside/outside: planar::classify_faces, already called; a ring's sides toggle it like any side.
    - trim_orientation checks every ring against the outer loop on every arm (wound with it: TrimLoopOrientation; no area: MissingEntity).
    - A ring's edges are sized by chords::tighten on both sides like any edge; ring vertices on a singular point decided by singular_vertices over every loop. The cone/sphere windows come from the outer loop, which bounds its region's v and |v| (points above and below any interior point).
    - No singular point lies inside a polygon behind the door (pole rows end the v domain; BothNappes keeps the apex on one side); a face round one refuses DomainUnread{OuterOpen} — the seamless frustum (apex in its ring's hole) and pipe, pinned in lift_probes. PoleInside, the polygon-side test of the same state, retires.
    - Cone: rings ride the development with the outer loop; a ring round the axis winds, and the door answers OuterOpen first.
    - Memo key: the trimmed lane folds each image as the door places it; a moved ring's image is re-read from its stored row, not a bit-function of its own lift. Unmoved faces fold loop_lift's images as before.
