Background, with the terms defined. The mesher turns each curved face into triangles in one of two **lanes**. The **rectangle lane** (`mesh::curved`) takes a face whose domain is a rectangle of its chart; on a cylinder or cone (a **ruled** face: its chart lines `u = const` are straight lines of the surface, its **rulings**) it lays one strip of triangles per column and no rows. The **chart lane** (`mesh::trimmed`) takes every other curved face: boundary chord points shared with neighbours, a lattice of interior points, a constrained Delaunay triangulation (on a cone, in the cone's **development**, the cone unrolled flat about its apex), and a per-triangle **certificate** (a closed-form bound on each triangle's distance from its surface, which accepts or refuses and knows nothing of how the triangle was made). Where the chart lane has no proved bound it sizes at a **headroom** (half of δ). A **split side** is a straight side of a ruled face carried by two or more edges, so it has a vertex part-way along; booleans make these. Such a face is routed to the chart lane (`walk::splits_a_straight_side`), where it meshed with lattice rows it does not need, at the headroom: 14 to 36 times the triangles of the same face without the split in the suites, hundreds to thousands of times on a slender cone.

**What this PR does.** In a first stage the implementer measured three cures over about 1,660 cells per ε and stopped: (1) no rows, the triangulation left alone, the lane refining where the certificate refuses; (2) no rows plus one held edge per split side; (3) **the walk**: no triangulation step, the face's triangles written by walking its two rows in order of angle about the axis. The project's owner prefers the triangulation left generic, which is why (1) was measured first; by the implementer's table it converges to roughly today's density on cones, adds `Fold` and `Triangulation` refusals and does not converge on a needle wall. The orchestrator then ruled: build (3). The walk is chosen inside the chart lane from the face's own loop (rim, side, rim, side; or rim, side, apex, side), the face sizes at δ with no headroom, and every walked triangle still goes through the certificate and the winding rule. Nothing goes on the design page.
