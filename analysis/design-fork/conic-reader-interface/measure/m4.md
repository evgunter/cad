# M4 — does a centre-held conic keep its bits

Branch `oneround/conic-harmonic-probe`, ε 1e-9 and 1e-12, K = 10.

## What decides

- **No. Bits move in all three converted readers, and verdicts in two.**
  Of 72 428 paired door calls at ε 1e-9, 641 answer with different bits
  and 61 with a different verdict; 5 of 6514 tests fail, the same five
  at both ε.
- **The sphere and wall reader moves toward answering**: 47 of its 57
  moved verdicts are a `Tangency` refusal becoming a certified answer,
  on poses the suite's own exact oracle then accepts.
- **The first-degree reader loses on the `Interval` lane**: a quadratic
  in `tan(θ/2)` encloses a touch's two roots as one wide interval, where
  crest ± half-chord does not, and two rows that built now escalate.
- I did not judge the moved bits against a truth of my own: the
  direction is known only where a suite's oracle speaks.

## Built and run

`CONIC_PROBE_MODE=6` (`geom::probe`), process-wide: every conic,
centre-held ones too, is read through the scratch polynomial with the
offsets `cos θ − k_c`, `sin θ − k_s`, `(k_c, k_s) = (0, 0)` at a centre.

| reader | under mode 6 | not converted |
|---|---|---|
| a conic's height against a plane (`curve_face::pose::plane_conic`; `curve_pair`'s `harmonic_roots`) | the constant read at the stored point (the same bits, at a centre); the two roots from `(h − p)·t² + 2q·t + (h + p)`, `t = tan(θ/2)`, solved without cancellation | the belly and extreme decisions, which still read `c₀ ± A` |
| a conic against a sphere or a wall, second harmonic definite (`conic_quadric_method`'s ladder arm) | the subdivision reads M2's offsets jet (`Terms::Local`) in place of the Fourier coefficients; the ladder and its noise meter as they stand | the cone arm, the torus doors |
| the same, second harmonic in band (the first-harmonic lane) | the constant read `((|e| − r)(|e| + r) + ρ²)/2r` at the stored point | the solve, the slack |

`geom::probe::record` appends every call of `plane_crossing_lane`,
`curve_pair::meet` (a conic on either side) and `conic_quadric_roots`
to a log: the test's name, the door, its inputs and its answer in
`Debug` form, which prints every `f64` exactly. Four runs
(`m4/run.sh`): `cargo nextest run -p geom-brep -p topo --profile ci
--no-fail-fast` at each ε, mode 0 and mode 6, `CAD_FUZZ_SEED` pinned to
one value so the randomized rows draw the same poses. 6514 tests run in
each (903 skipped by the profile). `diff_m4.py` pairs the calls within a
test by door, input and order.

**What a count is judged against.** A "moved" call is one whose answer
differs from mode 0's on identical inputs: the comparison is the branch
against the polynomial, not either against the truth. Where I say
"toward", the authority is the suite's own oracle (the row passes under
mode 6), named each time.

## Counts

The same at both ε except where two figures are given (1e-9 / 1e-12).

| door | calls paired | circle / ellipse carriers | bits moved | verdict moved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `plane_crossing_lane` | 60 762 / 57 863 | all but 5 are circles | 14 calls, 7 tests | 0 |
| `curve_pair::meet` | 7 414 | 5 693 / 1 831 | 592 / 593 calls, 21 tests; 9 / 10 more only reorder the same numbers | 4 calls, 2 tests |
| `conic_quadric_roots` | 4 252 / 4 249 | | 35 calls, 6 tests | 57 calls, 3 tests |

114 calls have no partner (110 `meet`, 4 plane door): their tests took
another path after an earlier answer moved.

**The plane door.** Roots move by at most 9.3e-14 rad
(`two_roots_apart_by_their_chord_are_two`, twice), 1.4e-15 in
`a_crossing_near_a_fast_end_is_placed_by_its_chord`, and one or two
last bits (1e-16 … 3e-16) in the other eleven. No verdict moves and
every row of the door passes. Direction not judged.

**`curve_pair::meet`.** At `f64`: 583 calls in 20 tests move some number
by less than 1e-9 (worst 6.7e-10); 6 calls in one test,
`a_segment_across_a_thin_ellipses_vertex_meets_it_at_a_root`, move one
by 2.5e-2, and the row still passes. The record holds points,
parameters and margins together, and I did not separate which moved or
judge any against a truth. At `Interval`: 3 calls move by 8e-15, and 4
calls in 2 tests go from an answer to
`Escalated(curve_pair_pose_root_gap)`, the two roots' enclosures
overlapping (gap enclosure `[0, 0.23]`). Away from the truth: both rows
state a meeting the branch builds.

**`conic_quadric_roots`.** Verdicts, by row:

So 49 toward an answer, 4 away, 4 neither. The two `Interval` losses
are my jet's own: it adds `rounding_charge` to an enclosure that already
carries its width. Bits: 35 calls move a root by at most 8.1e-11 (median
3.6e-15); these are roots the subdivision bisects on the residual, so
they move only where the piece walked changed.
