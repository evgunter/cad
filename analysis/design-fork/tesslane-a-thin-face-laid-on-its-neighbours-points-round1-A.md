Reconciliation round 1, designer A.

**(1) What moved me**

- **The construction, if there is one, should be the pair trigger.** Another designer's rule (two faces that each have a triangle built only from points they share both split those triangles' inside sides, at the join) is better than the alternative I offered Ev. Mine split every inside side whose ends one other face holds; that reads topology rather than patches and over-fires where the neighbour lays a row. I withdraw it in favour of theirs.

**(2) Point by point**

- **"The sign read alone turns the −60 cap into a refusal"** (both reports). This answers a state I also reject. My state returns the flat mesh as `Ok` and refuses only at the door that needs a solid.
- **The residue of that point stands against me.** Export of that cap refuses where the base wrote 0.73 of the volume, with vertices on the true sphere. My reply, that at δ·area ≥ volume no reader can rely on the amount, is conceded by both ("0.015 is not a defect"), but it does not make a refusal as useful as that file. Partly answered.
- **"The sign is `[mesh.winding]` for the whole body."** Not the same kind of fact. `[mesh.winding]` reads no δ and refuses what f64 cannot resolve. A sign refusal is a function of δ on a valid body, so it is a new criterion beside distance and needs its own argument.
- **Not engaged by either report: the viewer tessellates every body at δ = 1e9** (`scene::SCALE_PROBE_DELTA`). A refusal there is passed straight on by `combine::measured_at_scale` as `StepClearFault::Unmeasured`. Neither construction reaches the coarsest mesh: a face on three chord points has no inside side to split, which one report's own third measurement concedes.
- **Not engaged: positive sign is necessary, not sufficient.** One report files crossing sheets with positive net volume as a row. So the promise would be of a proxy, not of "a solid's boundary".
- **The own-point rule.** It is patch-only and memoisable, which is a real merit. But it triples every rim-to-rim strip (2n triangles to 6n) to cure a corner case, against `[mesh.sizing]`. Its author makes it conditional on a density measurement; the other report and I reject it for that reason.
- **Per-shell sign.** We agree it needs the body's shells and that a whole-mesh read misses an inverted thin void. That points to a check that takes the body, beside `check_volume`.
- **Foot on a spline as a seeded projection.** That is an iteration that can end inconclusive. The other report and I keep the chart midpoint there, by surface kind.
- **Agreed by all three:**
  - the census looks for coincident sides while the defect is coincident regions;
  - the base's insert did a second job (separation);
  - the split point is the foot;
  - the amount of volume is not owed;
  - `signed_volume`'s doc is unratified, and `check_volume` is vacuous in this regime.
