Taken from B: the instrument carries all three events and the retries; it runs per PR in the rows that pin those columns and nightly over the `Sym<Interval>` drives. The always-on assertion: sound at every rung, argued per rung (a registration is the one rung where the enclosure can exclude 0, and only when the axiom is false over the box, where a panic is right); nothing at `Sym<f64>`. One correction to B's premise: only plain-walk freezes count in `frozen`, so "frozen returns to 0" needs a measurement. One risk it could not close: `Hull` nodes.
- **B: moved to A on the difference.** Withdrew the drive dial and its default: the instrument's outputs tell a user nothing about their document, the pinned rows are about the check, so a dial is product surface for a test. Still differs in three places, none on the final state's shape: (1) the always-on assertion must exclude one door, `Decide::free_identity`, whose number is a bound on a distance and whose expression is the identity in a free variable, so a correct `Zero` can sit beside an enclosure that excludes 0; and its reach is only enclosures that exclude 0 inside or across the band, so "every shipped zero is cross-checked" overstates it; (2) nothing free at `Sym<f64>` (same as A); (3) a nightly-only lane is a day late for a change to the tier's rules, so the instrument's corpus lane also runs on a PR that touches the tier, and small planted-contradiction rows run on every PR. **After round 1:** agreed on the final state (a definite sign asks nothing; the enclosure assertion; the columns leave; the cross-check is a test instrument no shipped build contains).
