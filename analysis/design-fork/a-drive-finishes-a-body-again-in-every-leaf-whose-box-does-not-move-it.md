### Round 1 replies (both 05:38 UTC): each crossed to the other's side on the store

- **A moved to the parent's replay as the prior** (lean, "unless M8 says otherwise"; a threshold proposed: take the store if cousin-only repeats exceed about a fifth after the support-aware split). What moved it: no shared mutable state; a defect in "a node's result is a function of its key" would show deterministically under parent-only and by schedule under a store; adding the store later is cheap. Also moved, to B: the key defect is latent while lookup is by node id first; receipts are unchanged by the memo (withdrew "the drive's totals become the decisions it took"; totals are the sum of leaf receipts as today, plus a separate count of what was served and computed; `k_stats` samples travel with a node's value and are spliced at each serve); per-replay caps are a defect, with a rule: session state is either a cache whose misses never change an answer or it belongs to a node and travels with its value.
- **B moved to one store per drive.** What moved it: a structural case, not a rare one: two independent features that both need refining cost the PRODUCT of their boxes under parent-only and the SUM under a store, and independent toleranced features are the ordinary study; so "add the map later" is the design, not an addition. With a store looked up by content key the expression in the key is REQUIRED, not latent. Determinism by construction: no per-replay session at all (a node evaluation gets the drive's append-only store and a scratch scope of its own), each key computed once (a once-cell; later arrivals wait). Drive totals: each distinct node result once, defined by the tree, so invariant under the store and the schedule (re-baselined once); `k_stats` then takes each distinct decision once, which differs with the store off unless deduplicated. The new refusal is for an EMPTY read set only; "every axis at its depth budget" is the existing `Budget(Depth)`.
- **Both:** ONEHOME's finish-carry row is another axis (facts between nodes inside one build, by lineage) and no conflict; B cautions that row's "bit-identical" criterion.

**After round 1:** a crossing (each took the other's first position on where the memo lives). Still open: the store against the parent prior; what a drive's total counts and what `k_stats` takes; which refusals are new. Round 2 shows each the other's round-1 reply.
